## Read or listen to the complete information text

### Choosing and caring for a school bag

A school bag is designed to carry learning materials between home and school.

Its main compartment can hold books and exercise books, while smaller pockets can separate pencils and other small items.

A strong handle and comfortable, adjustable shoulder straps make a bag easier to carry.

Keeping items organised helps learners find what they need. Carrying only necessary items can help prevent the bag from becoming too heavy.

A bag should be checked for damaged straps, broken fasteners or sharp objects before it is used.

**Useful words:** designed, compartment, adjustable, organised, necessary, fasteners.

## Worked example

A school bag carries learning materials between home and school. Its main compartment holds books, while smaller pockets separate small items. A strong handle and adjustable straps help with carrying. Learners can organise the bag, pack only what they need and check it for damage.

A title tells the reader the topic. Facts give information. Labels can group facts that belong together.
