# PacEdu Class 1 English — Teaching-Aid Resource Set for Days 1–115

**Status:** Original PacEdu teacher resource design, pending teacher review and curriculum mapping. This is not an official Ministry of Education resource or an approved Fiji syllabus mapping.

**Source pack:** `docs/curriculum/english-class-1-daily-activities-days-1-115.md` on branch `pacedu/english-ece-form7-staging-20261119`. The 115-day plan uses 23 programme weeks, five learning days per week. Its own notice says the current official Fiji Class 1 English outcome/indicator, source edition and page must be checked against an authorized school/FEMIS/Ministry copy. This teaching-aid set preserves that limitation.

**Use:** English Subject Box → Class 1 → matching Term/Week/Day. These are teacher-preparation resources, not learner worksheet replacements. Each week has one matched aid kit and a five-day use sequence. Use only content and letter–sound correspondences already taught in the school's approved sequence.

## Non-negotiable checks

- The same weekly learning intention applies to Copper, Bronze, Silver and Gold. These are flexible support/extension pathways, not permanent labels.
- **Copper:** adult modelling, tactile/large-print or picture support, small choice set, repeated directions and extra wait time.
- **Bronze:** one worked example, visual cue and word bank, then a similar guided attempt.
- **Silver:** same objective with minimal prompting and only taught vocabulary/code.
- **Gold:** extend the same objective with a reason, comparison, extra example or independent edit; do not introduce untaught code.
- Describe every visual aloud; do not rely on colour alone. Offer tactile, large-print, signed, AAC, pointing, oral, dictated and written response modes as appropriate.
- Check object safety, hygiene and movement before use. No sharp, hot, toxic, unknown or otherwise hazardous materials.
- Do not use personal photographs or private family information unless the learner freely chooses and school policy permits.
- Every item remains `PENDING_TEACHER_REVIEW` until the teacher checks suitability, accessibility, local context and the exact lesson objective. Curriculum status remains `PENDING_VERIFICATION` until the official source and exact indicator are checked.

## 23 weekly teaching-aid kits

### Term 1 · Week 1 — Programme Week 1: Myself, name and identity

**Days:** 1–5  
**Kit contents:** Name cards with learner-chosen name; mirror or self-portrait frame; greeting sentence strip: “Hello. My name is ____.”; tactile name tiles; teacher-held photo/drawing prompts.  
**How to teach with the kit:** Learners match their spoken/signed/selected name to their card, then build or dictate the sentence. Never require a personal photograph; a drawing or symbol is an equal option.

**Five-day deployment:**
- **Day 1 — Listen and notice:** Introduce the aid; name or describe each item and invite learners to notice or select one.
- **Day 2 — Model and explore:** Teacher models one complete example with think-aloud, spoken description and a tactile/large-print or symbol alternative.
- **Day 3 — Practise together:** Complete guided practice with a partner or small group; rotate turns and reduce prompts when ready.
- **Day 4 — Apply independently:** Learner uses the same aid to show the shared objective independently or with their documented access support.
- **Day 5 — Review and demonstrate:** Review with a fresh but taught example; record evidence, response mode, prompt level and next teaching step.

**Ability pathway adaptations:** Copper uses a reduced choice set and adult modelling; Bronze uses the visual cue and one worked example; Silver completes the same task with fewer prompts; Gold explains a choice, compares examples or creates an additional example without moving beyond taught content.

**Teacher evidence to record:** learner response; accuracy against the weekly intention; prompt level; access format used; any misconception; next step. Do not record disability-related speech differences or accent as literacy errors.
