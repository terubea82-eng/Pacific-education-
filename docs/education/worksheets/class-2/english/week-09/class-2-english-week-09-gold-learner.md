## Read this story
### Sera's Class Garden Experiment
Sera's class planned a small growing experiment. Each learner planted a bean seed and recorded what happened. Sera filled a pot with soil, made a shallow hole and placed her seed inside. She covered it lightly, watered it and positioned the pot near a bright window. During the first few days, the soil looked unchanged, so Sera resisted the urge to dig up the seed. She checked that the soil stayed slightly moist. Several days later, a shoot emerged. Over the next few mornings, the stem grew taller and two leaves unfolded. Finally, Sera compared her notes with the plant and explained the stages to her group.

**Word bank:** experiment • positioned • unchanged • resisted • emerged • unfolded • compared • stages

## Worked example
If a story says, “First, Ana puts on her shoes. Next, she walks outside,” the correct order is: 1. Ana puts on her shoes. 2. Ana walks outside. The word **first** tells us which event happens earlier.
