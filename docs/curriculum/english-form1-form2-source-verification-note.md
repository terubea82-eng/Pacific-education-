# PacEdu English — Form 1 and Form 2 recovery note

**Purpose:** Resolve the missing secondary levels between Class 8 and Form 3 before adding learner worksheets to the English Subject Box.

## Level identity

- **Form 1 corresponds to Year 9.**
- **Form 2 corresponds to Year 10.**
- These are secondary levels and must not be substituted with the existing primary Class 1 and Class 2 packs. The Class 1 and Class 2 files remain Year 1 and Year 2 primary resources.

## Source discovery

A copy of the Fiji Ministry of Education, Curriculum Development Unit, *English Syllabi Years 9 & 10* (Fiji, 2014) was located at:

- https://theseeproject.org/wp-content/uploads/2015/05/l2_year910_english_syllabi.pdf

The located document identifies three integrated strands: Listening and Speaking; Reading and Viewing; Writing and Shaping. Its Year 9/Year 10 progression includes distinct oral text types, reading/viewing texts, writing forms, language features, socio-cultural contexts and language-learning strategies. The document's printed pages 16–28 contain Year 9 course detail; Year 10 detail begins on printed page 29.

The source is hosted outside the Ministry's current official curriculum repository. It is therefore a **source lead**, not sufficient proof that the 2014 syllabus remains the current authorised edition for the relevant cohort. The school's current Ministry-authorised Year 9 and Year 10 syllabus/teacher guides must be checked before any official indicator wording or codes are marked verified.

## Curriculum boundary

- Do not use the 2024 Revised Secondary English Curriculum (RSEC) teacher guide as the basis for this recovery work unless the owner explicitly changes the existing instruction to exclude unfinished/revised curriculum.
- Do not copy Year 9 outcomes into Year 10. Keep the two levels distinct.
- Do not invent official indicator IDs, prescribed texts, examination requirements or week/day allocations.
- Until the current authorised documents are obtained and checked, label the Form 1/Form 2 planning indicators as PacEdu working indicators and retain `PENDING_OFFICIAL_INDICATOR_VERIFICATION`.

## Worksheet production requirements

For each level, produce 115 learning days across the 9/9/5 planning frame. Each day must have four separate standalone learner worksheets: Copper, Bronze, Silver and Gold. Each worksheet must include learner identification fields (name, class, subject, term/week, date and pathway), all reading material/instructions needed to work independently, a worked example, Bloom's build-up questions, generous response space for every response, and a matching marking guide that credits accurate synonyms and equivalent wording. Do not combine the four pathways into one learner download.
