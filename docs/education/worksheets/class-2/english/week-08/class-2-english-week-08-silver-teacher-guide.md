# Teacher-only guide — Class 2 English Week 8 (Silver)

**Days 36–40 | Literal comprehension**

**Matching learner file:** class-2-english-week-08-silver-learner.md

## Learning intention
Locate and communicate information explicitly stated in a short text; use the text as evidence rather than guessing.

## Access and teaching support
Invite a first reading, then reread for evidence. Model how to find a key word in the question and locate the matching sentence. Allow extra time and all accessible response modes.

## Daily teaching and evidence
### Day 36 — Find a stated detail
**Teaching move:** Read the passage and model finding one clearly stated fact.

**Indicative evidence:** On a bench near the classroom.

### Day 37 — Who, what, where
**Teaching move:** Use who/what/where prompts. Ask the learner to locate the matching name, object, action, or place.

### Day 38 — Find actions and sequence
**Teaching move:** Identify action words and order two events using the text.

### Day 39 — Answer with text evidence
**Teaching move:** Model matching a question's key word to the sentence that contains the answer. Invite the learner to show the evidence.

**Indicative evidence:** Beside her friend's school bag.

**Record:** response mode __________  prompt level (independent / gesture / verbal / modelled) __________  text evidence located __________

### Day 40 — Review literal details
**Teaching move:** Reread and complete the comprehension questions. Encourage checking each answer against the passage.

**Indicative evidence:** Lani thanked the friend for keeping it safe.

**Record:** response mode __________  prompt level (independent / gesture / verbal / modelled) __________  text evidence located __________

## Indicative answer guide
1. **Where did Lani leave her hat?**
   - Answer: On a bench near the classroom.
2. **Why did she forget the hat?**
   - Answer: She hurried outside to join a game.
3. **What did Lani do when the hat was not on top of the bench?**
   - Answer: She checked underneath and looked around the nearby bags.
4. **Where was the hat found?**
   - Answer: Beside her friend's school bag.
5. **How do you know the friend helped Lani?**
   - Answer: Lani thanked the friend for keeping it safe.

Accept accurate equivalent wording and accessible communication modes. For literal comprehension, answers should be supported by information directly stated in the passage. Do not penalise spelling or handwriting where these are not the target skill.
