### Day 1 — Term 1, Week 1 — Identity, language and society — Listening and speaking
**Objective:** Analyse how language, identity and context shape meaning; develop a close reading and analytical response.
**Teacher-led activity:** Present a reasoned position and respond thoughtfully to questions. Learners work with an authorised level-appropriate text or prompt; identify purpose and context, select relevant evidence, and communicate a reasoned response.
**PacEdu working achievement indicator:** Learner can present a reasoned position and respond thoughtfully to questions.
**Worked example:** Interpret the task → identify audience/purpose → select relevant evidence → organise a line of reasoning → draft → review precision and clarity. Teacher models using the actual authorised text.
**Copper:** Teacher models each stage; divide the task into short steps; provide accessible text extracts, vocabulary support and planning prompts. Accept appropriate AAC, sign, oral, dictation or written response.
**Bronze:** Provide a worked model, structured planning frame, key vocabulary and guided feedback; reduce support as independence develops.
**Silver:** Complete the task independently; organise a coherent response, select relevant evidence and edit for clarity.
**Gold:** Extend the same objective through synthesis, qualification, comparison, counterargument or independent stylistic revision.
**Evidence:** Record an observable outcome (analysis, evidence selection, discussion, source evaluation, draft/revision or presentation).
**Answer guidance:** Credit accurate, substantiated interpretations; accept alternative conclusions when supported by relevant evidence. Credit equivalent vocabulary and sensible paraphrase.
**Official indicator:** PENDING_OFFICIAL_INDICATOR_VERIFICATION. Add exact authorised outcome, source edition and page only after checking current school guide. Review: PENDING_TEACHER_REVIEW.
