## Learning goal
I can listen for important details, answer questions and retell information in order.

## Listen to this complete message
On Friday, Class 2 will visit the school library. The class will meet their teacher beside the mango tree at nine o’clock. Each learner should bring a reading book and a water bottle. First, the teacher will explain the library rules. Next, learners will choose a book. Before returning to class, everyone will place the books on the return trolley.

You may listen to the teacher read this message more than once. You may follow the printed words, use picture cues or ask for a repeat.

## Worked example
Teacher says: “Class 2 will visit the school library.”  
Question: Where will Class 2 go?  
Worked answer: **To the school library.**  
The answer is in the first sentence.

## Support for this pathway
Listen with an adult. Use picture or object cues for Friday, library, book, bottle and mango tree. Give one short direction at a time, repeat the message and allow pointing, matching, sign, AAC or speech.
