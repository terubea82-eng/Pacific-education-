## What I am learning
I can understand and create simple ordered instructions. I can use action words and sequence words such as **first, next, then** and **last**.

You may write, draw, arrange picture/word cards, point, sign, use AAC or dictate your answers. Your teacher may record your exact words. This worksheet uses a familiar handwashing procedure. Follow your school's hygiene routine and adult guidance.

## Read or listen to the complete procedure

**How to wash your hands**

1. Check that clean running water, soap and a clean towel are ready.
2. Wet your hands under the running water.
3. Apply soap and rub palms, backs of hands, between fingers and around fingertips.
4. Rinse thoroughly so the soap is removed.
5. Turn off the tap without re-soiling clean hands where possible.
6. Dry your hands with a clean towel.

**Action words:** check, wet, apply, rub, rinse, remove, turn off, dry.

## Worked example

First, check that water, soap and a clean towel are ready. Next, wet your hands and apply soap. Then, rub your palms, the backs of your hands and between your fingers. After that, rinse thoroughly. Finally, turn off the tap and dry your hands with a clean towel.

The sequence words tell the order. Action verbs tell what to do.
