**Learning intention:** I can use clues in a text to make a sensible prediction and connect the text to something I know or have experienced.

**How to answer:** You may write, draw, point, choose, sign, use AAC or dictate your answer. Your teacher can record your response. Take your time.

## Read or listen to the whole passage

> On her way to school, Mere notices a line of dark clouds moving across the hills. The air becomes cooler, and leaves begin to move in the rising wind. She checks the umbrella in her bag and places her exercise books inside a waterproof folder. The bus is not yet at the stop. What is likely to happen, and how might Mere respond?

**Remember:** A prediction is what you think may happen next. A connection is how the text reminds you of something you know or have experienced. Use clues. A prediction is not a fact unless the text tells us it happened.

## Worked example

Likely prediction: A shower may arrive before the bus. Evidence: dark clouds, cooler air and rising wind. Possible response: Mere can wait under shelter and keep her books in the folder. The passage gives clues, but it does not state exactly what happens.
