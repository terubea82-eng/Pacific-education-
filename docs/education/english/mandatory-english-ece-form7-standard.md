# Mandatory PacEdu English Subject-Box Standard — ECE to Form 7

**Status:** Proposed mandatory system requirement; enforced in the live app only after review, merge, and deployment.

## Subject-box structure
English is one subject box. All English materials from ECE through Form 7 are opened and managed inside it. Do not create parallel English activity pages or place English materials in unrelated subject boxes. Keep the label ECE.

Within English, use this sequence:
1. Select class/form (ECE, Class 1–8, Form 3–7).
2. Select term, week, and day.
3. Open the approved daily English concept and learning objective.
4. Teacher guidance and lesson/work plan.
5. Student worksheets for Gold, Silver, Bronze, and Copper.
6. Teacher marking guide, answer guidance, and evidence review.
7. Revision activities linked to concepts already taught.
8. Class/form English assessments and examination papers.
9. Student progress and concept mastery records.

## Mandatory common framework
Every class/form MUST use the same underlying structure and four ability pathways: Gold, Silver, Bronze, and Copper. The framework, navigation, and instruction pattern stay consistent. The content must be age-, class-, and curriculum-appropriate; identical structure does NOT mean copying the same text or questions across classes.

For each class/day:
- State one approved English concept and learning objective.
- All four ability worksheets address that same concept.
- Differentiate scaffolding, vocabulary, text length, task complexity, expected independence, and challenge.
- Provide short, clear teacher and student instructions, response space, and concept-aligned marking criteria.
- Include suitable accessibility adaptations.

## English learning coverage
Plan and sequence listening, speaking, phonological/sound awareness where age-appropriate, vocabulary, reading, comprehension, grammar, spelling, handwriting/writing, composition, and oral communication according to the verified curriculum level. Do not force every strand into every daily lesson; map strands across the term.

## Registration and traceability
Every issued worksheet must resolve to the registered teacher and the correct registered student, including student ID, school name, school registration number, class/form, subject, term/week/day, date, and daily concept. Do not treat a worksheet as assigned if its student registration link fails. Restrict student information to authorised users.

## Curriculum and assessment safeguards
Use the currently adopted Fiji National Curriculum Framework and the verified syllabus/outcomes for each class/form. Record source/version and outcome reference when verified; flag uncertain mappings for teacher review rather than inventing official codes. Teacher-reviewed answer guidance and marking criteria are required before automated marking is relied upon.

## Existing materials and future generation
Audit all previously created English materials from ECE to Form 7 and rework each item to this standard: correct level, approved daily concept, four differentiated pathways, clear instructions, accessibility, marking criteria, and teacher/student registration links. Apply the standard by default to all new English materials. Track incomplete items; do not call them complete until checked.

## Release protection and acceptance tests
Implement additively; preserve existing working features. Before release, test subject-box navigation, class/form and term/week/day filtering, all four pathways, concept consistency, age-appropriate differentiation, teacher/student linkage, access control, offline operation, marking review, and regression checks.

This document records the requirement only. It does not by itself change the live app, complete the worksheet collection, or certify that existing materials have been reworked.
