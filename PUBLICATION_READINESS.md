2. Production database with access controls, backups and recovery.
3. Server/provider payment verification.
4. Security testing and independent review.
5. Privacy/data-governance review.
6. Child-safeguarding review and operational controls.
7. Real-device accessibility and offline/low-bandwidth testing.

9. Production hosting configuration, monitoring and rollback.
10. Owner approval plus required authorized external review.

## Important boundary
GitHub Pages can publish the prototype. It cannot by itself provide production-grade authentication, database security, payment verification, privacy controls, child-safeguarding operations or cybersecurity assurance.
