## Learning focus
I can notice and use describing words (adjectives) to make a description clearer and more precise.

## Read the passage
### The Market at Sunrise
At sunrise, the busy market began to fill. Fresh green pawpaw, ripe golden bananas and bright red tomatoes covered the wooden tables. A cheerful seller greeted the early customers. Cool morning air moved through the crowded stalls, carrying the sweet smell of fruit.

## Word bank
busy • fresh • green • ripe • golden • bright • cheerful • early • cool • crowded • sweet

## Worked example
A *flower* becomes more precise when I write **a yellow flower**. The word *yellow* describes the flower. An adjective tells us more about a noun. Some describing words tell colour, size, shape, number, feeling or quality.
