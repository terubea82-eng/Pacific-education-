## What I am learning
I can tell or write about an experience in the order it happened. I can plan a beginning, middle and ending.

You may write, draw, arrange picture/word cards, sign, use AAC or dictate your ideas. Your teacher may record your exact words. You do not have to share private family information. You may write about a school activity, a game, a garden task or an imagined event instead.

## Read or listen to the whole story

> During the school holiday, Mere visited her aunt in a nearby village. On the first morning, they walked to the market and bought vegetables for lunch. When they returned, Mere helped wash the vegetables while her aunt prepared the cooking area. After lunch, Mere wrote a short list of the activities they had completed. Before going home the next day, she thanked her aunt and promised to visit again.

## Worked example — put events in order

A clear recount follows the events in time order and includes relevant details: During the holiday, Mere visited her aunt. First, they went to the market. Next, Mere helped prepare lunch. After lunch, she listed their activities. Finally, she thanked her aunt before returning home the next day.
