## What I am learning

## My own prompt — read or listen
Picture prompt described in words: during a windy afternoon, a learner notices leaves blowing across the school courtyard and places a fallen book safely on a bench. Learners may use a drawing or teacher-read description.

**Think about the idea:**

When? on a windy afternoon. Who? a learner. What is noticed? leaves blowing across the courtyard. What action follows? places a fallen book safely on a bench.

## Worked example

On a windy afternoon, a learner watches leaves blow across the school courtyard and places a fallen book safely on a bench.

The sentence tells an idea. It starts with a capital letter, has spaces between words and ends with a full stop. Your teacher may point out each part while reading it aloud or showing it.
