Pacific Education Privacy Policy

Effective date: September 23, 2026

This privacy policy applies to the Pacific Education controlled prototype pilot and the Android application that loads the Pacific Education pilot website.

1. Pilot purpose

The current release is a controlled prototype for demonstration, testing, curriculum-alignment work and authorized feedback. It is not a production education service.

2. Information handled by the pilot

The pilot is designed not to require real children's sensitive information, passwords, payment credentials or exact child location. Prototype learning progress and test-session information may be stored locally on the device/browser for demonstration and testing.

Do not enter real child personal information into the pilot. If information is submitted voluntarily despite this warning, it should be treated as pilot test data and must not be assumed to have production-grade protection.

3. Technical information

The Android application loads the Pacific Education website over HTTPS. The website host, network providers and Android platform may process ordinary technical information such as IP address, device/network information, timestamps and diagnostic information according to their own policies. Pacific Education does not use this pilot to intentionally build a commercial profile of children.

4. Sharing

The pilot does not intentionally sell children's personal information. No third-party advertising SDK is intentionally included in this pilot release. Any technical processing by hosting, network, app-store or infrastructure providers is subject to the applicable provider terms and privacy practices.

5. Children

Pacific Education is intended for school learning and may be used by children. The pilot therefore applies a precautionary rule: do not enter real child personal or sensitive information. A production release will require a completed child-safeguarding and privacy review, appropriate parental/guardian and school processes where required, secure authentication, secure backend controls and verified data practices.

6. Security

The pilot uses prototype controls and is explicitly not a production security boundary. Local browser storage must not be treated as secure authentication or authorization. Production security controls remain required before production use.

7. Payments

The pilot must not receive or store payment credentials. Any future production payment processing must use a verified production payment provider/server-side verification process and will be covered by updated privacy and payment disclosures.

8. Changes

This policy will be updated before any production release if the application's data practices change.

9. Contact

Pacific Education owner/developer: Terubea Tion, Fiji.

Important: This pilot privacy policy is a publication draft and should be reviewed by an appropriate legal/privacy adviser before production launch.
