## Marking principles
- Accept accurate synonyms and equivalent wording; do not insist on model-answer phrasing.
- Award open-response credit for relevant evidence and defensible reasoning.
- Treat this as a diagnostic, not a formal Ministry assessment or sole basis for placement.

## Suggested answer points
1. Rain, the late bus and floodwater create delay and a safety conflict.
2. Jone draws attention to the dangerous moving water, helping Mere understand the driver's caution.
3. The driver is cautious and responsible because he checks for a safe crossing before proceeding.
4. It shows consideration and growth in Mere's care for another person.
5. The moment Jone points out the fast-moving water, or another defensible turning point with evidence.
6. A developed response with a clear interpretation, supporting details and logical explanation.
