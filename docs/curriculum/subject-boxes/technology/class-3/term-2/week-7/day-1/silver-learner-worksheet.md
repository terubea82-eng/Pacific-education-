### Read the full passage

**A label that fits**

The Class 3 book box needs a new label. The label must be wide enough for the words “Class 3 Books” and must fit on the front of the box. The group has clean card, a ruler, a pencil, blunt-ended scissors and a glue stick.

The group measures the space on the box before marking the card. The ruler helps make straight guide lines. The pencil marks the cutting line. The scissors cut the card to size. The glue stick fixes the label in place. The learners check that the card is clean and that the tools are safe before beginning.

They keep the desk clear, sit while cutting, keep fingers away from the blades and cut slowly away from the body. Scissors are passed closed, handle first. A damaged tool is not used; the learner stops and tells the teacher. Before attaching the label, the group checks that it fits the space and that the words can be read.

### Worked example

**Question:** The label is too wide for the box. What should the group do before gluing it?  
**Model answer:** Measure the space again and mark a narrower cutting line with the ruler and pencil.  
**Reason:** Checking the size before gluing avoids attaching a label that does not fit.
