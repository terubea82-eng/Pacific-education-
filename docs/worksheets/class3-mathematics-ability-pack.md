# Class 3 Mathematics — four ability worksheets

Status: original pilot worksheets; verify the current Fiji Class/Year 3 Mathematics syllabus and taught number range before release. Flexible subject-specific bands: Support 0–49%, Developing 50–64%, Expected 65–79%, Advanced 80–100%.

## A. Support (0–49%) — Number, addition, subtraction

**Example:** 23 = 2 tens and 3 ones.

1. Write 46 in tens and ones.
2. 18 + 5 = ___
3. 37 − 4 = ___
4. Which is smaller: 52 or 25?
5. Complete: 10, 20, 30, ___, ___.
6. There are 14 mangoes. Four are eaten. How many remain?

**Teacher key (10 marks):** 4 tens and 6 ones (2); 23 (2); 33 (2); 25 (1); 40, 50 (1); 10 (2).

## B. Developing (50–64%) — Place value and word problems

**Example:** 35 + 20 = 55 because two tens are added.

1. 46 + 27 = ___
2. 82 − 35 = ___
3. Write 304 in hundreds, tens and ones.
4. Round 68 to the nearest ten.
5. A bus has 36 passengers. At the next stop, 18 get on. How many passengers are there now?
6. A farmer packs 54 guavas into boxes of 6. How many full boxes can be filled?

**Teacher key (10 marks):** 73 (2); 47 (2); 3 hundreds, 0 tens, 4 ones (2); 70 (1); 54 (1); 9 boxes (2).

## C. Expected (65–79%) — Operations and measurement

**Example:** 4 groups of 5 = 4 × 5 = 20.

1. 7 × 6 = ___
2. 48 ÷ 6 = ___
3. A rope is 85 cm long. Sera cuts off 27 cm. How many centimetres remain?
4. A lesson begins at 9:15 and lasts 30 minutes. What time does it finish?
5. A rectangular garden is 6 m long and 4 m wide. Find its perimeter.
6. Explain how you checked one answer.

**Teacher key (12 marks):** 42 (2); 8 (2); 58 cm (2); 9:45 (2); 20 m (2); valid check/explanation (2).

## D. Advanced (80–100%) — Multi-step reasoning

**Example:** Three bags hold 8 shells each, and 5 shells are removed: 3 × 8 − 5 = 19.

1. A school buys 6 packets of pencils with 12 pencils in each. It gives 19 pencils to one class. How many remain?
2. A garden is 8 m long and 5 m wide. Find its perimeter and area.
3. A learner says 25 × 4 = 29 because they added 25 and 4. Explain the error and solve correctly.
4. A trip starts at 8:35 and takes 1 hour 45 minutes. What time does it finish?
5. Create a two-step market problem using multiplication and subtraction, then solve it.

**Teacher key (12 marks):** 53 pencils (3); perimeter 26 m and area 40 m² (3); multiplication means 4 groups of 25; answer 100 (2); 10:20 (2); valid problem and correct working (2).

## Access and release
Allow counters, number lines, large print, tactile shapes, and screen-reader-friendly text as appropriate. Read word problems if reading is not being assessed. Do not require color or sound to answer. Keep answers teacher-only. Confirm current syllabus outcome and taught range before release.
