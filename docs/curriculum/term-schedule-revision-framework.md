# PacEdu Term Schedule and Revision Framework

## Fiji pilot planning schedule

| Term | Teaching weeks | Daily slots | End-of-term resources |
|---|---:|---:|---|
| Term 1 | 9 | 45 | Revision practice and mock practice examination |
| Term 2 | 9 | 45 | Revision practice and mock practice examination |
| Term 3 | 5 | 25 | Revision practice and mock practice examination |

Each week has five stages: introduce and notice; model an example; guided practice; independent application; review and evidence. The workspace uses the selected class level, subject, term, week and one of four ability bands: support, developing, expected and extension. A term/week selector saves the selection and opens the selected lesson.

The initial subject-template inventory is English, Mathematics, Science, Social Studies, Healthy Living, Physical Education, Arts and Crafts, Music, Digital Literacy, iTaukei Language and Hindi. This inventory is not proof that all official subject outcomes are covered.

Revision practice is generated for the selected class, subject and ability group from the final two teaching weeks. The mock practice examination is explicitly labelled as practice, not an official or past examination paper. Revision and practice examination questions appear in the final teaching week; teacher answer keys are not shown to learners.

**Curriculum safety:** the weekly sequence is a planning framework, not an official Fiji Ministry timetable. Each achievement indicator remains NOT_YET_MAPPED until a teacher verifies the exact current authorised syllabus, strand, indicator ID/text, page or section, term/week placement and review evidence. Never invent official indicator codes or claim generic weekly stages are verified outcomes. Existing teacher approval and activity release controls remain in place. Missing work is not automatically evidence of low ability.
