# Pacific Education — Stage 26 Fiji Curriculum Source Validation & Alignment

## Status

**Stage 26 architecture contract: DEFINED — official-source verification, curriculum mapping, version control and authorized review required.**

**Important current-context correction:** Fiji's National Curriculum Framework is under review. Therefore Pacific Education must not hard-code the assumption that one framework/version will remain the permanent authority. The production curriculum registry must identify the applicable current official source/version and its status at the time each curriculum mapping is approved.

## 1. Curriculum authority

For Fiji implementation, Pacific Education must treat the applicable official Ministry of Education Fiji curriculum prescriptions, achievement indicators and other formally issued curriculum documents as the authoritative source.

Pacific Education must not present its own generated lesson structure as a replacement for the official curriculum.

## 2. Source verification

Before production curriculum release, each official source used by the platform must have:

- Official source/title
- Issuing authority
- Applicable year/version
- Class/form and subject scope
- Term or level applicability where specified
- Source location/reference
- Verification date
- Owner/reviewer record

Unverified or ambiguous curriculum content must not be labelled officially aligned.

## 3. Achievement-indicator mapping

Daily lessons and assessments should map to the applicable official achievement indicators.

The mapping should identify:

- Class/form
- Subject
- Term
- Official curriculum reference
- Achievement indicator
- Lesson/learning activity
- Assessment evidence where applicable
- Coverage status
- Reviewer/verification status

The system should distinguish official source text from Pacific Education instructional interpretation.

## 4. Integrated learning model

Where the approved Pacific Education model uses integrated learning:

- 50% should remain anchored to core achievement indicators.
- 50% may provide level-appropriate integration across other knowledge areas.

The integrated portion must not displace required core achievement coverage.

## 5. LANA/LANS prerequisites

LANA/LANS prerequisites must only be represented after official curriculum mapping and verification.

Where the approved sequencing requires LANA/LANS content in Term 1 before Term 2 progression, the production curriculum configuration must enforce that verified prerequisite sequence.

The platform must not invent official prerequisite relationships.

## 6. Revision and examination planning

Teacher revision and examination scheduling may redistribute remaining achievement coverage only within the verified curriculum structure.

Assessments and examinations must be limited to indicators and learning content that were actually covered according to the approved mapping.

## 7. Curriculum change control

Curriculum references must be versioned.

When an official curriculum source changes:

1. Identify affected mappings.
2. Review lessons and assessments.
3. Update the verified source record.
4. Re-run alignment checks.
5. Record reviewer approval.
6. Prevent outdated mappings from silently becoming current.

## 8. Curriculum integrity

The system must prevent unsupported claims such as:

- “Official Ministry lesson” when it is not.
- “Official assessment” when it is not.
- “Official achievement indicator” when the source has not been verified.
- “Curriculum compliant” without the required review evidence.

Generated educational content must remain distinguishable from official curriculum references.

## 9. Required Stage 26 evidence

Stage 26 should not be marked implementation-complete until evidence exists for:

- Official Fiji curriculum sources identified
- Source versions/dates recorded
- Applicable Class 1–Form 7 mappings reviewed
- Subject/term mappings verified
- Achievement-indicator mappings verified
- LANA/LANS prerequisite mapping verified where applicable
- Integrated-learning mapping reviewed
- Assessment coverage rules tested
- Revision/examination redistribution rules tested
- Curriculum change/versioning process documented
- Independent or authorized curriculum review completed
- Unsupported official-status claims removed or corrected

## 10. Curriculum change control

Because the Fiji National Curriculum Framework and related education policy are subject to review and change, production must support:

- Source/version status
- Effective and superseded dates where available
- Verification date
- Responsible reviewer
- Mapping version
- Impact assessment when an official source changes
- Revalidation of affected lessons and assessments
- Withdrawal or replacement of obsolete mappings
- Clear separation between official source text and Pacific Education instructional interpretation

A curriculum mapping must never be labelled "official" merely because it was created inside Pacific Education.

## 11. Release boundary

Stage 26 does not authorize production publication. Production curriculum claims require current-source verification and the applicable authorized review.

Production curriculum content must remain subject to official-source verification and authorized review before being represented as aligned to Fiji's official curriculum.
