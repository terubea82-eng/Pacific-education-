# PacEdu Teacher-Only Marking Guide — Class 1 English
## Week 3 · Days 11–15 · Bronze pathway

**Matched learner worksheet:** class-1-english-week-03-bronze-days-11-15.md
**Learning intention:** Use familiar object and position words to follow one-step and two-step directions.

**Fair marking:** Accept demonstration, matching, pointing, signing, AAC, drawing, dictation or writing. Do not score accent or handwriting. Record support used.

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| I | Independent |
| P | With visual/gesture prompt |
| M | After adult model |
| NYO | Not yet observed |

### Day 11 — Listen and match
- BOOK matches a book; PENCIL matches a pencil; BAG matches a bag.
- “Find the bag” → BAG / bag object.

### Day 12 — Follow a position word
- Book on table → on top of table.
- Pencil beside book → next to book.
- Book in bag → IN.

### Day 13 — Follow two steps
- First pick up pencil; next put it on book.
- First take book; next put it beside bag.
- Credit both actions in the correct order.

### Day 14 — Apply
- Pencil goes inside bag.
- Book goes beside table.
- Credit accurate action/position, demonstrated or communicated accessibly.

### Day 15 — Review
- ON = on top of; IN = inside.
- First take pencil; next put it in bag.
- “What helped?” may be answered by selecting any support actually used.
