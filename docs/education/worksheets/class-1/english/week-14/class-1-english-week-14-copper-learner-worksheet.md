## Read or listen to the whole story
Mere sees a ball. She puts it by a tree. She goes to class.

You may read, listen, use large print/tactile supports, or have the passage read aloud. You may answer by writing, drawing, pointing, signing, using AAC or dictating. You do not have to speak aloud.

## Learning goal
Put the story events in the order they happen and retell the story using first, next and last.

## Worked example
Worked example: If an event happens at the beginning, label it FIRST. If it happens after that, label it NEXT. The final event is LAST. Model: “First, Mere puts the ball by the tree.”

## Your task
Choose the three picture/event cards: MERE HAS A BALL; BALL BY TREE; MERE GOES TO CLASS. Put them in story order. Point, say, sign, use AAC or draw. Then retell with adult support.
