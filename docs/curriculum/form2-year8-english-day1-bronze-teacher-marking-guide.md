**Separate teacher-use file.** Match only with the Bronze learner worksheet.

**Status:** ORIGINAL PACEDU DIAGNOSTIC DRAFT — `PENDING_TEACHER_REVIEW`; `PENDING_OFFICIAL_INDICATOR_VERIFICATION`.

## Marking principles
- Accept accurate synonyms and equivalent wording; do not insist on model-answer phrasing.
- Award open-response credit for relevant evidence and defensible reasoning.
- Treat this as a diagnostic, not a formal Ministry assessment or sole basis for placement.

## Suggested answer points
1. A village bus shelter during rain.
2. The bus was late and floodwater covered part of the road.
3. She made room, covered the seedlings, or gave Jone the drier seat (any two relevant actions).
4. The water was moving quickly and the crossing might be unsafe.
5. She was impatient, then understood the danger after Jone pointed out the fast-moving water.
6. A coherent paragraph using story details about care/helpfulness.

## Suggested rubric (12 marks)
| Evidence | Marks |
|---|---:|
| Questions 1–2: retrieval and understanding | 0–2 |
| Questions 3–4: application and analysis | 0–4 |
| Question 5: evaluation supported by evidence | 0–2 |
| Question 6: coherent created response | 0–4 |
| **Total** | **12** |

For Question 6, assess relevance (1), story evidence (1), organisation (1) and clarity (1). Accept equivalent interpretations when supported by the story.

## Feedback
Record a strength, a next teaching step and the evidence for each. Use multiple sources of evidence; do not assign a permanent ability group from this one worksheet.
