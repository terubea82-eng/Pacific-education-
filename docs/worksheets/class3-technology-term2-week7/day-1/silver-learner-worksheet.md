### Read this passage
After a rainy break, the class notices that its shared pencils roll off a smooth desk. Ana proposes a pencil tray made from reused cardboard. She lists the requirements: it must hold several pencils, stay on the desk, have edges that stop pencils rolling away, and contain no sharp exposed parts. She compares thin paper with firm carton cardboard. She sketches a shallow tray with a flat base and folded sides. She plans to ask an adult to help cut the cardboard, fold the edges, and cover rough corners with paper tape. She will test the tray by placing six pencils inside and gently moving the desk.

### Worked example
**Requirement → design feature:** Pencils roll away → raised sides.  
**Requirement → material:** Tray must hold pencils → firm cardboard rather than thin paper.  
**Safety:** Adult help for cutting and cover rough edges.  
**Test:** Add pencils and gently check whether they stay inside.
