# PacEdu Teacher-Only Marking Guide — Class 1 English
## Week 5 · Days 21–25 · Silver pathway

**Matched learner worksheet:** class-1-english-week-05-silver-days-21-25.md
**Learning intention:** Independently discriminate S/s and A/a and apply recognition to familiar taught words.

**Fair evidence:** Accept oral, signed, AAC, dictated or written responses. Do not penalise accent or handwriting.

### Day 21
- S/s: copy/identify all instances of S and s.
- A/a: copy/identify all instances of A and a.
- Capital/small forms: S/s and A/a.

### Day 22
- Correct matches: S–s and A–a.
- Explanation should convey that case changes the form, not the letter identity.

### Day 23
- Use the class's previously taught word list only.
- **Sam** and **sat** are conditional examples; do not require them if their correspondences are untaught.
- Credit target-letter location/identification without decoding unknown patterns.

### Day 24
- Assess short-a only if already taught and included in the approved class word list.
- If not taught, accept “not yet taught” and assess finding A/a in familiar print.

### Day 25
- Credit correct letter forms, two familiar examples (one containing each target), and a reasonable explanation of the checking process.
