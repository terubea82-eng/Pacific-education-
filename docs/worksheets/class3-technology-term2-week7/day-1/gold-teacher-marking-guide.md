### My marking guide
1. Any three: light; strong; stable base; safe/smooth edges; capacity; secure handle (1).
2. 1 for a valid advantage and limitation: plastic may resist water but cut edges can be sharp; cardboard folds easily but may soften when wet.
3. Safe choice and sensible sequence; adult support for cutting or checking sharp edges (1).
4. Risks such as tearing handle, tipping, items falling, sharp edges; linked preventative feature (1).
5. Same load makes results comparable; additional control e.g. same carrying distance, surface or handling method (1).
6. 1 for labelled functional features and 1 for clear explanation against criteria. Accept equivalent wording. **Total: 6 marks.**

---

**Marking safeguard:** Accept equivalent correct wording, sensible examples and valid accessible responses. Do not treat a single worksheet result as a permanent ability classification. Record prompts and support used.
