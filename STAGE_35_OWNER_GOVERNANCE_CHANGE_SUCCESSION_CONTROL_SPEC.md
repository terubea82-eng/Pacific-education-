# Stage 35 — Owner Governance, Change and Succession Control

**Status:** PREPARED — implementation and owner/legal review required.

## Purpose
Preserve owner control while allowing authorized operational continuity.

## Controls
- owner-controlled governance register;
- explicit authority roles;
- separation of technical, professional and owner decisions;
- material-change approval;
- ownership/IP register;
- succession and continuity records;
- emergency authority with documented scope and expiry;
- conflict-of-interest declaration where applicable;
- change history and evidence retention;
- revocation of former access;
- controlled transfer of operational responsibilities without automatic transfer of ownership.

## Change classes
A — low-risk documentation/quality changes;
B — review-controlled product/data/curriculum/operations changes;
C — protected security, credentials, production authorization and ownership-sensitive changes.

## Completion
Governance changes require the authority appropriate to the change class. Software cannot create ownership, equity, partnership or succession rights.

## Transition
Verified governance records feed the continuous review cycle and affected production stage.

**Boundary:** Any succession, ownership, contractual or estate arrangement requiring legal effect must be separately prepared and reviewed through appropriate professional procedures.
