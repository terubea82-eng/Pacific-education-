# Stage 34 — Legal, Regulatory and Professional Review Cycle

**Status:** PREPARED — qualified professional review required where applicable.

## Purpose
Convert accumulated operational evidence into an organized professional-review package without allowing software to make legal conclusions.

## Review package
- current legal/publication register;
- applicable jurisdiction and population;
- current source/version records;
- privacy and data-governance evidence;
- child-safeguarding evidence;
- education/curriculum evidence;
- cybersecurity evidence;
- accessibility evidence;
- payment/business controls;
- contracts/vendor records;
- complaints/incidents;
- material changes since prior review;
- unresolved issues and proposed questions.

## Workflow
IMPLEMENTED → EVIDENCE_READY → SPECIALIST_REVIEW → LEGAL/PROFESSIONAL_REVIEW → REQUIRED_REVISIONS → OWNER_APPROVAL → PUBLISHED/EFFECTIVE where applicable.

## Automation boundary
Guardian may assemble indexes, summaries, evidence links and review questions. It must not label a requirement legally satisfied merely because evidence exists.

## Completion
Completion requires the relevant professional/authorized reviewer decision and recorded scope/date/version.

**Boundary:** The repository is a preparation and governance system, not a substitute for qualified legal or regulatory advice.
