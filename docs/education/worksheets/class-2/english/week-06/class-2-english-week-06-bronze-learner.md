## Read or listen to this passage

A GARDEN FOR OUR CLASS
Our class made a small garden beside the classroom. First, we loosened the soil and planted bean seeds. Mia watered the garden each morning. Tom pulled out weeds and moved dry leaves. A few days later, tiny green shoots came through the soil. We watched them grow and learned that plants need care.

## Helpful words
loosened · planted · weeds · shoots · care · grow

## Worked example
**Question:** What is the passage mainly about?

**How to work it out:** Look at the title. Notice what happens in most of the sentences. Join those ideas into one short answer.

**Example response:** The class plants and cares for a garden. (Your passage may use more details; answers can be spoken, signed, shown, drawn, or written.)
