## What I am learning
I am learning to notice, sort, read and build words using sound and spelling patterns that my class has already been taught. I will not be expected to use a new or untaught pattern.

## How to take part
Work independently where possible. Extend by explaining, comparing or checking patterns, but do not use untaught phonics rules as evidence of mastery.

## Read or listen to this complete passage

Mia has a red bag. She puts a pen in the bag. The bag is on a mat. Mia gets the bag and sits with her class. She checks the words she has built by comparing each letter with the passage.

**Word bank:** bag · red · pen · mat · gets · sits · class · checks · comparing
