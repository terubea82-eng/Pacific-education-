## Read or listen to the whole passage

> During a classroom tidy-up, Litia finds a smooth stone, a bright yellow pencil and a tiny paper boat. She places the smooth stone beside the pencil. The paper boat is light, so it moves when a gentle breeze comes through the open window. Describing words help a reader picture each object.

**Remember:** A noun (naming word) names a person, place, animal or thing. A describing word gives more information. An expanded noun group puts a noun and one or more describing words together, for example “a red ball”.

## Worked example

Noun: stone. Describing word: smooth. Expanded noun group: the smooth stone. “Smooth” tells the reader what the stone feels like.
