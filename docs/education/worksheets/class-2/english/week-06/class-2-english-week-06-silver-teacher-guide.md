# Teacher-only guide — Class 2 English Week 6 (Silver)

**Days 26–30 | Shared reading and meaning**

**Matching learner file:** class-2-english-week-06-silver-learner.md

## Learning intention
Use details and relationships between ideas to explain what a shared text means. Identify the main idea and supporting evidence.

## Preparation and access
Invite independent silent or paired reading first, then reread aloud if requested. Ask the learner to point to or quote a sentence that supports each response. Accept oral, signed, AAC, drawn, or written evidence.

Read the exact passage in the matching learner worksheet. The passage is repeated here for teacher reference:

> HOW OUR CLASS GARDEN GREW
> Our class decided to turn a bare patch beside the classroom into a garden. We loosened the soil, planted bean seeds, and watered them carefully. Mia checked the soil each morning, while Tom removed weeds that could take water from the young plants. After several days, green shoots appeared. The class understood that growing plants need water, space, and regular care.

## Indicative answer guide
1. **State the main idea in one sentence.**
   - Indicative answer: The class changed a bare patch into a garden and cared for growing plants.
2. **Why did Tom remove weeds?**
   - Indicative answer: They could take water from the young plants.
3. **Which detail shows that the class cared for the seeds regularly?**
   - Indicative answer: Mia checked the soil each morning / they watered them carefully.
4. **What does the word “appeared” mean in this text?**
   - Indicative answer: Became visible / came into view.
5. **Give two needs of growing plants mentioned in the text.**
   - Indicative answer: Water, space, and regular care; any two.

Accept accurate equivalent wording and evidence communicated through speech, sign, AAC, pointing, drawing, sequencing, or writing. Do not penalise spelling or handwriting when these are not the target skill. Give credit for the meaning demonstrated.
