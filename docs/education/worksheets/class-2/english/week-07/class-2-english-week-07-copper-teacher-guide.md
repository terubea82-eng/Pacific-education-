# Teacher-only guide — Class 2 English Week 7 (Copper)

**Days 31–35 | Fluency with familiar texts**

**Matching learner file:** class-2-english-week-07-copper-learner.md

## Learning intention
Build familiarity, accuracy, meaningful phrasing, and confidence through repeated reading or supported access to a short text. Fluency is not speed alone; understanding and comfortable pacing matter.

## Access and teaching support
Use large clear print or read the poem aloud. Track one line at a time with a finger or reading guide. Model a slow first reading and a smooth second reading. Repeat as often as needed; accept speech, sign, AAC, pointing, or drawing.

Use the complete passage printed in the matching learner worksheet. Do not require oral reading where this is not an accessible or appropriate response mode. For learners using AAC, sign, listening, or supported reading, assess the intended comprehension and engagement without treating speech as the only evidence.

## Indicative answer guide
1. **Read or listen to the poem once. What is it about?**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: A little bird in a tree.
2. **Read or listen to the first four lines again. Point to or say one word that tells what the bird does.**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: Sits or sings.
3. **Read one line slowly, then read it again smoothly. Which line did you practise?**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: Any line from the poem.
4. **Choose one line and say, sign, or show what it means.**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: A sensible interpretation of a chosen line.
5. **Read or listen to the whole poem again. Share one thing you remember.**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: Any accurate detail, such as the bird, tree, song, sun, sky, or flying away.

Accept valid equivalent wording and accessible communication modes. Give feedback on one manageable next step (for example, noticing a full stop, rereading a phrase, or recognising an action word). Avoid ranking learners by reading speed.
