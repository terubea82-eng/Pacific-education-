## Marking principles
- Award credit for accurate synonyms, equivalent wording and logically supported answers; do not require the exact model wording.
- Accept spelling errors that do not obscure meaning; record recurring errors for feedback rather than automatically withholding comprehension credit.
- For open responses, assess relevance, evidence, explanation and clarity. Do not penalise a learner for a different defensible interpretation.
- This is a diagnostic guide, not a formal Ministry assessment or a final ability-placement decision.

## Suggested answer points
1. Litia.
2. The water level became low.
3. The committee repaired the tap or put up a notice.
4. To avoid wasting water / to save the shared supply.
5. Reasoned answer: it allows the leak to be repaired and saves water.
6. Two relevant sentences about saving water; accept equivalent wording.
