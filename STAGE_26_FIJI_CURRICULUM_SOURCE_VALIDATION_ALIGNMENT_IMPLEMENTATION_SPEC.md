# Stage 26 — Fiji Curriculum Source Validation & Alignment Implementation Specification

**Status:** PREPARED FOR IMPLEMENTATION — official-source verification required; production remains BLOCKED.

## 1. Authority boundary
Use applicable current official Fiji Ministry of Education prescriptions, syllabi, achievement indicators and formally issued curriculum guidance as the authority for official curriculum claims. Internal content is not an official source.

## 2. Source register
For each source record title, issuing authority, publication/version identifier, date, subject, class/form, term if applicable, official location, retrieval date, reference/checksum where practical, status and reviewer.

## 3. Version control
Every mapping identifies its exact source version. When official curriculum material changes or is under review, affected mappings are flagged for revalidation and are not silently treated as current.

## 4. Mapping
Map each lesson and assessment item to a verified achievement indicator or authorized reference. Record exact source reference, interpretation notes, reviewer and evidence.

## 5. Integrated model
Where the owner-approved model uses approximately 50% core achievement coverage and 50% level-appropriate integration, integration must not displace required core coverage. The allocation remains subject to official requirements and review.

## 6. LANA/LANS and sequencing
Prerequisites and term sequencing are enforced only after the relevant official mapping is verified. Verified Term 1 prerequisites can be enforced before Term 2 where officially required.

## 7. Assessment
Assessment items must trace to covered verified indicators. Exams must not assess uncovered indicators, subject to authorized curriculum requirements.

## 8. Revision and exam scheduling
Teacher revision/exam dates may redistribute remaining verified achievements while preserving required coverage and recording the change.

## 9. Change impact
A changed official source triggers mapping review, affected lesson/assessment identification, reviewer assignment, evidence update and release hold where necessary.

## 10. Evidence
Official source references; version verification; curriculum mapping register; authorized/independent curriculum review; prerequisite evidence where applicable; assessment coverage evidence; change-impact record; resolved defects.

## 11. Completion
Stage 26 is COMPLETE only when official sources are verified, mappings are reviewed, mandatory evidence is VERIFIED, blocking issues are resolved and authorized review is APPROVED.

## 12. Guardian boundary and transition
Guardian may prepare mappings and flag source changes, but cannot declare an internal mapping official or replace Ministry/authorized review. Verified completion prepares Stage 27 controlled-pilot and user-testing evidence.

**Stage 26 remains incomplete until current official-source verification and authorized review occur.**
