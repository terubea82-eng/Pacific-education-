# Teacher-only guide — Class 2 English Week 6 (Copper)

**Days 26–30 | Shared reading and meaning**

**Matching learner file:** class-2-english-week-06-copper-learner.md

## Learning intention
Use the title and key words to understand a short shared text. Respond by speaking, signing, pointing, using AAC, drawing, or writing.

## Preparation and access
Read the passage aloud twice. Point to each sentence. Offer the word bank and picture/gesture choices. Accept one-word answers, pointing, signing, AAC, drawing, or dictated answers.

Read the exact passage in the matching learner worksheet. The passage is repeated here for teacher reference:

> OUR SCHOOL GARDEN
> Our class has a small garden. We plant bean seeds in soft soil. Mia gives the seeds water. Tom removes dry leaves. After some days, green shoots appear. The class is happy to see the plants grow.

## Indicative answer guide
1. **Look at the title. What place will the text tell us about?**
   - Indicative answer: the school garden
2. **Who gives the seeds water?**
   - Indicative answer: Mia
3. **What do the children plant?**
   - Indicative answer: bean seeds
4. **What appears after some days?**
   - Indicative answer: green shoots
5. **Tell or show the main idea of the text.**
   - Indicative answer: The class plants and cares for a garden; the seeds begin to grow.

Accept accurate equivalent wording and evidence communicated through speech, sign, AAC, pointing, drawing, sequencing, or writing. Do not penalise spelling or handwriting when these are not the target skill. Give credit for the meaning demonstrated.
