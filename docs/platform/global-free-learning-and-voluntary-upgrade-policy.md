# PacEdu Global Free Learning and Voluntary Upgrade Policy

**Status:** Staging product and policy specification only  
**Scope:** Global registration, free educational resources, optional paid services and ethical conversion  
**Implementation status:** Not implemented by this document

## 1. Purpose and guiding principle

PacEdu should make useful educational material available to eligible registered users without requiring payment details. Users may choose to purchase additional services when they understand the features, price, terms and alternatives.

The model is **Free to Learn; Optional Paid Expansion**. Free access must be genuinely useful and must not be deliberately degraded to pressure users into paying.

This policy does not create a legal entitlement to third-party content. Every resource must have a verified public-domain status, suitable licence, permission, or other lawful basis for distribution in each relevant jurisdiction.

## 2. Free learning access

Subject to age-appropriate safeguards, account eligibility and lawful content rights, the free library may include:
- Openly licensed or PacEdu-authored reading materials and notes.
- Selected practice worksheets and revision exercises.
- Basic subject explanations, examples and general knowledge.
- Selected audio readings and accessible text formats.
- Public educational resources that PacEdu is authorised to link to or reproduce.
- Basic self-practice activities and access to saved free materials.

Free resources should identify their source, author or publisher where applicable, licence or permission basis, curriculum jurisdiction, class/level, subject, language, version and review date.

Do not describe material as officially curriculum-aligned unless the relevant authorised curriculum source and mapping have been verified. Material for another country's curriculum must be labelled supplementary unless that pathway is formally authorised.

## 3. Optional paid services

PacEdu may offer additional services such as:
- Full structured daily learning pathways.
- Expanded question and worksheet collections.
- Personalised progression and advanced skill reports.
- Authorised teacher workspaces and school administration tools.
- Advanced assessment, analytics and reporting.

These are proposed product tiers, not a statement that every feature is currently available. Each paid feature must be implemented, tested and described accurately before it is advertised or charged for.

Indicative pricing direction, subject to approved terms:
- Standard student price: US$5 per agreed billing unit, or a negotiated country-equivalent rate under a signed agreement.
- Individual learner: US$10 per agreed billing unit.
- Business and organisation pricing: separate existing arrangements.
- The billing unit, period, taxes, renewal and cancellation terms must be stated before purchase. Do not assume monthly, termly or annual billing without an approved decision.

## 4. Fair user journey

1. Explain the free and paid options before registration is completed.
2. Do not require payment-card details to access the free library.
3. Let eligible users begin learning immediately after registration and any necessary age/guardian checks.
4. Clearly mark free, supplementary, trial, sponsored and paid content.
5. Show paid feature descriptions, total price, currency, billing period, renewal, cancellation and applicable taxes before confirmation.
6. Require an affirmative purchase action by the account holder or authorised guardian/payer.
7. Never silently convert a free account or trial into a paid subscription.
8. Provide a clear account page showing current plan, receipts, renewal date and cancellation route.
9. Keep access to already-paid-for entitlements for the period promised in the applicable terms, subject to lawful exceptions.

## 5. Child protection and accessibility

- Do not use manipulative countdowns, shame, pressure, repeated disruptive prompts or child-targeted purchase persuasion.
- Do not ask children to make payments or provide payment credentials. Use a verified parent, guardian or authorised payer workflow where required.
- Do not sell children's personal information or use learning responses for advertising profiles.
- Keep essential accessibility supports and safety information available without a paid upgrade wherever feasible.
- Provide text alternatives and accessible navigation; audio availability must be accurately labelled.
- Avoid advertising in learning activities, assessment screens and child-focused workflows.
- Apply age-appropriate privacy, consent, retention and safeguarding requirements in each operating jurisdiction.

## 6. Content rights and quality controls

Every library item must have a content record containing:
- Stable resource ID and title.
- Creator/publisher and source reference.
- Copyright/licence/permission status and evidence.
- Permitted uses, territories and expiry/review date where applicable.
- Country/curriculum jurisdiction, class/level, subject and language.
- Accessibility format and content version.
- Review status and responsible reviewer.

If rights or source reliability are uncertain, do not distribute the item; quarantine it for review. A link to an external resource must not imply PacEdu owns or endorses that resource.

## 7. Country and curriculum separation

Maintain separate fields for registration country, preferred learning country/curriculum and contracting/billing country. Choosing a curriculum does not make a user a resident, enrolled student or government-sponsored learner of that country.

Free supplementary learning must not be counted as official school enrolment or government-billable student numbers unless the signed agreement explicitly defines it as covered. A later move into an authorised school pathway requires the appropriate school registration, identity/roster checks and teacher or authority approval. Preserve the learner's prior history while keeping supplementary and official assessment records distinct.

## 8. Owner reporting and responsible growth

The Owner dashboard may report aggregated, privacy-protected measures:
- Unique free registrations and active free learners by registration country.
- Paid conversions, renewals, cancellations and sponsored enrolments.
- Free-resource use and completion, in aggregate.
- Revenue, refunds, payment failures and service costs.
- Conversion rates by cohort, without exposing children's personal learning data unnecessarily.

Define each metric and its time window. Deduplicate users across subjects and curriculum selections. Restrict country-level views to authorised roles and audit access to personal information. Do not expose individual children's activity in global marketing reports.

## 9. Transparent upgrade prompts

Upgrade prompts should be occasional, factual and dismissible. Explain the additional capability and price without suggesting that a child is less capable or will fail without payment. Do not interrupt an active assessment or essential learning task. Respect user communication preferences and applicable marketing-consent rules.

## 10. Rollout and acceptance requirements

Before release:
- Legal/content review for copyright, consumer terms, privacy, child protection, tax and subscription requirements in launch jurisdictions.
- Verify free resources and their licences.
- Test that free users can register, access permitted resources, save/reopen materials and cancel or delete accounts as applicable.
- Test that free users are not accidentally charged and that no trial silently renews.
- Test guardian/payer authorisation for child accounts.
- Test country/curriculum/billing separation and access controls.
- Test accessibility, low-bandwidth use and offline behaviour where supported.
- Verify paid feature availability against the actual product before displaying an upgrade offer.
- Review analytics for duplicate counting and unnecessary personal-data collection.

## 11. Explicit non-goals and safeguards

This document does not implement registration, subscriptions, payment processing, content licensing, user analytics, access control or runtime paywalls. It does not authorise changes to Firebase, production data, payment providers, prices or live services. No live charge, receipt or paid subscription may be created solely from this specification.

All implementation must be separately reviewed, tested and approved before merge or deployment.
