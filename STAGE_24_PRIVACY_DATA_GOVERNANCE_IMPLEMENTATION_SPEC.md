# Stage 24 — Privacy & Data Governance Implementation Specification

**Status:** PREPARED FOR IMPLEMENTATION — NOT LEGALLY APPROVED. Production remains BLOCKED until required implementation, evidence, specialist/legal review and owner approval are completed.

## 1. Purpose
Define practical privacy and data-governance controls and the evidence required before Stage 24 can be marked complete.

## 2. Principles
Privacy by design/default; purpose limitation; data minimization; production server authority; deny-by-default access; prototype/test separation; no secrets or unnecessary real child information in source control; auditable changes. Documentation is not proof of legal compliance.

## 3. Data inventory and classification
Maintain a versioned inventory for accounts, identity, profiles, education relationships, learning/progress, assessment/marks, curriculum references, communication, payment/entitlement, support, audit/security and safeguarding data. Every field records purpose, source, owner, sensitivity, access role, retention rule, sharing rule and correction/deletion process.

## 4. Data lifecycle
Define collection, validation, use, storage, access, sharing, retention, archival where justified and secure deletion. Deletion must cover active data, approved copies, offline queues, exports and vendor copies where applicable.

## 5. Processing determination
For each processing activity record jurisdiction, purpose, affected population, authority/basis to be confirmed by the appropriate professional, transparency requirements, risks and unresolved questions. Do not label processing legally compliant without qualified review.

## 6. Children and education data
Minimize child data. Enforce server-side role and verified-relationship controls, restricted exports, monitored administration and safeguarding controls. Child/adult communication requires explicit authorization and safeguarding rules.

## 7. Transparency and requests
Prepare plain-language privacy notices and procedures for applicable access, correction, deletion, objection or other requests. Legally sensitive requests route to appropriate professional review.

## 8. Sharing, vendors and location
Maintain a processor/vendor register covering purpose, data categories, access, location, subprocessors, security, retention/deletion and incident obligations. Record production hosting, backups and international transfers and obtain professional review where required.

## 9. Privacy risk assessment
Maintain privacy risk/impact assessments for high-risk processing, children's data, communications, analytics, integrations and new uses. Record mitigations, residual risk and approval status.

## 10. Data quality
Provide authorized correction workflows. Preserve integrity and audit history. Assessment/marks changes require appropriate authority.

## 11. Incidents
Detect, contain, investigate, document and escalate suspected unauthorized access, disclosure, loss or misuse. Legal notification decisions must follow authorized incident/legal procedures.

## 12. Evidence
Required evidence includes data inventory, privacy-by-design review, access-control tests, retention/deletion tests, request workflow tests, vendor/data-location register, privacy risk assessment where applicable, children's-data safeguards, independent privacy verification and legal/privacy review where required.

## 13. Completion
Stage 24 is COMPLETE only when mandatory evidence is VERIFIED, required review is APPROVED, blocking defects are zero and authorized completion is recorded.

## 14. Guardian boundary
Guardian may prepare documentation, evidence indexes and review-controlled PRs, but cannot declare legal compliance, waive privacy obligations, approve legal wording or authorize production.

## 15. Automatic transition
Verified Stage 24 completion may automatically prepare the Stage 25 accessibility, low-bandwidth and device-verification package.

**Stage 24 remains incomplete until practical implementation and verification occur.**
