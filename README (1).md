# Pacific Education — Final Master

Pacific Education is a locally developed initiative focused on improving learning, teaching and educational opportunities across the Pacific through innovative, inclusive and culturally relevant solutions that empower students, teachers, families and communities.

## Status
Private development / working master.

- Teacher classroom support
- English-language learning
- Early learning and literacy
- Home learning
- Pacific culture and community relevance
- Inclusive education
- Independent practice and support for slower learners
- Digital education resources
