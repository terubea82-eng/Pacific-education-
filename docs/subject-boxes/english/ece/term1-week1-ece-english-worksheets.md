## Day 1 — Listening to and naming familiar objects
**Learning intention:** Listen to a short description and identify familiar objects.  
**PacEdu indicator:** ECE-ENG-T1W1-D1-01 — identifies or names familiar objects from spoken or read descriptions.  
**Materials:** Real objects or tactile-safe classroom objects: cup, ball, book, spoon. Offer spoken, tactile, object-choice or AAC responses.

### Copper — Day 1 learner worksheet
**Instructions:** Listen as your teacher reads: “I can see a cup. I can see a ball.” Touch, point to, select or say the object names.
**Worked example:** Teacher says “book”; learner selects or names the book.
1. Select the cup. Response: ____________________
2. Select the ball. Response: ____________________
3. Say, sign, point to or communicate the name of one object you use at home.  
My response: __________________________________
**Draw, trace, dictate or describe:** An object I know: ____________________
**Teacher marking guide — Copper:** Q1 cup (1); Q2 ball (1); Q3 recognisable familiar object (1); participation using an accessible response mode (1). Total 4. Accept pointing, signing, AAC, tactile selection or spoken answer.

### Bronze — Day 1 learner worksheet
**Instructions:** Listen to the sentences: “The cup holds water. The ball can roll.”
**Worked example:** A book is for looking at pictures or reading.
1. Which object can hold water? ____________________
2. Which object can roll? ____________________
3. Name or select two objects you know.  
First: __________ Second: __________
4. Complete: “I use a __________.”
**Teacher marking guide — Bronze:** Q1 cup (1); Q2 ball (1); Q3 two appropriate object names (2); Q4 sensible completion (1). Total 5. Accept equivalent vocabulary.

### Silver — Day 1 learner worksheet
**Instructions:** Listen to or read: “Mere puts her book beside a cup. Her brother rolls a ball.”
**Worked example:** What does the brother roll? A ball.
1. Name the three objects in the passage. ____________________
2. Which object is used for reading? ____________________
3. Make a sentence about one object. ____________________
4. Explain one way you care for a book or classroom object.  
________________________________________________
**Teacher marking guide — Silver:** Q1 book, cup, ball (3); Q2 book (1); Q3 meaningful sentence (2); Q4 sensible care action (2). Total 8.

### Gold — Day 1 learner worksheet
**Instructions:** Read/listen to: “The class is preparing a reading corner. They place books on a low shelf, put a cup of water on a safe table and keep a ball in the play basket.”
**Worked example:** Why is the ball in a basket? To keep the reading area tidy and safe.

## Day 2 — Recognising and saying first sounds
**Learning intention:** Notice the beginning sound in familiar words.  
**PacEdu indicator:** ECE-ENG-T1W1-D2-01 — recognises or communicates the initial sound in familiar spoken words.  
**Accessibility:** Use clear spoken models, mouth-shape description, tactile/object cues and visual letter cards as optional supports; do not assess hearing or speech ability.

### Copper — Day 2 learner worksheet
**Instructions:** Listen to or feel/say the teacher's model for “sun”. Choose the picture/object that begins like sun from sun, cup and ball.
**Worked example:** “ssssun” begins with /s/.
1. Select the sun object/picture. ______
2. Select the sock from sock, book and cup. ______
3. Match sun to its starting letter: S / B / C.
**Teacher marking guide — Copper:** correct sun (1), sock (1), S (1). Total 3. Accept matching, pointing, signing or AAC.

### Bronze — Day 2 learner worksheet
**Instructions:** Say, sign, select or match the first sound/letter.
**Worked example:** fish begins with /f/ and F.
1. ball begins with: B / M / S.
2. cup begins with: C / T / P.
3. Match: sun—S; moon—M; fish—F.
4. Name another word beginning with /b/ or select one from teacher's objects.
**Teacher marking guide — Bronze:** Q1 B (1); Q2 C (1); Q3 three matches (3); Q4 valid /b/ word (1). Total 6.

### Silver — Day 2 learner worksheet
**Instructions:** Read/listen to each word. Identify its first sound and letter.
**Worked example:** “map” begins with /m/, written M.
1. Write or select the first letter of goat: _____
2. Write or select the first letter of leaf: _____
3. Sort: bag, sun, bed, sock into B words and S words.  
B: __________ S: __________
4. Give one more word beginning with the same sound as “leaf”. __________
**Teacher marking guide — Silver:** Q1 G (1); Q2 L (1); Q3 bag/bed and sun/sock (4); Q4 suitable /l/ word (1). Total 7.

### Gold — Day 2 learner worksheet
**Instructions:** Explain how you know which words begin with the same sound.
**Worked example:** “pan” and “pen” begin with /p/, even though their middle sounds differ.
1. Which two begin with the same first sound: kite, key, sun? Explain.  
________________________________________________
2. Sort: fish, fan, goat, gate into two beginning-sound groups.  
________________________________________________
3. Think of two new words beginning with /m/. __________ / __________
4. Explain why the first letter alone may not tell us every sound in a word.  
________________________________________________
**Teacher marking guide — Gold:** Q1 kite/key and explanation /k/ (2); Q2 fish/fan and goat/gate (4); Q3 two valid /m/ words (2); Q4 recognises spelling-sound complexity in age-appropriate terms (1). Total 9.

## Day 3 — Understanding a short story
**Learning intention:** Listen to or read a short text and answer simple questions.  
**PacEdu indicator:** ECE-ENG-T1W1-D3-01 — responds to literal questions about a short, familiar text.

**Shared story:** “Lani has a red bag. She puts a book in the bag. Lani walks to school with her sister.”

### Copper — Day 3 learner worksheet
**Instructions:** Listen to the story. Point, select, sign, say or use AAC to answer.
**Worked example:** Who has a bag? Lani.
1. Who has a bag? __________
2. What colour is the bag? __________
3. What does Lani put in the bag? __________
**Teacher marking guide — Copper:** Lani (1); red (1); book (1). Total 3.

### Bronze — Day 3 learner worksheet
**Instructions:** Listen/read the story and complete.
**Worked example:** Lani has a red bag.
1. Lani has a __________ bag.
2. She puts a __________ in it.
3. Who walks with Lani? __________
4. Draw, select or describe one thing you take to school. __________
**Teacher marking guide — Bronze:** red (1); book (1); sister (1); appropriate school item (1). Total 4.

### Silver — Day 3 learner worksheet
**Instructions:** Answer in words or complete sentences.
**Worked example:** Where does Lani walk? She walks to school.

4. Put these events in order: walks to school; puts book in bag; has a bag.

## Day 5 — Retelling and sequencing
**Learning intention:** Put familiar events in order and retell them.  
**PacEdu indicator:** ECE-ENG-T1W1-D5-01 — sequences three familiar events and communicates the order.

**Shared sequence:** “First, wash your hands. Next, dry your hands. Last, sit down to eat.”

### Copper — Day 5 learner worksheet
**Instructions:** Listen and put the three picture/object cards in order. You may point, touch, sign or select.
**Worked example:** First means it happens at the beginning.
1. What happens first? __________
2. What happens next? __________
3. What happens last? __________
**Teacher marking guide — Copper:** wash (1); dry (1); sit/eat (1). Total 3.

### Bronze — Day 5 learner worksheet

## Teacher implementation and inclusion notes
- Accept spoken, signed, AAC, tactile selection, pointing, object matching, dictated or written responses according to the learner's communication access.
- Never score a learner down solely for not speaking, hearing, seeing, drawing or holding a pencil. Assess the English learning intention through an accessible response mode.
- Use real, safe familiar objects and large-print/high-contrast or tactile alternatives as needed.
- Give each learner only their own pathway worksheet. Do not require access to another group's paper.
- The pathway is a temporary instructional scaffold, not a permanent label. Reassess using current evidence.
- Teacher marking guides are not student handouts.
- Record teacher review and official source mapping separately. This pack's PacEdu indicators are authored for this draft and are not quoted official Fiji Ministry indicators.
