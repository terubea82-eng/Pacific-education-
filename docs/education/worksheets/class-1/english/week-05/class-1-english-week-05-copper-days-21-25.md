**Learning intention:** Recognise and use the letters S/s and A/a in familiar, previously taught words; review the short-a sound where it has already been taught.

**Important teacher check before issuing:** Use only words and letter-sound correspondences already taught in this class. The sample words below (sat, map, cat, Sam) are conditional practice examples: replace any word not yet taught with an approved familiar example. Do not teach an untaught letter through this review sheet. If short-a has not yet been taught, assess S/s and A/a recognition only and record short-a as not yet taught.

**Ways to answer:** Speak, sign, point, use AAC, select or arrange letter cards, trace, draw, dictate or write. Teacher reads and describes each task. Accept local-language discussion as a bridge; keep the target English letter/word clear.

**Access:** Large-print/high-contrast or tactile letter cards, uncluttered page, sound boxes, extra wait time and repeated directions are available. Do not require speech or handwriting if another response mode demonstrates learning.

**Ability pathway:** Copper — tactile letter matching and adult-guided recognition

## Day 21 — Find S/s and A/a
**Worked example:** Teacher places S and A cards side by side, points to S and says “This is S.” Learner matches the same letter.

1. Match this: **S** → S / A. My choice: __________
2. Match this: **a** → s / a. My choice: __________
3. Point to or circle every S/s on this line: **S  a  s  A  S**
4. Trace or place a letter card to show S: __________

## Day 22 — Explore the letter shapes
**Worked example:** Big S and small s are the same letter in two forms: S — s.

1. Match: S → s / a.
2. Match: A → s / a.
3. Put the matching pairs together: S with ____ ; A with ____.
4. Trace, build or select the letter you want to practise: __________

## Day 23 — Find letters in familiar words
**Teacher word check:** Use only familiar words already taught. If needed, teacher substitutes a known word.

**Worked example:** In the word **sat** (only if taught), point to the first letter: s.

1. Look at **Sam** (only if taught). Find S/s: __________
2. Look at **sat** (only if taught). Find A/a: __________
3. Teacher shows one approved familiar word. Point to S/s or A/a.
Word used: __________ Letter found: __________
4. Match a letter card to the same letter in the word.

## Day 24 — Review with support
1. Choose the capital S: S / A.
2. Choose the small a: s / a.
3. Find S/s in one familiar word chosen by your teacher.
Word: __________
4. If short-a has been taught, listen to the teacher say an approved short-a word and choose its matching picture/card.

## Day 25 — Show what I remember

2. Match A with a.
3. Find one target letter in a familiar taught word.

4. Show one letter you can recognise: __________
5. My reflection: I can recognise _____________________________
