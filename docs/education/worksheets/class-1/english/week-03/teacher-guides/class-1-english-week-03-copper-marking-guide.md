# PacEdu Teacher-Only Marking Guide — Class 1 English
## Week 3 · Days 11–15 · Copper pathway

**Matched learner worksheet:** class-1-english-week-03-copper-days-11-15.md
**Learning intention:** Respond to familiar one-step and two-step spoken directions.

**Fair marking:** Score listening and direction-following, not speech, accent, reading, speed or handwriting. Accept object placement, pointing, signing, AAC, drawing, dictated or written evidence. Record prompt level and response mode.

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| I | Independent |
| P | With visual/gesture prompt |
| M | After adult model |
| NYO | Not yet observed |

### Day 11 — Listen and find
- “Find the book” → selects/indicates book.
- “Find the pencil” → selects/indicates pencil.
- Correct object selection earns the evidence point; a spoken answer is not required.
- Record: ☐ I ☐ P ☐ M ☐ NYO  Notes: __________________

### Day 12 — Follow one direction
- “Book on table” → book is placed/shown on table.
- “Pencil beside book” → pencil is next to book.
- ON/BESIDE choice must match the demonstrated position.
- Record: ☐ I ☐ P ☐ M ☐ NYO  Notes: __________________

### Day 13 — Follow two directions
- First: pick up pencil; next: put it on book.
- First: take book; next: give it to teacher.
- Credit the correct order and actions. If mobility access requires pointing or directing an adult, credit equivalent communication.
- Record: ☐ I ☐ P ☐ M ☐ NYO  Notes: __________________

### Day 14 — Try a new direction
- Book beside bag; pencil in bag.
- Credit correct placement or clear accessible indication.
- Record: ☐ I ☐ P ☐ M ☐ NYO  Notes: __________________

### Day 15 — Review
- Accept any familiar one-step and two-step directions the learner has practised.
- Record what was followed and which support helped.
