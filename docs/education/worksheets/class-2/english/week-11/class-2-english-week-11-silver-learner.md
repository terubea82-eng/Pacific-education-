## Learning focus
I can notice and use describing words (adjectives) to make a description clearer and more precise.

## Read the passage
### A Rainy School Morning
Dark clouds covered the quiet school yard. Soon, heavy rain tapped on the shiny roof. Litia carried her large umbrella to the classroom. Her wet shoes made tiny marks on the floor. She placed them neatly by the door.

## Word bank
dark • quiet • heavy • shiny • large • wet • tiny • neatly

## Worked example
A *flower* becomes more precise when I write **a yellow flower**. The word *yellow* describes the flower. An adjective tells us more about a noun. Some describing words tell colour, size, shape, number, feeling or quality.
