# TEACHER ONLY — Class 1 English Week 20 Marking Guide (Copper)

**Writing: Recount a familiar experience**  
**Programme days:** 96–100 | **Term:** 3 | **Week:** 2  
**Pathway:** Copper | **Keep this guide separate from the learner worksheet.**

## Learning intention
Learner sequences and communicates a short recount with a beginning, middle and ending. Learners may recount a familiar, school-based or imagined event; personal disclosure is never required.

## Passage in the matching learner worksheet
> Look at or listen to the picture story: “I went to the park. I played on the swing. I went home.”

## Worked-example sequence
Beginning: I went to the park. Middle: I played on the swing. End: I went home. Put the events in the order they happened.

## Suggested sequence for passage task
1. I went home.
2. I went to the park.
3. I played on the swing.

## Day-by-day evidence and marking guidance

| Day | Evidence | Teacher guidance |
|---|---|---|
| 96 | Recognises or orders events from a short story | Credit correct event sequence shown by pointing, picture arrangement, speech, sign, AAC or writing. Record if the learner needs the story repeated. |
| 97 | Identifies beginning, middle and ending; plans an experience | Accept a drawing, objects, symbols, dictated ideas or written notes as a plan. Do not require personal/family disclosure. |
| 98 | Orally, visually or with cards rehearses a recount | Look for a sensible sequence. Prompt with first/next/last or beginning/middle/end as needed. |
| 99 | Composes a short recount | Credit meaningful events in an understandable order. Note use of time words and relevant details at the pathway level. |
| 100 | Selects and shares a recount and reflects on sequencing | Record evidence of sequence, clarity and level of support. This is formative evidence, not a permanent ability label. |

## Pathway-specific scaffolding and expected evidence

- **Copper:** Use three clear pictures, objects or gestures for first/next/last. Adult models and repeats. Accept pointing, ordering, signing, AAC, speech or dictated words. Expected evidence: communicates or selects a three-part sequence with support.
- **Bronze:** Provide a four-step picture/word sequence and time-word bank. Expected evidence: orders familiar events and completes a simple recount frame with cues.
- **Silver:** Invite independent planning and a short recount with at least three ordered events. Expected evidence: clear beginning, middle and ending with time words or phrases.
- **Gold:** Invite relevant details, at least two middle events and a coherent ending. Expected evidence: independently composes and reviews a short recount, using sequence language and maintaining logical order.

## Indicative marking and acceptable responses

- For the passage ordering activity, use the order printed in the learner worksheet's sequence list as the answer set; learners should reorder by story chronology, not by the displayed list order.
- Accept equivalent time words and phrases such as “first”, “then”, “next”, “after that”, “later” and “finally” when they express a sensible sequence.
- Accept an imagined or school-based experience if the learner does not wish to share a personal event.
- A recount may be communicated through speech, sign, AAC, picture sequence, dictated text or writing. Do not make handwriting the only evidence of composition.
- Do not penalise spelling, handwriting, accent, speech clarity, eye contact or speed unless explicitly the teaching target.
- No fixed numerical pass threshold or permanent ability placement is assigned. Record: secure / developing / revisit, with evidence and next step.
