# PacEdu Teacher-Only Marking Guide — Class 1 English
## Week 5 · Days 21–25 · Bronze pathway

**Matched learner worksheet:** class-1-english-week-05-bronze-days-21-25.md
**Learning intention:** Identify S/s and A/a and locate them in familiar words already taught.

**Fair evidence:** Accept oral, signed, AAC, pointing, card sorting, dictated or written answers. Record prompts; do not score accent or handwriting.

### Day 21
- S/s letters in sequence: S, s, S, s.
- A/a letters in sequence: a, A, a.
- Capital/small forms: S/s and A/a.

### Day 22
- S → s.
- A → a.
- Credit correct pair construction.

### Day 23
- Word examples only if previously taught.
- In **Sam**, identify S; in **sat**, identify S and A. Substitute class-approved words if these are not taught.
- Do not ask learner to decode any untaught correspondence.

### Day 24
- Identify S/s and A/a in the printed sequence.
- Short-a only if explicitly taught; otherwise use target-letter identification in familiar words.

### Day 25
- Credit correct written/selected pairs and one familiar taught example for each target letter.
