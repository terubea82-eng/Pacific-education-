## 1. Source identity and limits

- Issuer: Fiji Ministry of Education, Curriculum Development Unit.
- Document: *Teachers Guide for Y6 & Y8 Teachers — Term 3*.
- Edition: First edition 2015.
- Official Ministry URL: https://www.education.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2022/07/Y6_Y8_Guideline_2-Teachers_Guide.pdf
- Relevant guide pages: PE pp. 3–5; English reading pp. 6–8; Constitutional/Citizenship pp. 9–10; Financial Education pp. 10–12; Culture p. 13; Road Safety pp. 14–15.
- Scope restriction printed in the guide: lessons are designed for the period **after the external examination**. The guide is not a full-year syllabus or a pre-examination teaching sequence.
- Copyright notice: Ministry ownership; school classroom reproduction is permitted with acknowledgement, while other uses must be referred to the Permanent Secretary through the Director Curriculum Advisory Services. PacEdu stores concise summaries and source references here; it does not republish the guide.
- Current status: the guide is historical (2015). A current 2026 circular or controlled replacement confirming continued use has not been retrieved. Do not call these current mandatory indicators until Ministry/CAS confirms.

## 3. English achievement evidence — source has outcome and achievement statements, not a separately coded indicator ID

### Year 6 English reading

- Source outcome label: EN 6.1.1.
- Source outcome summary: evaluate a range of written texts and construct meaning.
- The guide's achievement-indicator column lists evidence including identifying text types and main ideas, using skimming/scanning, summarising, reading with understanding, applying vocabulary, and answering comprehension questions in complete sentences.
- Source location: p. 6.
- Register treatment: store `EN6.1.1` as a source outcome reference, not as a fabricated achievement-indicator code. Individual evidence statements remain un-coded until the controlled syllabus or CAS confirms their formal identifiers.

### Year 8 English reading

- The guide gives reading/literary-text outcomes and achievement statements but no distinct coded indicator ID in the extracted table.
- Source evidence includes identifying and comparing characters, themes, settings, tone and audience; presenting analysis in an appropriate format; and responding to literary texts through discussion or performance.
- Source locations: pp. 7–8.
- Register treatment: do not invent an EN8 indicator code. Keep as un-coded source achievement statements pending controlled syllabus verification.

## 4. Other achievement-indicator columns without distinct codes

These are included as source leads only. The guide uses achievement-indicator columns, but does not provide a unique formal code for each listed statement in the relevant table. Keep them un-coded pending CAS confirmation.

- **Year 6 Financial Education (pp. 10–11):** identify sources and types of income/wealth; compare income and tax examples; order monetary values; read currencies/exchange values; calculate income and tax; recognise savings/profit and the relationship of income/wealth to personal, family and community well-being.
- **Year 8 Financial Education (pp. 11–12):** identify earned and unearned income; interpret payslips, remittance receipts and bank statement entries; explain remittances; identify personal expenses; use budgets and savings plans; describe the effect of inflation on savings goals.
- **Constitutional/Citizenship (pp. 9–10):** collect information about Fiji as a republic; describe government structure and roles; relate government assistance to communities; present information about peaceful coexistence and social/cultural groups. Do not assign a class level or code beyond what the controlled guide table confirms.
- **Culture (p. 13):** the table includes a coded outcome reference `SS4.1.2.1` and achievement tasks around describing and demonstrating cultural processes and practices. Treat the code as the table's outcome reference, not automatically as an achievement-indicator code.
- **Road Safety (pp. 14–15):** the table contains RS6 and RS8 strands/outcomes and indicator-like codes, but the extracted table repeats some RS6 code labels for different statements and mixes strand/outcome/indicator columns. Do not import the RS6/RS8 rows as validated coded indicator records until the PDF table layout and code hierarchy are visually checked by an educator.
