# Teacher Marking Guide — Silver
## Class 3 · Technology · Term 2 · Week 7 · Day 1

**Matches:** `silver-learner-worksheet.md`  
**Activity:** Planning a simple paper label and selecting tools safely  
**Review status:** `PENDING_TEACHER_REVIEW`  
**Indicator status:** `PENDING_INDICATOR_VERIFICATION`

### Marking guidance

Award marks for correct meaning, sensible steps and relevant reasons. Accept equivalent wording and accessible response modes. Do not require the exact model sentence.

| Item | Expected evidence | Marks |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Three suitable tools/materials with matching purposes: ruler measures/marks; pencil marks; scissors cut; card is the label material; glue stick attaches | 3 |
| 2 | Measuring first ensures the label fits the box and helps plan the correct size | 2 |
| 3 | Four logical steps: measure/mark; check safety; cut on marked line; test fit/attach with glue (accept equivalent safe sequence) | 4 |
| 4 | Stop; do not handle sharp edge; tell teacher/adult; use safe material only with approval | 2 |
| 5 | Identifies likely mismatch/waste/poor fit and proposes measuring before marking/cutting | 2 |
| 6 | Any two: check label dimensions/fit; check words are readable; check edges are neat/safe; check position is straight | 2 |

**Total: 15 marks**

### Acceptable equivalents
- “It might be too big or too small” for a size mismatch.
- “Keep away and tell the teacher” for a sharp edge.
- “Check it before sticking” for testing fit before gluing.

### Evidence record
Note the learner's tool selection, sequencing, hazard response and quality checks. Record support provided. Do not treat an unattempted item as proof of low ability without checking access, understanding and opportunity.
