# Pacific Education — Legal and Professional Procedure Preparation Register

## Purpose
Prepare a controlled register of matters that may require formal legal, regulatory or specialist treatment later.

## Categories
- Privacy and data governance
- Child safeguarding and protection
- Education-sector requirements and curriculum authority
- Cybersecurity and incident response
- Accessibility
- Payment, refunds and consumer/business controls
- Terms of service and user agreements
- Parent/guardian consent where applicable
- Teacher/staff responsibilities and agreements where applicable
- Intellectual property and ownership
- Hosting, vendor and service-provider agreements
- Records retention and deletion
- Complaints and dispute handling
- Insurance and risk allocation where applicable
- Tax, business registration and other applicable commercial requirements

## Status values
PLANNED → IMPLEMENTED → EVIDENCE_READY → SPECIALIST_REVIEW → LEGAL_REVIEW → OWNER_APPROVAL → RELEASE_ELIGIBLE

A status cannot skip required review.

## Required record
Each matter should identify:
- requirement or question;
- applicable jurisdiction and population;
- source/version/date;
- implementation owner;
- evidence;
- reviewer;
- review date;
- unresolved issues;
- decision;
- affected policy/contract/system control;
- release dependency.

## Guardrail
No entry may be labelled LEGALLY COMPLIANT merely because documentation exists. Current applicable requirements and professional review must support any legal or regulatory conclusion.

## Owner control
No external reviewer, funder, provider or technical contributor receives ownership or control merely through participation. Any separate rights must be established through an appropriate lawful agreement.
