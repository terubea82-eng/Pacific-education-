## Read this story
### How Sera Grew a Bean Plant
Sera wanted to grow a bean plant for the class garden. She chose a clean pot, added soil and made a small space for the seed. First, she placed the bean seed in the soil. Next, she covered it gently and watered the pot. She set it beside a sunny window. At first, nothing seemed to happen, but Sera checked the soil each morning. After several days, a green shoot appeared. She continued to give the plant water and light. Finally, its first two leaves opened. Sera recorded the change in her class notebook.

**Word bank:** first • next • at first • after several days • continued • finally • recorded • change

## Worked example
If a story says, “First, Ana puts on her shoes. Next, she walks outside,” the correct order is: 1. Ana puts on her shoes. 2. Ana walks outside. The word **first** tells us which event happens earlier.
