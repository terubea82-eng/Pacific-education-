## What I am learning
I can create a short description or information text for a reader. I can use a title, labels and grouped facts.

You may write, draw, point, sort picture/word cards, sign, use AAC or dictate your answers. Your teacher may record your exact words.

## Read or listen to the complete information text

### A school bag

A school bag carries books and other school things.

It has a space inside for books.

Some bags have pockets for small things.

A handle or straps help a learner carry the bag.

**Useful words:** carries, space, pockets, handle, straps.

## Worked example

A school bag carries books. It has a space inside and may have pockets. A handle or straps help a learner carry it.

A title tells the reader the topic. Facts give information. Labels can group facts that belong together.
