# PacEdu ECE outcome-indicator index — Na Noda Mataniciva

**Purpose:** Operational PacEdu planning index based on the six Foundation Areas of Learning and Development (FALD) and their outcomes in the Fiji Ministry of Education's *Na Noda Mataniciva: Kindergarten Curriculum Guidelines for the Fiji Islands* (2009). This index uses source references and original PacEdu labels; it does not reproduce the protected outcome text.

**Source:** Ministry of Education, *Na Noda Mataniciva: Kindergarten Curriculum Guidelines for the Fiji Islands*, copyright 2009, official Ministry PDF: https://www.education.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Na-Noda-Mataniciva.pdf

**Age/level scope:** Kindergarten / ECE ages 3–6 only. The guideline states that its FALD framework also informs Classes 1 and 2, but this index does not establish the current Year 1 or Year 2 prescriptions. Those must be separately verified.

**Authority and currency note:** The Ministry's 2025 National Pre-Primary Education Policy identifies Na Noda Mataniciva as Fiji's national curriculum guidelines for ages 3–6. The Fiji Government announced on 2 September 2025 that the guideline continued to guide ECE while an update was underway with UNICEF support. Therefore use this as an official Ministry outcome framework for PacEdu ECE planning, with a current-update check required before each release. Do not label it a newly issued statutory syllabus or claim the 2025 update is already incorporated.

**Copyright note:** The source says all rights are reserved and reproduction requires prior written permission (copyright page ii). This index stores FALD/strand/sub-strand names and page references only. It does not copy outcome paragraphs or illustrations. Obtain written permission/licence before reproducing source wording in PacEdu. All PacEdu activity text and explanatory labels must be original and identified as PacEdu-authored.

## Source structure

The guideline explains that teachers should select relevant outcomes, unpack them into concepts, skills and attitudes, and plan learning experiences; sub-strand outcomes are the more detailed planning level (printed page 17; PDF lines 1204–1222). It also says the outcomes are guidelines and children develop at different rates. PacEdu must therefore use developmental observation and responsive support, not treat this as a rigid pass/fail checklist.

## Record format for PacEdu activity mapping

Each activity mapping must store:
- source key (one of the six MATA-ECE keys above);
- exact source page and sub-strand location (teacher verifies against the official PDF);
- PacEdu-authored concept/skill/attitude label (not presented as Ministry wording);
- original play-based activity and materials;
- observable evidence and teacher observation notes;
- child's current developmental evidence and next supportive step;
- accessibility adaptations and home/community context where relevant;
- review status: `PENDING_TEACHER_REVIEW` until a qualified ECE teacher validates it.

Do not invent official outcome codes or copy long outcome passages. Do not translate, alter or publish Ministry source wording as if licensed. The six source keys above are PacEdu indexing keys, not official Ministry codes.

## Release gates

1. Check CAS/Ministry for the revised ECE curriculum announced in 2025 and compare any authorised replacement or supplement with this 2009 framework.
2. Obtain written reuse permission before publishing verbatim outcomes, illustrations or substantial extracts.
3. Have a qualified Fiji ECE reviewer validate each PacEdu concept/activity mapping.
4. Preserve play-based, child-centred, inclusive, developmentally responsive practice; do not impose rigid age cut-offs or high-stakes grades from these outcomes.
5. Keep ECE records distinct from Year 1–13 subject syllabus indicators and the 115-day school timetable distribution.
