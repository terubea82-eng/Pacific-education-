# Teacher-only guide — Class 2 English Week 8 (Copper)

**Days 36–40 | Literal comprehension**

**Matching learner file:** class-2-english-week-08-copper-learner.md

## Learning intention
Locate and communicate information explicitly stated in a short text; use the text as evidence rather than guessing.

## Access and teaching support
Read the short passage aloud or together. Pause after each sentence. Point to the sentence that contains the answer. Offer two spoken, signed, pictured, or AAC choices when helpful.

## Daily teaching and evidence

**Teaching move:** Reread and complete the comprehension questions. Encourage checking each answer against the passage.

## Indicative answer guide
1. **What colour was Lani's hat?**
   - Answer: Red.
2. **Where did Lani put her hat first?**
   - Answer: On the bench.
3. **What happened when she came back?**
   - Answer: The hat was gone.
4. **Where did Lani find the hat?**
   - Answer: Beside a bag.
5. **What did Lani do after she found it?**
   - Answer: She picked it up and smiled.

Accept accurate equivalent wording and accessible communication modes. For literal comprehension, answers should be supported by information directly stated in the passage. Do not penalise spelling or handwriting where these are not the target skill.
