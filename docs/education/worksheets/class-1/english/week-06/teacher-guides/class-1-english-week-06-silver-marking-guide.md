# PacEdu Teacher-Only Marking Guide — Class 1 English
## Week 6 · Days 26–30 · Silver pathway

**Matched worksheet:** class-1-english-week-06-silver-days-26-30.md
**Learning intention:** Explain print's purpose, distinguish letters from words and use spaces as word boundaries.

**Fair evidence:** Accept oral, signed, AAC, dictated or written work. Do not penalise accent or handwriting.

### Day 26
- Accept valid community print examples (sign, label, book title) and a reasonable explanation of the message.
- Title names the book or gives a clue to its topic.

### Day 27
- A=L; go=W; t=L; sun=W; M=L.
- Credit two different letters and a familiar word.
- Explanation: a letter is one alphabet symbol; a word is made from one or more letters and carries meaning.

### Day 28
- **I like my class.** = four words.
- **We play at school.** = We / play / at / school.
- Credit “spaces separate words” or equivalent explanation.

### Day 29
- Check answers against the actual familiar text selected by the teacher; do not impose a single title or word.
- Accept correct identification of capital/small letters and reading direction shown for the chosen print.

### Day 30
- Credit meaningful print example, letter/word distinction, sentence with spaces and explanation of how spaces help readers recognise words.
