# Teacher-only guide — Class 2 English Week 6 (Bronze)

**Days 26–30 | Shared reading and meaning**

**Matching learner file:** class-2-english-week-06-bronze-learner.md

## Learning intention
Use the title, sequence words, and important details to build meaning from a shared text. Respond in an accessible way.

## Preparation and access
Read aloud or offer paired reading. Pause after each sentence and invite the learner to point out a key detail. Give extra wait time and allow oral, signed, AAC, drawn, or written responses.

Read the exact passage in the matching learner worksheet. The passage is repeated here for teacher reference:

> A GARDEN FOR OUR CLASS
> Our class made a small garden beside the classroom. First, we loosened the soil and planted bean seeds. Mia watered the garden each morning. Tom pulled out weeds and moved dry leaves. A few days later, tiny green shoots came through the soil. We watched them grow and learned that plants need care.

## Indicative answer guide
1. **What is the text mainly about?**
   - Indicative answer: The class making and caring for a garden.
2. **Where did the class make the garden?**
   - Indicative answer: Beside the classroom.
3. **Name one job Mia or Tom did.**
   - Indicative answer: Mia watered it / Tom pulled weeds or moved dry leaves.
4. **What happened a few days after planting?**
   - Indicative answer: Tiny green shoots came through the soil.
5. **What did the class learn about plants?**
   - Indicative answer: Plants need care.

Accept accurate equivalent wording and evidence communicated through speech, sign, AAC, pointing, drawing, sequencing, or writing. Do not penalise spelling or handwriting when these are not the target skill. Give credit for the meaning demonstrated.
