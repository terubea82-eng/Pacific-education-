## Learning focus for this practice

Listen to or read a short sentence, connect words to meaning, and communicate one clear idea. The teacher selects the band using evidence from the Day 1 diagnostic. A learner may answer by speaking, pointing, signing, drawing, using communication support, or writing.

## Student reading card

**At the school gate**

Mere walks to school with her brother. They stop at the gate. Mere greets her teacher. She puts her bag beside her desk.

## Supported start

1. Point to or choose the picture/word that shows where Mere stops: **gate / desk**.
2. Show who walks with Mere: **brother / teacher**.
3. Choose or say one word from the story: **school / fish**.
4. Tell, point, sign or draw one thing Mere does.

## Developing

1. Who walks to school with Mere?
2. Where do Mere and her brother stop?
3. Match **teacher** to its meaning: **a person who helps children learn / a place to sleep**.
4. Complete: “Mere greets her ______.”

## Expected-level practice

1. Where does Mere put her bag?
2. Put these actions in story order: **greets her teacher / walks to school / puts her bag beside her desk**.
3. Write or dictate one sentence about something you do when you arrive at school.
4. Find a word or sentence in the reading card that supports one of your answers.

## Advanced extension

1. Explain why Mere might greet her teacher.
2. Retell the story in three sentences, keeping the events in order.
3. Write a new ending sentence that fits the story.
4. Identify one detail from the story that helped you understand what Mere did at school.
