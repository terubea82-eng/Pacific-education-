## Learning intention and evidence
Learner demonstrates that a simple sentence communicates a meaningful idea; orders familiar words; identifies or uses capital at the start, spaces between words and a full stop at the end; creates a simple caption.

## Access and marking rules
Accept speech, pointing, sign, AAC, card arrangement, dictation, drawing or writing when it demonstrates the target. Score literacy skill, not speech/accent, handwriting neatness, speed or disability-related access needs. Give wait time. Use familiar, taught words. Accept equivalent meaningful word order and valid captions.

## Worked example
Model: [is] [The] [red] [bag] → The red bag. Check capital, word order, spaces and final full stop.
Teacher models meaning first, tracks each word, then checks capital, spaces and final full stop. Do not mark untaught spelling patterns as failure of this sentence objective.

## Suggested answer checks (not rigid answer key)
- Capital letter begins the sentence; words are separated; a full stop closes a statement.
- Reordered cards should form a meaningful sentence. Accept equivalent familiar vocabulary and valid grammar taught in class.
- Captions relate to the selected picture/object and communicate a clear idea. Do not require one exact sentence.
