### Read the full passage

**Making a name label**

Class 3 is making a name label for a book box. The learners choose clean paper or thin card. They use a ruler to measure and mark a straight line. A pencil makes the guide mark easy to see. Blunt-ended scissors cut along the marked line. A glue stick attaches the finished label to the box.

The learners first place the materials on a clear desk. They sit while cutting, keep fingers away from the cutting edge and cut slowly away from their bodies. When passing scissors, they hold the closed blades and offer the handle to the next person. They never use a damaged tool. If a tool is broken or has a sharp edge, they stop and tell the teacher.

### Worked example

**Question:** Why use a ruler before cutting?  
**Model answer:** A ruler helps measure and mark a straight line, so the label can be cut to the planned size.
