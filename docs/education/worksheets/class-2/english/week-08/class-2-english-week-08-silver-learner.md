## Read or listen to this passage

A HAT FOUND
During break, Lani placed her red hat on a bench near the classroom. She hurried outside to join a game and forgot to take it with her. When the bell rang, she returned to the bench, but the hat was no longer on top. Lani checked underneath and looked around the nearby bags. She found the hat beside her friend's school bag. She thanked her friend for keeping it safe and put it away.

## Helpful words
