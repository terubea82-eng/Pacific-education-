## Purpose and pathway support
Learner completes the shared objective with minimal prompting, using only taught correspondences and vocabulary. Offer accessible response modes.

## Complete practice text and worked example
Before school, Mere packs a book in her red bag. She checks that her lunch is inside. Then she walks along the path to school.

Model: “Mere has a red bag.” Identify Mere as the person and “a red bag” as what she has. Demonstrate how to locate the relevant words or matching picture. Think aloud, then complete a second example with the learner.

## Suggested response checks
- Who has the bag? **Mere.**
- What is in the bag? **A book** (Copper text).
- Where does Mere go/walk? **To school.**
- Accept other accurate details stated in the applicable pathway's text.
- For Bronze, Silver and Gold, accept the details and sequence supported by their respective passage.
- For Gold's reason question, credit a sensible explanation linked to the text; do not require one exact phrase.
- For the additional sentence, credit a relevant, meaningful sentence that fits the passage.

## Marking and accessibility
- Assess the stated review objective, not handwriting, accent, speech production or speed unless explicitly taught and assessed.
- Accept oral response, pointing, matching, sign, AAC, drawing, dictated words or writing where these demonstrate the target.
- For comprehension, record the detail or text/picture evidence used.
- Do not penalise unrelated spelling or punctuation when not the focus.
- Record prompts honestly: independent / with prompt / with model / not yet observed.
- Copper: adult modelling, reduced choices, repeated directions and wait time.
- Bronze: visual cue, word bank and guided practice, with prompts reduced where possible.
- Silver: minimal prompting and familiar taught vocabulary/correspondences.
- Gold: extension through reasoning, comparison, an extra sentence or self-check; do not introduce untaught code as mastered.
- Pathways are adjustable supports, not fixed learner labels.
