## My own prompt — read or listen
Picture prompt described in words: after a rain shower, a boy places his wet shoes beside the classroom door. Learners may use a teacher drawing, objects or an oral description.

**Think about the idea:**

Who? a boy. When? after the rain. What action? places. What? his wet shoes. Where? beside the classroom door.

## Worked example

After the rain, the boy places his wet shoes beside the classroom door.

The sentence tells an idea. It starts with a capital letter, has spaces between words and ends with a full stop. Your teacher may point out each part while reading it aloud or showing it.
