## Read or listen to the whole passage

> Read or listen: “On Monday, Jone brings a green lunch box to school. He puts a banana inside it. At break time, Jone shares the banana with Ana.”

**Remember:** “Who” asks about a person. “What” asks about a thing or action. “Where” asks about a place. “When” asks about a time. Listen to the whole question and give an answer that matches it.

## Worked example

Question: What does Jone bring? Answer: A green lunch box. The question asks what he brings, so I find that information in the first sentence.
