# PacEdu · TEACHER-ONLY MARKING GUIDE
## Class 1 English · Week 3 · Days 11–15
**Pathway:** Copper  
**Learning intention:** Listen for key words, understand order/position words, and follow familiar one- and two-step spoken directions (with Gold extension to three-step direction creation).

**Important marking rule:** Assess listening comprehension and direction-following, not accent, speech production, reading ability, speed, or handwriting. Accept demonstration, object placement, pointing, signing, AAC, drawing, dictated or written evidence. Record the response mode and support level. The pathway is a temporary scaffold, not a permanent learner label.

**Materials:** Familiar classroom objects, picture cards, sequence strip, large counter. Check safe object size and accessible placement.

| Evidence code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| I | Independent after the instruction was given |
| P | Completed with a visual/gesture prompt |
| M | Completed after adult modelling |
| NYO | Not yet observed; do not treat as failure |


## Pathway-specific support
Adult models each direction once, then gives one short direction at a time. Use real objects, gesture/picture cues and two choices. Allow extra wait time and accept pointing, placing, signing, AAC or dictated answers.

### Day 11 · Listen and choose
**Read aloud:** “Put the pencil beside the book.”

**Expected evidence / marking:** Draw or place a tick beside the object you moved: pencil / book.

**Worked example and observation:** Teacher places pencil and book in view. Learner listens and points to or places the pencil beside the book.
