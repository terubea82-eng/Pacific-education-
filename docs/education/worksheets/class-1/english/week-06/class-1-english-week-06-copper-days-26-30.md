**Learning intention:** Understand that print carries meaning, distinguish a letter from a word, and notice how print is arranged on a page.

**Teacher preparation:** Use a familiar, approved picture book or short printed class text. Check that its cover/title and words are visible. Do not require learners to decode text beyond what has been taught. Use a clean, enlarged copy or tactile/raised print where useful.

**Ways to answer:** Point, speak, sign, use AAC, trace, match, draw, dictate or write. Teacher reads instructions aloud and describes visual information. Accept a response by eye gaze or partner-assisted scanning where appropriate.

**Access:** Large print, high contrast, uncluttered print samples, tactile letter cards, a line guide, extra wait time and repeated instructions. Do not treat visual tracking or speech as the only way to demonstrate understanding.

**Ability pathway:** Copper — tactile/large-print matching and adult-guided exploration

## Day 26 — Print carries a message
**Worked example:** Teacher shows a familiar book cover and says, “This is a book. The picture and printed words tell us what the book is about.”

1. Touch, point to or select the book cover.
2. Show the picture on the cover.
3. Show where the printed title is, with teacher support.
4. Choose what print can do: tell us a message / make a sound by itself.
My choice: ______________________

## Day 27 — Find letters and words
**Worked example:** Teacher shows a large **S** card. “This is one letter.” Teacher shows **sun** on a card. “This is a word made from letters.” Use the word only if already taught; otherwise use a familiar printed word.

1. Match the single letter card **A** to the same letter.
2. Match the familiar word card to the same word.
3. Point to which card shows one letter: **m** / **map**.
4. Point to which card shows a word: **s** / **sun**.

## Day 28 — Look at spaces in print
**Worked example:** Teacher shows **I see a cat.** and points to the gap between words.

1. Look at **I see a cat.** Find one space between words.
2. Put a finger or pointer on one word. Teacher reads it aloud.
3. Match a printed word card to the same word on the sentence strip.
4. Show where one word ends and the next begins.

## Day 29 — Find print on a page
1. On the familiar page, point to or select the title.
2. Find one letter that your teacher names.
3. Find one familiar word that your teacher names.
4. Show where the teacher should start reading on this page. You may point, use a line guide or follow a tactile marker.
