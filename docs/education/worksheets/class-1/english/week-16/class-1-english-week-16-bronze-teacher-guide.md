# TEACHER ONLY — Class 1 English Week 16 Marking Guide (Bronze)

**Comprehension: prediction and connection**  
**Programme days:** 76–80 | **Term:** 2 | **Week:** 7  
**Pathway:** Bronze | **Do not place this answer guide in the learner download.**

## Learning intention
Learner makes a sensible prediction from text clues and connects the text to familiar knowledge or experience. All pathways address the same intention; supports and response complexity differ.

## Passage used in the matching learner worksheet
> Mere looks outside. Dark clouds cover the sky. She puts an umbrella in her bag before leaving home. What might happen on her way to school?

## Worked-example rationale
I notice dark clouds and an umbrella. I predict that it may rain on Mere's way to school. The clues support my idea.

## Day-by-day marking guidance

| Day | Evidence to look for | Marking guidance |
|---|---|---|
| 76 | Identifies or selects a relevant clue and makes/shows a plausible prediction | Accept any sensible prediction supported by the passage. Credit pointing to a clue, selecting a symbol, signing, AAC, drawing or dictated response. |
| 77 | Notices the relevant weather/event clue and explains or indicates a possible next event/action | Credit the meaning, not exact wording. Accept equivalent words such as “rain”, “shower” or “wet weather” where relevant. |
| 78 | Makes a personal or familiar connection and identifies a meaningful similarity | The experience need not be identical. Credit a relevant connection communicated in any accessible mode. Do not require disclosure of a personal or distressing experience; a familiar observed example is acceptable. |
| 79 | Applies prediction independently or with the pathway's planned support and names/shows a clue | Record prompt level. For Gold, distinguish stated facts from inference and accept more than one defensible inference. |
| 80 | Gives a final supported prediction and a relevant connection/reflection | Record evidence and next step. Do not turn this formative task into a permanent ability label. |

## Pathway-specific scaffolding and expected evidence

- **Copper:** Adult reads passage aloud, uses tactile/large-print or picture choices, points to clue with learner, allows wait time and one-step prompts. Expected evidence may be one selected clue plus one plausible picture/gesture/word prediction. Do not require independent writing.
- **Bronze:** Provide a visual cue and small word/picture bank; model one response, then reduce prompts. Expected evidence: a relevant prediction and a simple clue or connection, orally, by drawing, pointing, signing, AAC or writing.
- **Silver:** Offer minimal prompts and allow learner to identify a clue and explain a prediction/connection. Expected evidence: a plausible prediction supported by at least one relevant clue and a meaningful connection.
- **Gold:** Invite two clues, comparison or reasoned response; distinguish text fact from inference. Expected evidence: a defensible prediction supported by multiple clues, plus a relevant connection or comparison. Do not demand one predetermined prediction.

## Indicative responses — not an exclusive answer key

- Possible prediction: It may rain on the way to school. Clues: dark clouds and umbrella in the bag. Connection: carrying rain protection before going outside.

## Marking principles

- Credit accurate meaning and valid equivalent wording; do not insist on the sample sentence.
- Do not mark a prediction wrong solely because a different plausible outcome is given. Check whether the learner used a relevant clue and whether the prediction is sensible.
- Distinguish what the passage states from what the learner predicts. Do not credit an unsupported guess as text evidence, but acknowledge it as an idea if appropriate.
- For a connection, accept a relevant personal, family, community, observed-story or prior-learning link. Never require sensitive personal disclosure.
- Assess comprehension, not handwriting, spelling, accent, speech clarity, eye contact or speed unless one is explicitly the learning target.
- Accept oral, sign, AAC, pointing, matching, drawing, dictated or written responses. Record the response mode and any access support.
- No fixed numerical pass threshold is assigned in this draft. Teacher records: secure / developing / revisit, with evidence and next step.
