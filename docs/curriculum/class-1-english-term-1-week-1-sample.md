## Week focus

**Concepts:** listening to instructions, oral vocabulary, identifying initial sounds, matching a simple word to a picture, and understanding a short sentence.

**Learning intention:** Learners practise listening, speaking, noticing the first sound in familiar words, and answering simple questions about a short text.

**Success checks:** the learner can attempt the task orally, by pointing, by selecting a picture, or in writing; identify the target sound in familiar examples; and answer a simple who/what question with suitable support.

**Materials:** teacher voice, board or paper, locally familiar pictures/objects (e.g., sun, bag, bus, mat), and pencils if available. Do not require families to buy materials or bring devices.

## Day 1 — Initial check and learner support

**Purpose:** observe what each learner can currently do. This is a starting check, not a final grade or permanent ability label.

1. Ask the learner to listen to a one-step instruction: “Point to the bag.”
2. Show two or three familiar pictures/objects and ask the learner to name or indicate one.
3. Say “sun” slowly and ask what sound the word begins with; allow pointing to a matching picture or repeating the word.
4. Read aloud: “Mere has a bag.” Ask “Who has a bag?”
5. Invite the learner to draw, point, speak, or write one thing they can see at school.

**Record:** task attempted, support needed, response mode, and observed skill. Keep notes private and linked to the correct student ID only after the identity has been confirmed by the teacher.

**Support routes**
- **Supported start:** offer two picture choices, repeat the instruction, and model one answer.
- **Developing:** offer a short prompt or first-sound model, then allow another attempt.
- **Class-goal practice:** use the tasks without a model first.
- **Extension:** ask the learner to give another word with the same initial sound and explain how they know.

**Teacher note:** do not infer a learner's overall ability from one short check. Consider language background, hearing/vision access, confidence, attendance, and familiarity with the words.

## Day 2 — Listening and the first sound /s/

**Teach:** listen to a word, say it, and notice its first sound.

**Worked example:** “sun” begins with /s/. Say the word naturally, then stretch the first sound: “ssssun.” Avoid adding an extra vowel after the consonant sound.

**Guided practice:** sun, bag, mat. Ask which word begins with /s/.

**Daily tasks**
1. Point to or select the picture for “sun”.
2. Circle or mark the word/picture that begins with /s/ from: sun, mat, bag.
3. Say or select another /s/ word from teacher-checked examples.
4. Listen to: “The sun is up.” Answer: “What is up?”

**Four flexible versions**
- **Supported start:** choose between sun and bag after hearing each word; teacher models once.
- **Developing:** choose the /s/ word from two familiar picture/word choices.
- **Class-goal practice:** choose the /s/ word from three choices and answer the sentence question.
- **Extension:** name or draw another word beginning with /s/ and explain the first sound.

## Day 3 — Match a familiar word to meaning

**Teach:** a word can name a person, place, animal, object, or action. Match a spoken word to its picture or object.

**Worked example:** teacher says “bag”; learner points to the bag.

**Daily tasks**
1. Match **bag** to a picture/object.
2. Match **mat** to a picture/object.
3. Listen to “Mere has a bag.” Answer: “What does Mere have?”
4. Complete orally or in writing: “I see a ____.” Choose a familiar object.

**Four flexible versions**
- **Supported start:** match one spoken word to one of two pictures.
- **Developing:** match two words to two pictures.
- **Class-goal practice:** match three words and complete the sentence.
- **Extension:** create a new sentence using one matched word and draw a picture to show its meaning.

## Day 4 — Understand a short sentence

**Read aloud:** “Mere has a bag. The bag is red.”

**Explain:** the sentence tells us about Mere and her bag. The second sentence gives one detail about the bag.

**Daily tasks**
1. Who has a bag?
2. What colour is the bag?
3. Choose a picture that matches the two sentences.
4. Draw the bag or select a matching picture; describe it using a word or short sentence.

**Four flexible versions**
- **Supported start:** answer by pointing to Mere and choosing between two colours/pictures.
- **Developing:** answer the two questions using one-word responses.
- **Class-goal practice:** answer both questions and complete “The bag is ____.”
- **Extension:** add one sensible detail to the description and explain whether it was stated in the text or added by the learner.

## Day 5 — Practise, show learning, and choose the next step

**Short review**
1. Listen and select the word that begins with /s/: sun / mat.
2. Match **bag** to its picture.
3. Listen to: “Mere has a bag. The bag is red.” Answer: “Who has a bag?”
4. Answer: “What colour is the bag?”
5. Choose a response mode: say the answer, point, draw, select, or write.

**Feedback pattern:** say what was correct, show or explain the correct response, and give one retry. Example: “You chose the sun. Sun begins with /s/. Try finding another word that starts the same way.”

**Next-step rule:** assign extra modelling and a simpler matching task if needed; give independent practice when the learner is ready; offer extension when the learner demonstrates the skill. Recheck later rather than treating a single result as permanent.

## Printable student worksheet — Class 1 English, Week 1

**Name or student ID:** ____________________  **Date:** ____________________

### A. Listen and choose
Teacher says the words aloud. Circle or point to the word/picture that begins with /s/.

1. sun / mat
2. bag / sun
3. sun / bag

### B. Match
Draw a line or point to match each word to the correct picture/object.

- bag
- mat
- sun

Teacher supplies or draws three clear pictures. If pictures are unavailable, the teacher may use safe real objects or read the words aloud.

### C. Listen to the short text
“Mere has a bag. The bag is red.”

1. Who has a bag? ____________________
2. What colour is the bag? ____________________

### D. My sentence
Complete orally, by selecting a word, or in writing:

“I see a ____________________.”

**Accessibility:** learners may answer orally, point, use picture/word cards, draw, or write. Do not penalise a learner for using an approved alternative response mode.

## Teacher-only answer guidance and marking criteria

Keep this section separate from the student worksheet until the teacher releases it.

- A1: sun
- A2: sun
- A3: sun
- B: each word matched to the corresponding teacher-provided picture/object
- C1: Mere
- C2: red
- D: accept a sensible word that completes the sentence; score meaning and participation appropriate to the learner's current instruction, not handwriting alone.

**Suggested formative record:** record each item as independent / completed with prompt / not yet shown / not attempted. Do not convert this short sample directly into a high-stakes grade. Check the exact school assessment policy before assigning percentages.

## Revision activity

1. Listen to three familiar words and identify which begins with /s/.
2. Match a word to a picture/object.
3. Listen to the two-sentence text and answer who/what questions.
4. Retry one item previously completed with support.

Use new familiar words only after the teacher checks that learners know the words. Keep revision linked to skills already taught.
