# Class 2 General Science — four ability worksheets

Status: original pilot activities; verify current Fiji Class/Year 2 Science syllabus outcomes before release. Flexible bands: Support 0–49%, Developing 50–64%, Expected 65–79%, Advanced 80–100%.

## A. Support (0–49%) — Living things

**Example:** A fish is an animal and needs a suitable place to live.

1. Name one animal.
2. Name one plant.
3. Which needs water to live: a living plant or a clean empty cup?
4. Match: fish — water; bird — nest/tree as a place to rest.
5. Give one way to care for an animal safely.

**Teacher guide (8 marks):** reasonable examples (2); living plant (1); matches showing a suitable habitat/resting place (3); safe caring action (2).

## B. Developing (50–64%) — Materials and changes

1. Name an object made of metal.
2. Name an object made of wood.
3. What happens to an ice cube when it is warmed?
4. Is melting a change from solid to liquid or liquid to solid?
5. Why should a learner ask an adult before heating an object?
6. Record one observation before and after ice melts.

**Teacher guide (10 marks):** suitable examples (2); melts into water/liquid (2); solid to liquid (2); hot objects can burn or be dangerous (2); observation records state before and after (2).

## C. Expected (65–79%) — Weather and observations

Read: “On Monday, the sky was cloudy and the wind was strong. On Tuesday, the sky was clear and the wind was light. The class recorded the weather at the same time each day.”

1. Which day was cloudier?
2. Which day had lighter wind?
3. Why is it useful to record weather at the same time each day?
4. Make a two-row table comparing Monday and Tuesday.
5. Name one safe action during a thunderstorm.

**Teacher guide (10 marks):** Monday (1); Tuesday (1); fair comparison (2); accurate table (4); safe shelter indoors and away from open areas/trees (2).

## D. Advanced (80–100%) — Design an investigation

Two seedlings are similar in size. One is watered daily and the other is not watered for several days.

1. What question could this investigation answer?
2. What should be kept the same?
3. What is changed?
4. What should be measured or recorded?
5. Predict what may happen and explain why.
6. Why must the learner not leave a plant without water for too long?

**Teacher guide (12 marks):** clear question (2); same plant type, soil, pot and light (2); watering (2); height/leaf condition/observations (2); sensible prediction linked to water (2); care and avoid harming the plant (2).

## Access and release
Use safe demonstrations, written instructions, tactile/large-print descriptions and screen-reader-friendly content. Provide a visual or written alternative to sound-based observations. Never require students to touch, taste or heat unknown materials. Confirm current syllabus outcomes before release.
