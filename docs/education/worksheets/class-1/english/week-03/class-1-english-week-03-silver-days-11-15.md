**Learning intention:** I listen for important words and follow familiar one-step and two-step directions.

**Ways to answer:** Speak, sign, point, use AAC, move an object, draw, dictate or write. The teacher reads and describes each task. The learner may ask for a direction to be repeated. Do not score accent or disability-related speech differences as errors.

**Teacher safety/access note:** Use familiar safe classroom objects and actions. Demonstrate first where indicated. Never require running, climbing, touching another person or handling unsafe items. Adapt print size, tactile cues, audio description, movement and response mode while keeping the learning intention.

**Ability pathway:** Silver — core task with minimal prompting
