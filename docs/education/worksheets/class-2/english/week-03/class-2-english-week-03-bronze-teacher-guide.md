## Learning intention and boundary
Learners review, sort, segment, blend, build, read and check familiar words using sound and spelling patterns already taught in this class. Confirm the exact pattern set from the current class teaching record before use. Do not introduce an unverified pattern or mark an untaught pattern as a deficit.

## Complete text used in the matching learner worksheet

Mia has a red bag. She puts a pen in the bag. The bag is on a mat. Mia gets the bag and sits with her class.

**Word bank:** bag · red · pen · mat · gets · class

## Worked example used
For “pen”, say the word, stretch the sounds, then blend them back together. Find “pen” in the passage and check that each letter matches the taught pattern.

## Pathway-specific support and marking
- **Copper:** Model one step at a time; reduce the number of choices; use concrete letter tiles, tactile/symbol cues, matching and repeated short practice. Credit accurate matching or building when it demonstrates the target.
- **Bronze:** Provide a word bank and one visual cue; model once, then allow an independent attempt. Credit correct use of a taught feature even if the explanation is brief.
- **Silver:** Invite independent selection, segmentation, blending and checking. Ask the learner to identify the clue used.
- **Gold:** Invite a justified comparison or transfer to another teacher-confirmed, previously taught example. Do not reward guessing or untaught-rule recall as evidence of this week's target.

## Marking principles
1. Credit valid equivalent words, explanations and response modes where they demonstrate the learning intention.
2. Do not require spelling terminology unless it has been taught.
3. For an oral, signed, AAC, matched, pointed, drawn or dictated response, record what the learner actually did.
4. Distinguish independent evidence from prompted or modelled performance.
5. Do not use a weekly score to permanently assign or rank a learner's pathway.
6. If the class has not yet been taught the pattern required by an item, adapt the item and record the change.
