# Class 2 English — Week 11 (Silver Pathway)

**Teacher-only guide — Days 51–55: Adjectives and Precise Description**

**Matching learner file:** class-2-english-week-11-silver-learner.md  
**Pathway:** Silver  
**Status:** PENDING_TEACHER_REVIEW  
**Official indicator mapping:** PENDING_VERIFICATION

## Teaching intention
Support learners to identify adjectives in meaningful text, connect each adjective to the noun it describes, and select precise vocabulary when speaking, signing, using AAC, drawing with labels or writing.

## Suggested daily teaching sequence
- **Day 51:** Read the passage aloud or provide accessible text. Model finding a describing word and the noun it modifies.
- **Day 52:** Ask learners to locate a detail in the passage and explain what the adjective tells them.
- **Day 53:** Compare possible words. Accept a suitable alternative where it preserves the meaning.
- **Day 54:** Model expanding a noun phrase with one or more relevant adjectives.
- **Day 55:** Invite an independent description and ask the learner to explain one word choice.

## Indicative responses and marking notes
- **Day 51:** Accept any accurate adjectives from the passage and require the learner to identify the noun described where appropriate.
- **Day 52:** Accept the explicit matching detail from the passage; see the learner text.
- **Day 53:** Accept a contextually suitable adjective, including an equivalent word not listed in the word bank.
- **Day 54:** Look for a noun phrase or sentence with a relevant describing word; do not require one fixed answer.
- **Day 55:** Look for relevant detail, understandable meaning and appropriate adjective use. Credit valid equivalent wording.

## Accessibility and differentiation
Offer enlarged print, uncluttered page presentation, extra processing time, repeated reading, tactile or picture cues, a scribe, sign, AAC or oral response. Do not penalise a learner for choosing an accessible communication mode. Reduce the number of choices when needed while retaining the same learning intention. Extend confident learners by asking them to compare near-synonyms and justify which is more precise.
