## Read or listen to the whole passage

> At the classroom table there is a blue cup, a round ball and a long green leaf. The cup is small. The ball is soft. The leaf is dry. Describing words tell us more about naming words.

**Remember:** A noun (naming word) names a person, place, animal or thing. A describing word gives more information. An expanded noun group puts a noun and one or more describing words together, for example “a red ball”.

## Worked example

Naming word: cup. Describing word: blue. Expanded noun group: the blue cup. The word “blue” adds information about the cup.
