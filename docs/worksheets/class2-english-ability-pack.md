# Class 2 English — four ability worksheets

Status: **original pilot material; official Fiji syllabus outcomes not yet mapped or teacher-verified.** Confirm the current Class/Year 2 English syllabus before classroom release.

**Teacher setup:** Write the date and current learning outcome on the student copy. Give one ability version based on recent English evidence; reassess regularly. Suggested bands: Support 0–49%, Developing 50–64%, Expected 65–79%, Advanced 80–100%. These are flexible support bands, not fixed labels.

## Worksheet A — Support (0–49%)

**Learning focus:** Read a short sentence, find key words, and write a simple sentence.

**Example:** “Mere has a bag.” Who has a bag? **Mere.**

Read: “A fish is in the pond. Joeli sees the fish.”

1. Who sees the fish?
2. Where is the fish?
3. Circle the correct word: fish / dish — “A ___ is in the pond.”
4. Fill the gap: Joeli ___ the fish. (sees / sleeps)
5. Put the words in order: **has / Mere / a book**
6. Write one sentence about something you see at school.

**Teacher marking guide (10 marks):** Q1 Joeli (1); Q2 in the pond (1); Q3 fish (1); Q4 sees (1); Q5 “Mere has a book.” (2); Q6 meaningful sentence, capital and full stop (up to 4). Accept equivalent clear wording.

**Next step:** Practise reading short sentences aloud or silently, then identify who/what/where.

## Worksheet B — Developing (50–64%)

**Learning focus:** Understand a short passage and use complete sentences.

**Example:** “Litia waters the plant.” What does Litia do? **She waters the plant.**

Read: “On Monday, Saki and Ana planted bean seeds near their classroom. They watered the seeds each morning. After some days, small green shoots appeared.”

1. What did Saki and Ana plant?
2. Where did they plant the seeds?
3. When did they water the seeds?
4. What appeared after some days?
5. Choose the best title: (a) The Lost Shoe (b) Bean Seeds (c) A Bus Ride.
6. Write two sentences about caring for a plant.

**Teacher marking guide (10 marks):** Q1 bean seeds (1); Q2 near their classroom (1); Q3 each morning (1); Q4 small green shoots (1); Q5 (b) (1); Q6 two relevant sentences (up to 5: meaning 3, sentence punctuation/capitals 2).

**Next step:** Answer who/what/where/when questions using evidence from the passage.

## Worksheet C — Expected (65–79%)

**Learning focus:** Infer information and explain an idea from a passage.

**Example:** If the ground is wet after rain, it probably rained recently. This is an inference supported by a clue.

Read: “Before break, the class put rubbish into the correct bins. When the wind blew, one empty wrapper escaped from an uncovered bin. Alipate picked it up and told the class that the lid should be closed.”

1. What did the class do before break?
2. Why did the wrapper escape?
3. What clue shows that Alipate cared for the school?
4. Suggest a better title.
5. What lesson can readers learn? Give one reason from the passage.
6. Write a short, four-sentence paragraph about keeping your school clean.

**Teacher marking guide (12 marks):** Q1 sorted rubbish into bins (1); Q2 wind blew it from uncovered bin (2); Q3 picked it up/told class to close lid (2); Q4 suitable title (1); Q5 reasonable lesson plus evidence (2); Q6 relevant paragraph (up to 4: ideas 2, order 1, punctuation/capitals 1).

**Next step:** Support opinions with a detail from the text.

## Worksheet D — Advanced (80–100%)

**Learning focus:** Compare ideas, evaluate a solution, and write a persuasive paragraph.

Read: “Two groups wanted to reduce litter. Group A placed a sign beside the bins. Group B placed a sign, explained how to sort waste, and checked the bins after lunch for one week. By Friday, Group B found fewer items on the ground, but the class had not counted the litter before the project began.”

1. Name one difference between the two groups' plans.
2. What evidence suggests Group B's plan may have helped?
3. Why can the class not be completely certain that the plan caused the improvement?
4. Propose one fair way to check whether the plan works next week.
5. Write a persuasive paragraph (5–7 sentences) encouraging learners to reduce litter. Include a clear claim, two reasons, and a practical action.

**Teacher marking guide (12 marks):** Q1 explanation plus checking/teaching difference (2); Q2 fewer items by Friday (2); Q3 no starting count / other factors possible (2); Q4 fair repeated count using same place/time/method (2); Q5 claim (1), two relevant reasons (2), practical action and clear writing (1).

**Next step:** Evaluate evidence, recognise limits, and propose a fair check.

## Accessibility and delivery

- **Blind/low vision:** Provide clean linear text for screen readers, large print as needed, verbal reading on request, and Braille/tactile materials through the school's support arrangements. Do not require color-based answers.
- **Deaf/hard of hearing:** All instructions and passage content are written; provide clear picture cues where useful. No task depends on hearing a recording.
- Allow oral, typed, dictated, or supported responses where the learning outcome permits. Do not change the assessed skill without documenting the adjustment.

**Teacher release check:** Confirm the current Fiji syllabus outcome, adjust local vocabulary, check reading level, retain this answer key in the teacher workspace, and approve before student release.
