## What I am learning
I can plan an idea and make a meaningful simple sentence. I can share my idea by writing, drawing, arranging word cards, signing, using AAC or dictating. My teacher can record my exact words.

## My own prompt — read or listen
Picture or object prompt: a girl carries a blue book to a classroom table. The teacher can draw a simple picture or use a real book and table.

**Think about the idea:**

Who? a girl. What does she do? carries. What? a blue book. Where? to the classroom table.

## Worked example

The girl carries a blue book to the table.

The sentence tells an idea. It starts with a capital letter, has spaces between words and ends with a full stop. Your teacher may point out each part while reading it aloud or showing it.
