**Ways to answer:** Speak, sign, point, use AAC, select or arrange letter cards, trace, draw, dictate or write. Teacher reads and describes each task. Accept local-language discussion as a bridge; keep the target English letter/word clear.

**Access:** Large-print/high-contrast or tactile letter cards, uncluttered page, sound boxes, extra wait time and repeated directions are available. Do not require speech or handwriting if another response mode demonstrates learning.

**Ability pathway:** Bronze — visual cues and guided letter/word search

## Day 21 — Identify S/s and A/a
**Worked example:** S is the capital form; s is the small form. A/a are another matching pair.

1. Circle every S or s: **S  a  s  A  S  b  s**
2. Underline every A or a: **a  S  A  m  a  s**
3. Write or select the two forms of S: ______ and ______.
4. Write or select the two forms of A: ______ and ______.

## Day 22 — Match capital and small letters
**Worked example:** S matches s; A matches a.

1. S → s / a.
2. A → a / s.
3. Draw a line: S ___ a; A ___ s. Correct the pairings.
4. Write one letter pair neatly or build it with cards:
____________________________________________________________

## Day 23 — Find the target letters
**Teacher word check:** Use only words already taught. Replace any unfamiliar sample.

## Day 24 — Review
1. Circle S/s: **a  S  t  s  m**
2. Circle A/a: **s  A  m  a  t**
3. Choose one familiar word from your class word bank. Find the target letter:
Word: __________ Target letter: __________
4. If short-a has been taught, select or say one approved short-a word. Do not guess a new word.
