Accept equivalent correct wording, oral or signed responses, and reasonable sequencing. Award credit for the concept even when grammar or spelling is imperfect.

### Acceptable alternatives
- “Pass handle first” / “give the other person the handle.”
- “Tell an adult” / “tell the teacher and wait for help.”
- For Q7 accept “so tools do not fall,” “so paper does not get mixed up,” or another relevant safety/organisation reason.

### Evidence record
Record which steps the learner can explain independently and which require prompting. A single result is not a permanent ability classification.
