# PacEdu Teacher-Only Marking Guide — Class 1 English
## Week 4 · Days 16–20 · Silver pathway

**Matched learner worksheet:** class-1-english-week-04-silver-days-16-20.md
**Learning intention:** Identify and compare sound categories and familiar rhyme patterns.

**Fair marking:** Accept oral, signed, AAC, picture, drawn, dictated or written evidence. Do not assess accent or handwriting.

### Day 16 — Sound sorting
- BIRD CALL → animal.
- CLAP → human-made.
- Credit one sensible sound example and safe representation.

### Day 17 — Rhyme
- SUN–FUN.
- BELL–SHELL.
- Credit explanation that final sounds/patterns match.

### Day 18 — Compare
- CAT–HAT rhyme.
- SUN–FUN rhyme.
- BELL–CAT do not rhyme.
- Explanation can be “the endings sound different” or equivalent.

### Day 19 — Create
- CLAP–TAP.
- SUN–FUN.
- Accept another familiar, accurate rhyme pair and a meaningful sentence.

### Day 20 — Review
- Credit two correct rhyme pairs and one pair that does not rhyme.
- Learner explains or demonstrates the difference.
