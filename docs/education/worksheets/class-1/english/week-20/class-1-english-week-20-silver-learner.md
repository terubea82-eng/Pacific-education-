## What I am learning
I can tell or write about an experience in the order it happened. I can plan a beginning, middle and ending.

You may write, draw, arrange picture/word cards, sign, use AAC or dictate your ideas. Your teacher may record your exact words. You do not have to share private family information. You may write about a school activity, a game, a garden task or an imagined event instead.

## Read or listen to the whole story

> Last Friday, Saki went to the school field with his class. First, the learners lined up beside the classroom. Their teacher led them to the field, where they played a passing game with a soft ball. After the game, Saki and his friends collected the equipment. Finally, the class walked back to the room and washed their hands.

## Worked example — put events in order

Beginning: Last Friday, Saki went to the field with his class. Middle: They played a passing game and collected the equipment. End: They returned to class and washed their hands. The time words show the sequence.
