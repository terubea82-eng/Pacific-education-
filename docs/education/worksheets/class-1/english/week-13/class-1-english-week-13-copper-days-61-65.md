## How I can respond
You may say, point, sign, use AAC, dictate, draw, arrange picture cards or write. Take your time.

## My story — read this page
Mere has a ball. She is at school. The ball is by a tree.

## My pathway support
Read the story aloud twice. Offer a large-print or tactile picture/scene, two choices at a time and adult pointing. Learner may point, sign, use AAC, dictate, draw or write.

## Worked example
Teacher models: “Who is the story about?” Find the name in the story. Example answer: “Mere.” Then model: “Where does the story happen?” Find the place word(s), such as “school”. A **character** is who the story is about. The **setting** is where the story happens.
