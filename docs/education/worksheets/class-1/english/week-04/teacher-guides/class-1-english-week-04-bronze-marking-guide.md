# PacEdu Teacher-Only Marking Guide — Class 1 English
## Week 4 · Days 16–20 · Bronze pathway

**Matched learner worksheet:** class-1-english-week-04-bronze-days-16-20.md
**Learning intention:** Identify familiar environmental sounds and match rhyming words.

**Fair marking:** Accept matching, pointing, signing, AAC, drawing, dictation or writing. Record prompts; do not score accent or speech clarity.

### Day 16 — Sound identification
- Soft clap → CLAP.
- Soft desk tap → TAP.
- Accept a drawing or named/selected familiar sound.

### Day 17 — Rhyme
- CAT–HAT.
- SUN–FUN.
- Credit recognition that the words sound alike at the end.

### Day 18 — Matching
- BELL–SHELL.
- CLAP–TAP.
- “Ending” is the correct choice for the part that sounds alike.

### Day 19 — Apply
- HAT–CAT.
- FUN–SUN.
- Accept safe sound demonstration or picture selection.
- Explanation may be “same ending sound” or an equivalent demonstration.

### Day 20 — Review
- Accept two correct pairs from the week's taught set: CAT–HAT, SUN–FUN, BELL–SHELL, CLAP–TAP.
- Accept one sound represented through picture, action or description.
