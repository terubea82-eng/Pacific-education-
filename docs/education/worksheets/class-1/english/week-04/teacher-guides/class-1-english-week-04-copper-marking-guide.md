# PacEdu Teacher-Only Marking Guide — Class 1 English
## Week 4 · Days 16–20 · Copper pathway

**Matched learner worksheet:** class-1-english-week-04-copper-days-16-20.md
**Learning intention:** Notice familiar sounds and identify familiar rhyming word pairs.

**Fair marking:** Accept pointing, matching, signing, AAC, gesture, sound/action demonstration, drawing, dictation or writing. Do not assess speech clarity, accent, hearing ability or handwriting. Record access method and prompts.

| Code | Meaning |
|---|---|
| I | Independent |
| P | With picture/gesture cue |
| M | After adult model |
| NYO | Not yet observed |

### Day 16 — Listen and notice
- Correct response matches the gentle clap/tap or visual representation.
- BELL is an object that can make a sound; CAT is an animal, not a sound source in this example.
- Record response mode and whether the learner recognises one familiar sound.

### Day 17 — Explore rhyme
- CAT–HAT is the target pair.
- SUN–FUN is the target pair.
- BELL–SHELL is the target pair.
- Credit selection/matching; learner need not pronounce the words.

### Day 18 — Practise together
- CAT–HAT; BELL–SHELL; CLAP–TAP.
- Credit correct matching with the support level recorded.

### Day 19 — Try a new choice
- SUN–FUN; HAT–CAT.
- Safe sound demonstration may be a soft clap or tap, or selecting a picture.

### Day 20 — Review
- Credit one correctly selected rhyme pair and one familiar sound representation.
- Record what support helped.
