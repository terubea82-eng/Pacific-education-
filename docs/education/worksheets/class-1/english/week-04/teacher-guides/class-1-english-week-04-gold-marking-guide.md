# PacEdu Teacher-Only Marking Guide — Class 1 English
## Week 4 · Days 16–20 · Gold pathway

**Matched learner worksheet:** class-1-english-week-04-gold-days-16-20.md
**Learning intention:** Classify sounds, justify rhyme choices and compose a short rhyme.

**Fair marking:** Accept spoken, signed, AAC, dictated or written work. Assess sound/rhyme understanding and message clarity, not accent or handwriting.

### Day 16 — Classify sounds
- Rain → natural.
- Bell → object-made sound.
- Clap → human-made.
- Description may include soft/loud, long/short or high/low.
- Safe representation avoids sudden or excessive volume.

### Day 17 — Analyse
- SHELL–BELL.
- TAP–CLAP.
- Explanation identifies matching final sound pattern.
- Accept other familiar accurate pairs.

### Day 18 — Compare
- CAT–HAT rhymes; CAT–SUN does not.
- BELL–SHELL rhymes; BELL–FUN does not.
- Credit one valid rhyming and one non-rhyming pair.

### Day 19 — Compose
- “The sun is bright. I play with fun.” The supplied choice FUN completes the intended rhyme.
- For learner-created lines, credit a pair of words with matching final sound and a meaningful two-line composition.

### Day 20 — Review
- Two correct rhyming pairs, one non-rhyming pair and an explanation of checking the endings.
- Credit reflection that describes a real improvement or learning step.
