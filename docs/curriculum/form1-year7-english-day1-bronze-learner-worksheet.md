## How to work
Read the passage twice. Underline the problem and circle the actions taken.

## Read this passage

### A Shared Water Tank

At the edge of Namosi Village, a shared water tank supplies water to several nearby homes. During a dry week, the water level became low. Litia noticed that a tap was dripping. She told the village water committee. The committee repaired the tap and placed a clear notice beside the tank: “Turn the tap off after use. Take only the water you need.”

The families discussed other ways to save water. They agreed to report leaks quickly and keep the area around the tank clean. By working together, they helped protect a resource that everyone depended on.

**Word help:** *committee* — a group chosen to help organise or make decisions; *resource* — something useful that people need or use.

## Worked example — read the steps
Question: What did Litia notice? Step 1: Locate the sentence beginning “Litia noticed”. Step 2: Identify the noun phrase “a tap”. Step 3: Answer in a complete sentence: Litia noticed that a tap was dripping.
