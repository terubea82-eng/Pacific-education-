# PacEdu Fiji curriculum subject register

Status: **curriculum verification register — not a claim that every subject below is compulsory at every level.**

## Rule before writing or publishing a worksheet

1. Use the current Fiji Ministry of Education syllabus/prescription for the selected year/form and subject.
2. Record the document title, publication/revision year, syllabus strand/outcome, and page or section.
3. Do not label a worksheet “officially curriculum-aligned” until a teacher or curriculum specialist checks the match.
4. Generate only subjects that apply to the selected class, school programme, and (for senior secondary) student's approved subject choices.
5. Keep a separate teacher answer/marking guide. Student views must never expose answer keys before the teacher releases them.
6. Use local Fiji contexts respectfully and safely. Do not use stereotypes or assume every learner has the same home language, religion, resources, or access to devices.

## Levels PacEdu must support

- ECE / Kindergarten: play-based learning and development across the six Foundation Areas of Learning and Development in Na Noda Mataniciva. Do not force a secondary-style subject worksheet structure onto ECE.
- Primary: Class/Year 1 to 8. Build each year's subject list from the currently approved primary syllabuses and the school's offered programme.
- Secondary: Forms 3 and 4 (also referred to as Years 9 and 10 in the year-based structure): use the actual approved subject syllabus. The project's requested science label is **Basic Science** for Forms 3–4; verify this name against the school's current syllabus before publishing.
- Senior secondary: Forms 5–7 (Years 11–13): create separate packs only for the subjects the student is actually enrolled in. Chemistry, Physics, and Biology must be separate packs when those subjects are offered/selected; do not assume every student takes all three.

## Subject catalogue to verify, not a universal compulsory list

The following are candidate subject families to check against official syllabuses and each school's programme:

- Language and literacy: English and approved language subjects (for example iTaukei, Hindi, Rotuman or other language offerings where prescribed).
- Mathematics.
- Science: primary/general Science; Basic Science for Forms 3–4 as requested, pending exact syllabus-name verification; senior Chemistry, Physics and Biology as separate subjects where offered.
- Social studies and citizenship-related learning.
- Health, physical education, and wellbeing.
- Arts, crafts, and music where included in the relevant syllabus.
- Technology, digital/computer studies, and applied learning where included.
- Secondary elective, commerce, agriculture, technical, vocational, and other specialist subjects only where the current syllabus and school programme confirm them.

This catalogue is deliberately not presented as an official class-by-class subject list. The Fiji National Curriculum Framework identifies six Foundation Areas for ECE and seven Key Learning Areas for primary and secondary schooling, while the specific subjects and prescriptions must be checked at the appropriate level. Fiji announced a review of the National Curriculum Framework in 2025, so the register must be refreshed when the Ministry issues updated requirements.

## Worksheet production standard

Each pack should include:
- Class/year/form, subject, date, term/week/day, curriculum source and verified outcome (or clearly marked **PENDING VERIFICATION**).
- Four distinct ability versions for school-age learners: Support 0–49%, Developing 50–64%, Expected 65–79%, Advanced 80–100%. These are flexible instructional bands, not permanent labels; placement is subject-specific and updated from evidence.
- A short instruction, worked example, real questions, and a progression check.
- Teacher-only answers, mark allocation, acceptable alternatives, and a simple reteach/extend suggestion.
- Inclusive alternatives: screen-reader-friendly text and tactile/Braille-ready materials where appropriate; captions, written instructions, clear visuals and non-audio response options where appropriate. Never make color, sound, or internet access the only way to complete a task.
- Downloadable student worksheet separated from teacher answer key.
- Review and release by a qualified teacher before students receive it.
