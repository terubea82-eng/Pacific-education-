## Term 1, Week 1 — Days 1–5

## Day 1 — Listening and speaking: Two Stories of Belonging
**Core message:** belonging grows when people are welcomed and can participate in ways that feel safe. Evidence: learners chose what to contribute; no one was pressured to share private family information; visitors asked thoughtful questions and learned about shared and different traditions.
**Marking (5):** 1 central message; 1 each for two relevant details; 1 explanation of choice/respect; 1 coherent response. Accept paraphrase and accessible communication.
**Pathway evidence:** Copper selects the message and one detail; Bronze uses the summary frame; Silver gives independent summary and response; Gold compares choice and respectful questions with evidence.

## Day 2 — Reading and viewing: Restoring a Stream
**Core idea:** a community is monitoring and caring for a stream safely over time. Details: volunteers record cloudy areas/rubbish; they plant suitable vegetation with the landowner; they compare notes and photographs. Inference: records help identify change and where further advice is needed.
**Marking (5):** 1 central idea; 1 each for two details; 1 inference; 1 evidence link. Accept any defensible inference supported by the passage.
**Pathway evidence:** Copper identifies message and one detail; Bronze uses the frame; Silver supplies two details and an inference; Gold distinguishes observed facts from inference.

## Day 3 — Vocabulary and language: A Local Enterprise
**Core answers:** durable = able to last; practical = useful/suitable for a purpose; survey = questions used to collect information; feedback = comments/information used to improve. Other contextually accurate definitions and synonyms are acceptable.
**Marking (6):** up to 1 point each for four word meanings; 1 for a suitable synonym; 1 for a justified precise word choice or accurate revised sentence.
**Pathway evidence:** Copper matches meanings; Bronze completes definitions; Silver explains and rewrites; Gold analyses connotation and audience.

## Day 4 — Grammar and sentence craft: Improving an Information Report
**Core answer:** “A clean space is safer and more welcoming.” Other accurate combinations include “Walkways should be kept clear because everyone uses them.” Accept valid alternatives.
**Marking (5):** 1 each for accurate revisions, suitable conjunction, reduced repetition, correct punctuation/capitalisation and explanation of one revision.
**Pathway evidence:** Copper combines one supported pair; Bronze combines two pairs; Silver revises at least three sentences; Gold edits for audience, cohesion and precision.

## Day 5 — Writing and shaping: A Proposal for Change
**Marking (6):** 1 clear problem; 1 audience/people affected; 1 realistic proposal; 1 each for two supporting reasons; 1 practical action/closing request. Gold may earn evidence of inclusive access measure and response to a likely concern within the same objective.
**Pathway evidence:** Copper uses a frame to identify a problem and action; Bronze drafts two short linked paragraphs with support; Silver drafts two developed linked paragraphs; Gold writes a persuasive two-paragraph proposal for a named audience and revises it.

## Access, marking and learner placement
- Accept Braille, large print, screen-reader text, audio directions, sign, AAC, pointing, eye gaze, object choice, dictation, typing, scribing or handwriting.
- Provide extra processing time, sensory breaks and adapted tools. Do not infer low ability from disability, speech, accent, handwriting or lack of device access.
- Record actual evidence, response mode, prompt level and next teaching step.
- Copper/Bronze/Silver/Gold are flexible evidence-based support pathways, not permanent labels.
- Accept equivalent wording and valid alternative answers. Do not require one memorised sentence.
- Keep teacher review and official source mapping pending until reviewed against the authorised Fiji source, edition, exact outcome/indicator and page.
