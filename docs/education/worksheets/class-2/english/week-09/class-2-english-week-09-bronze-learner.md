## Read this story
### A Seed for the Garden
On Monday, Sera found a bean seed in the garden shed. She filled a pot with soft soil and made a small hole. First, she placed the seed in the hole. Next, she covered it with soil and watered it. She put the pot where it could receive sunlight. A few days later, a green shoot pushed through the soil. Sera watered the plant when the soil felt dry. Finally, two leaves opened and the plant began to grow.

**Word bank:** first • next • then • a few days later • finally • sunlight • shoot • opened

## Worked example
If a story says, “First, Ana puts on her shoes. Next, she walks outside,” the correct order is: 1. Ana puts on her shoes. 2. Ana walks outside. The word **first** tells us which event happens earlier.
