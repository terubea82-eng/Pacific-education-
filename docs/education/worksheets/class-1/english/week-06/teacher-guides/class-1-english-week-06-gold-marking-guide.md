# PacEdu Teacher-Only Marking Guide — Class 1 English
## Week 6 · Days 26–30 · Gold pathway

**Matched worksheet:** class-1-english-week-06-gold-days-26-30.md
**Learning intention:** Analyse how print communicates, how words are built from letters, and how spaces/line breaks organise text.

**Fair evidence:** Accept oral, signed, AAC, dictated or written work. Mark understanding and clarity of message, not accent or handwriting.

### Day 26
- Accept two valid community print examples and accurate messages.
- Title helps identify/select the book; picture and title can jointly preview its topic/content.

### Day 27
- Letters: T, a, S. Words: we, read.
- Credit a familiar word and identification of its component letters.
- Explanation distinguishes one alphabet letter from a meaningful word formed from letters.

### Day 28
- **Our class reads every day.** = five words.
- Credit correct boundaries and a sensible explanation that missing spaces make word boundaries unclear.
- Accept a clear four- or five-word sentence.

### Day 29
- Verify first/last words, capital/small letters and direction against the actual selected page.
- Credit a reasonable explanation of how spaces and line breaks organise print.

### Day 30
- Credit two print/message examples, letter/word distinction, sentence with accurate word count and one print feature explanation.
