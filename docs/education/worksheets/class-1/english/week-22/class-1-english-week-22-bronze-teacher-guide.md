# TEACHER ONLY — Class 1 English Week 22 Marking Guide (Bronze)

**Writing: Description and information**  
**Programme days:** 106–110 | **Term:** 3 | **Week:** 4  
**Pathway:** Bronze | **Keep this guide separate from the learner worksheet.**

## Learning intention
Learner creates a short description or information text for a clear audience, using a title, labels/grouping and relevant facts. The objective is shared across pathways; scaffolding and independence vary.

## Complete source text in the matching learner worksheet

### A school bag

A school bag carries books and other school things.

It has a space inside for books.

Some bags have pockets for small things.

A handle or straps help a learner carry the bag.

**Useful words:** carries, space, pockets, handle, straps.

**Worked example:** A school bag carries books. It has a space inside and may have pockets. A handle or straps help a learner carry it.

## Pathway-specific scaffolding and expected evidence

- **Copper:** Use real school objects or large/tactile pictures and a small choice set. Scribe learner's dictated fact if needed. Expected evidence: identifies the topic and communicates one relevant fact with support.
- **Bronze:** Provide a word bank, picture cues and a simple title/fact/parts frame. Expected evidence: creates or dictates a short text with a title and at least two relevant facts, with prompts.
- **Silver:** Invite independent selection and grouping of facts under simple labels. Expected evidence: creates a short organised information text with a title and several relevant facts.
- **Gold:** Invite a clear reader-focused text with grouped facts, precise vocabulary and a useful concluding detail. Expected evidence: independently organises relevant information and checks that each sentence contributes to the topic.

## Indicative content and marking principles

- Topic is a school bag and its purpose, parts, use, organisation or care. Credit any accurate, relevant fact; learners are not required to reproduce the source wording.
- Possible facts include carrying books/materials, compartments, pockets, handles, straps, organisation and checking for damage. Accept other correct facts based on a real bag observed in class.
- Possible labels include PURPOSE, PARTS, USE, ORGANISING and CARE. Accept learner-created labels that group facts logically.
- Credit a meaningful title, relevant information and a logical grouping/order. Do not require a fixed number of facts beyond the pathway expectation.
- Accept sign, AAC, pointing, picture/word-card sorting, drawing with explanation, dictation or writing. A teacher may scribe the learner's exact words.
- Do not penalise developing spelling or punctuation unless it is the explicit teaching target. Do not make handwriting or speech the only evidence.
- No fixed numerical pass threshold or permanent ability placement is assigned. Record: secure / developing / revisit, with evidence and next step.
