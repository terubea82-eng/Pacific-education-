## Consistent pathway and access rules

All pathways address the same weekly learning intention. Copper, Bronze, Silver and Gold are adjustable teaching supports, not fixed ability labels or a permanent placement. Use actual learner evidence to adjust support; do not rank learners from weekly scores.

- **Copper:** explicit adult modelling, smaller task steps, picture/word/tactile supports, reduced choice sets, repeated short directions and generous wait time. Accept pointing, matching, speech, sign, AAC, drawing, movement or dictation where these show the target.
- **Bronze:** one worked example, visual cue, small word bank and guided practice; reduce prompts when possible.
- **Silver:** same core objective with minimal prompting; use taught vocabulary and spelling/sound patterns.
- **Gold:** same objective with an accessible unfamiliar example, reason, comparison, extra detail or independent editing. Do not treat untaught content as mastered.

Every learner worksheet developed from this plan must be a complete standalone document with learner name, class, subject, term/week, date and pathway; full passage/instructions; worked example; five-day tasks; generous answer spaces; accessible response options. Each pathway also requires its own separate teacher-only guide with indicative answers, evidence and marking guidance. Do not combine pathways into one learner download.

## Teacher assessment and marking guidance

- Assess the stated learning target, not accent, public speaking confidence, handwriting or speed unless explicitly taught and assessed.
- For comprehension, accept accurate responses by speech, pointing, matching, sign, AAC, drawing, dictation or writing where these demonstrate understanding. Record text evidence where required.
- For phonics and spelling, assess only the sound patterns and spelling conventions already taught. Distinguish sound identification, letter recognition, decoding and spelling.
- For grammar, assess meaning and use in context; do not treat isolated terminology as proof of sentence competence.
- For writing, use a transparent teacher rubric for meaning, organization and the specific skill being taught. Credit valid equivalent wording; do not rely on one exact phrase.
- MCQ checks, where used, must use A/B/C/D only. Keep answer keys and marking guidance in the teacher-only area.
- Copper/Bronze/Silver/Gold are adjustable supports, not labels of learner potential. Record evidence and allow movement between pathways.
