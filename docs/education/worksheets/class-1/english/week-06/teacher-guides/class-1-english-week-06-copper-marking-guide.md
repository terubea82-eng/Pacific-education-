# PacEdu Teacher-Only Marking Guide — Class 1 English
## Week 6 · Days 26–30 · Copper pathway

**Matched worksheet:** class-1-english-week-06-copper-days-26-30.md
**Learning intention:** Recognise that print communicates a message and distinguish a single letter from a word.

**Fair evidence:** Accept pointing, tactile matching, eye gaze, partner-assisted scanning, sign, AAC, gesture, drawing, dictation or writing. Do not require decoding, speech or independent visual tracking.

### Day 26 — Print carries a message
- Learner locates the cover/picture and, with support, the printed title.
- Credit the choice that print can tell a message/name.

### Day 27 — Letter and word
- **m** is a single letter; **map** is a word (use only if familiar; substitute if not taught).
- Credit matching a single-letter card and a familiar word card.

### Day 28 — Spaces
- Credit locating a gap between printed words in **I see a cat.**
- Accept pointer, tactile marker or partner-assisted selection.

### Day 29 — Print on a page
- Credit locating title, teacher-named letter/word and indicating the starting point for the teacher's reading.

### Day 30 — Review
- Credit showing one letter, one word and one word space; accept an accessible response to “Print can…”
