# Teacher Marking Guide — Gold
## Class 3 · Technology · Term 2 · Week 7 · Day 1

**Matches:** `gold-learner-worksheet.md`  
**Activity:** Designing, planning and checking a safe paper label  
**Review status:** `PENDING_TEACHER_REVIEW`  
**Indicator status:** `PENDING_INDICATOR_VERIFICATION`

### Marking guidance

Gold responses should justify choices, sequence steps, identify limitations and evaluate against the stated purpose. Accept other valid designs and safe methods. Award credit for sound reasoning rather than matching the sample wording.

| Item | Expected evidence | Marks |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Four suitable tools/materials and accurate purposes: card as label material; ruler to measure/mark; pencil to mark; scissors to cut; glue stick to attach | 4 |
| 2 | Distinguishes measuring available box space from measuring/marking card to match that space | 2 |
| 3 | Four logical steps with at least one relevant safety control placed appropriately | 4 |
| 4 | Explains that gluing before measuring can cause poor fit/waste; gives better order: measure, mark, check, cut, test, then attach | 3 |
| 5 | Recognises that straightness is met but readability criterion is not; identifies need to enlarge/reposition lettering | 3 |
| 6 | Practical improvement and a relevant test, e.g. larger clear lettering; view from normal reading distance / ask a classmate to read | 2 |
| 7 | Design includes shape, word placement and a fit check | 2 |

**Total: 20 marks**

### Acceptable alternatives
- Q2: “Measure the space on the box first, then make the card label the same suitable size.”
- Q4: “It may stick in the wrong place or be too large/small.”
- Q6: Larger letters, stronger contrast using safe materials, or a clearer layout; test by checking readability and fit.
- Q7: Any safe design that includes the requested features.

### Quality judgement
A strong response links the design to its purpose, explains why each tool is selected, includes safety during the process and uses a test to decide whether the finished label works.

### Evidence record
Record reasoning, planning, safety and evaluation separately. Use the evidence alongside other classwork; do not assign a permanent pathway from this one task.

**Next step:** Ask the learner to transfer the same design cycle to a different safe classroom object and explain what must change.
