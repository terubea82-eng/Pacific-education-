# TEACHER ONLY — Class 1 English Week 15 Marking Guide (Bronze)
**Days:** 71–75 | **Focus:** Comprehension — literal questions
**Pathway:** Bronze | Keep this guide in the teacher-only area.

## Matching passage
Lani has a small black cat. The cat sits on a mat near the door. Lani gives the cat a bowl of water.

## Learning intention
Learner answers who, what, where and when questions using details explicitly stated in the passage.

## Expected answers
1. Lani. 2. A cat. 3. On a mat near the door. 4. A bowl of water.

## Marking principles
- Credit accurate equivalent wording, a clear point/selection, sign, AAC, drawing or dictated answer where it demonstrates comprehension.
- Do not require the exact sample wording. Accept “by the door” for “beside the door” where the passage supports it.
- Do not penalise accent, speech difference, handwriting or spelling unless explicitly assessed.
- A literal answer must be supported by information actually stated; do not credit an unsupported guess as text evidence.
- Record whether the learner found the answer independently, with a cue, with a model or with adult assistance.

## Pathway expectations
Use the word bank and visual pointer as needed. Look for correct responses to the four questions and an attempt to connect an answer to the passage.

## Five-day assessment notes
- **Day 71:** Record whether learner identifies a detail after listening/reading.
- **Day 72:** Record use of the worked example to locate an explicit answer.
- **Day 73:** Note accuracy and prompts during shared practice.
- **Day 74:** Record independent transfer to the pathway questions.
- **Day 75:** Select evidence and record a next step.
