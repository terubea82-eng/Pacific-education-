## Learning focus
I can put words in a sensible order and build complete sentences that communicate a clear idea.

## Read the passage
### At the Beach
Sera walks to the beach. She sees a shell. The shell is pink. A wave comes. Sera picks up the shell. She puts it in her bag.

## Word bank
who • what happens • where • when • because • and • then • first • after

## Worked example
Mixed words: **the / boy / runs**

Complete sentence: **The boy runs.**

A complete sentence shares a whole idea. It usually tells who or what the sentence is about and what happens. Start with a capital letter and finish with suitable punctuation.
