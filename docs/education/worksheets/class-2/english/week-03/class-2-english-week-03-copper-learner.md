## What I am learning
I am learning to notice, sort, read and build words using sound and spelling patterns that my class has already been taught. I will not be expected to use a new or untaught pattern.

## How to take part
Work with an adult. Say, sign, point to, match, select, use AAC, draw or write. Use only sound and spelling patterns already taught in class. The adult may read the passage aloud.

## Read or listen to this complete passage

Mia has a red bag. She puts a pen in the bag. The bag is on a mat. Mia gets the bag and sits with her class.

**Word bank:** bag · red · pen · mat · sits

## Worked example
Look at the word “bag”. Say the word slowly, listen for its sounds, then match it to the same word. Trace or build the word if helpful.
