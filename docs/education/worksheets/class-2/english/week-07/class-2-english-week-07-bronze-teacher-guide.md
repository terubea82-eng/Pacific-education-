# Teacher-only guide — Class 2 English Week 7 (Bronze)

**Days 31–35 | Fluency with familiar texts**

**Matching learner file:** class-2-english-week-07-bronze-learner.md

## Learning intention
Build familiarity, accuracy, meaningful phrasing, and confidence through repeated reading or supported access to a short text. Fluency is not speed alone; understanding and comfortable pacing matter.

## Access and teaching support
Preview the title and key words. Read together in short phrase groups. Model punctuation pauses and natural phrasing. Allow repeated readings and accessible response modes.

Use the complete passage printed in the matching learner worksheet. Do not require oral reading where this is not an accessible or appropriate response mode. For learners using AAC, sign, listening, or supported reading, assess the intended comprehension and engagement without treating speech as the only evidence.

**Indicative evidence:** It sits/sings/looks at the garden; at the end it flies away.

## Indicative answer guide
1. **Read or listen to the passage. Who or what is it about?**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: A little bird.
2. **Find a word that tells what the bird does. Say or show the word.**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: Sits, sings, looks, spreads, or flies.
3. **Choose one sentence. Read it slowly once and smoothly once.**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: Any sentence read or repeated with a supported attempt at smooth phrasing.
4. **What happens after the bird looks down at the garden?**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: It spreads its wings and flies away.
5. **Retell the passage in two parts: what the bird does first and what it does at the end.**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: It sits/sings/looks at the garden; at the end it flies away.

Accept valid equivalent wording and accessible communication modes. Give feedback on one manageable next step (for example, noticing a full stop, rereading a phrase, or recognising an action word). Avoid ranking learners by reading speed.
