# PacEdu English — Corrected Level-by-Level Concept Register (ECE–Form 7)

**Purpose:** This register replaces the broad English concept bands in the general subject-distribution draft with a level-by-level working progression. It is intended to sit inside the **English** subject box: English → Level → Term → Week → Concept → Daily activities → Worksheets → Revision/assessment.

**Planning frame:** PacEdu uses Term 1 = 9 weeks, Term 2 = 9 weeks and Term 3 = 5 weeks (23 planning weeks). This is a PacEdu planning frame, not a claim about the Ministry's official school calendar or weekly syllabus sequence.

**Curriculum integrity:** The concepts below are teaching-planning summaries, not copied Ministry syllabus text. A concept is not to be labelled an official achievement indicator unless the exact current Ministry source, edition, level, strand/outcome, page and reviewer have been recorded. Where the source status is partial or pending, the teacher must check the school's currently authorised syllabus and scheme of work before assigning formal curriculum alignment. Do not invent missing outcomes.

## Common teaching and assessment rules

- Teach listening/speaking, reading/viewing and writing/shaping in an integrated way where suitable; do not reduce English to grammar drills.
- Each week has one central concept and five connected daily lessons: introduce/model; guided practice; apply; communicate/create; review and check understanding.
- Copper (Foundation), Bronze (Supported), Silver (Developing) and Gold (Extension) are flexible task-support pathways, not fixed labels for a child's overall ability. Keep the intended learning goal consistent while varying scaffolds, text complexity and independence.
- Accept accessible evidence: spoken response, sign-supported response, tactile/large-print/Braille material, assistive technology, pointing/choice, dictated writing or a scribe where writing mechanics are not the assessed skill.
- For every weekly concept, create four matched learner worksheets and a separate teacher guide/answer key. Do not make four unrelated worksheets.
- Use original PacEdu practice questions. Never label generated practice as an official past examination paper.

## ECE — Early Childhood Education

**Curriculum reference identified:** *Na Noda Mataniciva: Kindergarten Curriculum Guidelines for the Fiji Islands* (2009), Language, Literacy and Communication. The identified structure has two strands — Listening and communicating; Reading and writing — and four sub-strands: Listening and responding; Speaking and communicating; Becoming a reader; Becoming a writer.

**Correct concept progression**
- Listening and responding: attend to a speaker; respond to name and familiar language; listen to songs, rhymes and stories; follow simple familiar instructions; take turns.
- Speaking and communicating: greetings; names and familiar vocabulary; express needs and ideas; describe familiar people/objects/events; participate in conversation, songs and retelling.
- Becoming a reader: enjoy books; handle books; understand pictures carry meaning; connect pictures, spoken words and familiar print; notice rhyme, rhythm and environmental sounds.
- Becoming a writer: drawing and mark-making; purposeful marks; picture-and-talk; experiment with shapes and letter-like forms; attempt own name and communicate meaning through drawings/marks.

**ECE boundary:** Keep learning play-based, oral-language rich and developmentally appropriate. Do not turn ECE into a formal primary reading or grammar syllabus. Support the child's first language while developing English.

**PacEdu 23-week planning sequence**
- Term 1 (Weeks 1–9): familiar people and self; greetings; listening and turn-taking; environmental sounds; familiar instructions; naming objects; describing people/objects; songs and rhymes; shared stories and retelling.
- Term 2 (Weeks 1–9): book handling; print and picture awareness; story characters and events; rhyme; syllable/clap patterns; initial sound awareness; familiar letters in meaningful contexts; familiar words and labels; sequencing a story.
- Term 3 (Weeks 1–5): drawing to communicate; purposeful mark-making; letter-like forms; own-name awareness; create and share a picture/story.

**Status:** Partially verified against the 2009 guideline structure. A revised ECE guideline/update was reported in 2025; obtain and check the latest authorised edition before marking this register fully current.

## Class 1 — Year 1

**Curriculum reference identified:** Fiji Year 1 English / Language, Literacy and Communication learning materials. The mapped skill areas include listening/communication, reading/viewing and writing/composing. Exact current syllabus edition and page-level outcome mapping still require confirmation.

**Correct concept progression**
- Listening: follow simple oral directions; listen for key information; take turns; respond to questions; speak clearly about familiar topics.
- Speaking: greetings and introductions; conversations; role-play; describe familiar experiences; retell a short event.
- Phonological awareness within English: rhyme; syllables; onset and rime; hear and identify taught sounds; blend and segment simple spoken words.
- Reading: letter names and taught letter-sound links; familiar/high-frequency words; decode taught patterns; read familiar words, phrases and simple sentences; understand a short text.
- Writing: draw and label; write familiar words; compose a simple sentence or short text; use familiar vocabulary; begin sentence boundaries and basic punctuation in context.

**PacEdu 23-week planning sequence**
- Term 1: listening routines and one-step instructions; conversation and turn-taking; alphabet awareness; taught letter-sound links; rhyme; onset/rime; oral blending; familiar/high-frequency words; consolidation.
- Term 2: decode taught words; read phrases; read simple sentences; picture/text meaning; answer literal questions; sequence events; describe a person/place; write a message; sentence boundaries; consolidation.
- Term 3: compose simple sentences; write to entertain; write to inform; reread and improve meaning; revision and practice assessment.

**Status:** Partially verified. Confirm current Year 1 syllabus and exact achievement indicators with the school's authorised copy.
