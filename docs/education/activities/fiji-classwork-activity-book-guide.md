# PacEdu Fiji Classwork Activity Book — curriculum correction and release rules

**Status:** Implementation guide and original practice-content specification. Not a Ministry-issued syllabus, approved scheme of work, or claim of complete curriculum alignment.

## 1. Correct curriculum hierarchy

PacEdu's 15 subject boxes are the requested application navigation structure. They are **not** a claim that Fiji's National Curriculum Framework (NCF) has 15 official Key Learning Areas.

The Fiji Islands National Curriculum Framework identifies:
- Six Foundation Areas of Learning and Development (FALD) for early childhood.
- Seven Key Learning Areas (KLAs) for primary and secondary schooling.

The 2013 Fiji National Curriculum Framework is under review. Cabinet endorsed the review on 7 October 2025 and established a taskforce for initial planning. Until an authorised replacement and its effective date/adoption are confirmed, store the 2013 NCF as the historical/currently identified framework baseline with a visible review notice. Do not import a draft replacement as approved curriculum.

Official/context references:
- Fiji Islands National Curriculum Framework (Ministry of Education, 2007; revised 2013): https://neqmap.bangkok.unesco.org/wp-content/uploads/2019/08/The-Fiji-Islands-National-Curriculum-Framework.pdf
- Fiji Ministry, *Na Noda Mataniciva*: https://www.education.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Na-Noda-Mataniciva.pdf
- Cabinet decision, 7 October 2025, review of the 2013 NCF: https://www.fiji.gov.fj/14th-cabinet-meeting-decisions-7-october-2025/
- Fiji Ministry, Years 3–4 Literacy Guide: https://www.education.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2022/06/Literacy_Guide_-for_years_34.pdf
- Fiji Ministry, Teacher's Guide to Disability-Inclusive Education: https://www.education.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2024/09/SIE-guide-high-res-web-version-1.pdf

**Source correction:** A PDF previously listed in a draft inventory as a Fiji *Early Childhood Education Curriculum Framework (November 2024)* is actually a Bahrain framework (its pages identify the Kingdom of Bahrain and Bahrain's education system). It must not be used as a Fiji curriculum source. Fiji ECE mapping must use Fiji-authorised ECE materials and confirm any current revision/adoption with the Ministry.

## 2. ECE and primary/secondary curriculum

- Keep the user-facing level label **ECE**.
- ECE activities must be play-based, child-centred, observation-led and developmentally appropriate. Use the six Fiji FALD areas from Fiji-authorised guidance; do not turn ECE into a stack of long written worksheets.
- Years 1–2 need careful mapping to the relevant Fiji ECE/early-primary guidance and current adopted literacy/numeracy materials.
- From Year 3 onward, map each subject to the applicable NCF KLA and current subject syllabus.
- The app supports user-facing labels ECE, Class 1–8 and Form 3–7. The school's official year/form equivalence must be confirmed before mapping. Fiji public education references also use Year 1–8 and Year 9–13; do not silently assume that every school's "Class" label equals a specific "Year" or "Form".

## 3. Science naming correction for PacEdu

Keep one visible PacEdu **Science** subject box, but display the course label according to the level and the school's authorised syllabus:
- Class 1–6: **Elementary Science** (user-required naming; confirm exact school syllabus title and current adoption).
- Class 7–Form 4: **Basic Science** (user-required naming; confirm exact school syllabus title and current adoption).
- Forms 5–7: show only the senior science course(s) actually selected and offered by the school, such as Biology or Chemistry where prescribed. Never show generic Science as a substitute for a selected senior course.

Do not confuse Science with Social Science. Do not merge Agriculture or Home Economics into Science. Every course label and outcome remains pending until its current official syllabus is recorded.

Internal content-bank aliases must never change the learner-facing box name or imply official equivalence:
- Social Studies practice bank may be used only as a draft source within Social Science after level/syllabus review.
- Healthy Living and Physical Education are distinct content strands; do not silently treat them as one official subject. They may be displayed as separate strands within the requested Health box only where the school's programme supports this.
- Arts and Crafts and Music remain separately identified strands within the requested Arts box where applicable.

- Vosa Vakaviti, Fiji Hindi, Urdu and Rotuman require their own language-specific materials, scripts/orthography, dialect decisions and fluent educator review. Never substitute generic Hindi for Fiji Hindi or fabricate translations.

## 5. Classwork Activity Book structure

Each subject box contains one ordered classwork sequence for the selected learner level:
- Term 1: planning frame of 9 weeks.
- Term 2: planning frame of 9 weeks.
- Term 3: planning frame of 5 weeks.
- Five daily learning slots per week (115 planned learning-day slots per year).

These are PacEdu planning slots only. Apply the school's approved calendar, holidays, timetable, subject periods and assessment schedule. Never invent dates or claim the 9+9+5 distribution is the Ministry's official calendar.

Every daily classwork page should contain:
1. Class, subject, term, week and day.
2. Official curriculum source/version and exact outcome/achievement-indicator reference, or a clear **PENDING_TEACHER_REVIEW** notice.
3. One shared learning objective and one clearly named concept.
4. Brief teacher explanation and a worked example/demonstration.
5. Guided practice.
6. Independent classwork task and answer space.
7. A short understanding check and feedback/retry opportunity.
8. Accessibility alternatives: large print, screen-reader-friendly text, tactile/concrete objects, oral/sign/point/draw or practical response as appropriate.
9. Teacher evidence and next-step record.

## 6. Four flexible ability pathways

All four pathways use the **same learning objective and core concept**. Only the scaffolding, amount of prompting and depth of challenge change.

- Copper: modelled first step, concrete objects/pictures, short instructions, repeated practice and accessible response choice.
- Bronze: guided example, word bank or limited prompts, followed by an independent attempt.
- Silver: independent class-level task, working/evidence and self-check.
- Gold: extension, transfer to a new example, comparison and justification.

These are PacEdu teaching groups, not official Fiji ability labels and not permanent labels for a child. Placement must use teacher-reviewed evidence and remain adjustable. Do not invent percentage cut-offs.

## 7. Separate downloadable files

For each selected subject/class/term/week:
- Student daily activity: separate Copper, Bronze, Silver and Gold files.
- Weekly Classwork Activity Book: separate five-day book for each ability pathway.
- Teacher Lesson Plan: separate teacher file.
- Teacher Lesson Notes: separate teacher file.
- Teacher Answer Guide & Marking Criteria: separate teacher file.
- Teacher Teaching Aids: separate teacher file.

Never put answer keys in student downloads. Do not put a learner's full name, student ID or FEMIS number into a public/static class pack filename or URL. Personalised copies must be access-controlled.

## 8. Release and verification gate

A lesson may be labelled **Fiji curriculum-aligned** only when its record contains:
- issuing authority and official source URL/document;
- exact syllabus/framework title, version and effective/adoption status;
- exact level and subject/course;
- strand/unit and exact outcome/achievement-indicator ID and wording;
- source page/section;
- term/week placement evidence;
- reviewer name/role and review date;
- teacher approval for student release.

Until all required fields are checked, use **PacEdu teacher-review practice — curriculum mapping pending** and keep student release behind teacher approval. Do not describe a generated book as a verified Ministry workbook.

## 9. Activity content and quality rules

- Use Fiji-relevant, safe, low-cost scenarios and locally available materials.
- Do not require children to disclose family income, private information, or upload personal photos.
- Every multiple-choice question must have exactly A, B, C and D.
- Reading comprehension questions must be answerable from the supplied passage.
- Do not reuse the same generic worksheet as a substitute for a missing subject's curriculum.
- Subject content must match the selected class and syllabus; senior courses must be selected individually.
- ECE must remain play-based and observation-led.
- Keep all classwork, revision, checkpoint assessments and practice exam preparation inside the relevant subject box.
- Practice assessments must be labelled as PacEdu practice, not official Fiji examination papers.

## 10. Current implementation boundary

The five-day downloadable classwork-book control uses existing PacEdu daily worksheet content and produces a separate HTML file for each ability group. It is an implementation of the download structure, not proof that every subject has complete, unique, officially mapped five-day content. Missing or unavailable subject banks must fail closed; never substitute another subject. The live app must still be refreshed and visually tested on a phone, and teachers must review the actual downloaded pages before classroom use.
