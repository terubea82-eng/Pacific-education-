# Fiji Government Current-Affairs Register for PacEdu Daily Activities

**Register status:** Initial verified source batch — 12 November 2026.  
**Purpose:** Preserve relevant Fiji Government updates as dated, source-linked teaching context and connect them to the daily curriculum concept/indicator only when the connection is genuine.  
**Important:** This is not a curriculum syllabus or achievement-indicator bank. A Government update never becomes an official curriculum indicator merely because it is useful in a lesson.

## Mandatory use rule

- Search official Fiji Government, Ministry, Parliament and authorised public-agency sources for new and changed updates during daily-activity preparation.
- Verify the underlying source page/document, not only a search snippet or media summary.
- Record every update in the register, including those with no immediate subject match. A no-match item remains available for later current-affairs learning; do not force it into a daily lesson.
- Link a daily activity to the exact verified indicator/outcome being taught. The update is only a real-world example, data source or context.
- Where no current official indicator is verified, label the lesson **DRAFT — CURRICULUM MAPPING PENDING**. Do not invent a code or wording.
- State the legal/administrative status precisely: proposal, Cabinet endorsement, Cabinet approval, Bill passed, enacted law, strategy, framework, operational programme, or completed action. Re-check status before reuse.
- Use factual, politically neutral, age-appropriate explanations. For public-policy topics, teach evidence, institutions, rights, processes and source evaluation—not advocacy for or against an actor or policy.
- Sensitive subjects (child protection, violence, health, drugs, disasters, hazardous materials) require age/safeguarding review. Never ask learners to handle hazardous materials or disclose personal experiences.
- Use original PacEdu questions and activities. Link to official sources; obtain permission before reproducing protected content at scale.

### Update A — Rights of Indigenous Peoples Bill 2025

- **Issuer:** Fiji Government; statement concerning parliamentary proceedings.
- **Exact title:** *Parliament Approves Bill to Strengthen Indigenous Rights*.
- **Publication date:** 3 November 2026.
- **Official URL:** https://www.fiji.gov.fj/parliament-approves-bill-to-strengthen-indigenous-rights/
- **Verified content:** The Government report states that Parliament approved the Bill and describes provisions concerning recognition and protection of indigenous identity, cultural heritage, traditional knowledge and customary practices, and a National Action Plan with consultation and reporting.
- **Status:** Bill approved by Parliament as reported in the Government release. Do not assume commencement, regulations or completed implementation without checking the enacted Act and commencement notice.
- **Possible connection:** Social Science and English (Parliament, law-making, rights, consultation, source-based civic literacy); Arts and language subjects (cultural heritage) only with appropriate community/fluent-educator review.
- **Classroom safeguard:** Neutral explanation of legislative process and rights; no political persuasion or unsupported claim about effects.

### Update B — PAFCO modernisation guarantee

- **Issuer:** Fiji Government; statement about a parliamentary motion.
- **Exact title:** *Government Seeks $20 Million Guarantee to Support PAFCO’s Modernisation*.
- **Publication date:** 3 November 2026.
- **Official URL:** https://www.fiji.gov.fj/government-seeks-20-million-guarantee-to-support-pafcos-modernisation/
- **Verified content:** The Government reported that it was seeking Parliament's approval for an additional $20 million guarantee to support modernisation of Pacific Fishing Company Limited's processing facilities and equipment, food-safety compliance and market development.
- **Status:** Proposal seeking Parliament's approval at the time of publication; do not describe it as approved or paid unless a later official record confirms this.
- **Possible connection:** Mathematics (money, percentages, interpreting figures); Social Science (employment, economic activity and public finance); Agriculture/food systems and Technology (food processing, equipment and safety); English (summarising a proposal and identifying conditional language).
- **Classroom safeguard:** Clearly distinguish proposed guarantee from completed expenditure and separate stated facts from forecasts or claims.
