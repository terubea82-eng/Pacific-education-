**Learning intention:** I notice sounds around me, hear words that rhyme, and create or identify a simple rhyming pair.

**Ways to answer:** Speak, sign, point, use AAC, select picture/word cards, clap, draw, dictate or write. The teacher reads and describes every task. Learners may use familiar local-language examples orally as a bridge, while the assessed English examples remain clear and familiar. Do not penalise accent or speech differences.

**Access and safety:** Offer large-print/high-contrast or tactile picture cards, quiet listening space, captions/visual sound cues and extra wait time. Do not use sudden or painfully loud sounds. A learner who cannot hear an environmental sound can work with a visual/tactile representation or teacher-described sound; record the response mode.

**Word/picture set for this practice:** cat–hat; sun–fun; bell–shell; clap–tap. These are practice examples, not a claim about official textbook sequence. Teacher checks vocabulary is familiar before use.

**Ability pathway:** Copper — concrete sound/picture matching and adult-guided practice

## Day 16 — Listen and notice
**Teacher model:** Make a gentle clap or tap a desk softly. Show the matching picture/action card.

**Worked example:** Teacher taps softly, shows the TAP card and says “I heard a tap.”

1. Listen to a gentle clap or look at its picture. Point to or choose: CLAP / TAP.
2. Look at the pictures: BELL / CAT. Which one shows something that can make a sound? __________
3. Show or draw one sound you know.

## Day 17 — Explore rhyming words
**Teacher model:** Say “cat, hat.” Stretch the final sound gently: cat—hat. The words sound alike at the end.

**Worked example:** CAT matches HAT because both end with the same “-at” sound.

1. Match CAT with HAT or SUN. My choice: __________
2. Match SUN with FUN or BELL. My choice: __________
3. Say, sign, point to or select the pair that rhymes: BELL / SHELL.
What did you choose? _________________________________________

## Day 18 — Practise together

2. BELL matches: SHELL / CAT. Choose one: __________
3. Teacher says “clap, tap.” Point to the two matching word/picture cards.
4. Draw or place two cards that rhyme:

## Day 19 — Try a new choice
**Teacher model:** Give two choices and enough time to respond.

1. Which rhymes with SUN? FUN / CAT. My choice: __________
2. Which rhymes with HAT? CAT / BELL. My choice: __________
3. Choose one gentle sound you can make safely (clap or tap). Show it or choose its picture.
