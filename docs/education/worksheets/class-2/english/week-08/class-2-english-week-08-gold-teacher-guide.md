# Teacher-only guide — Class 2 English Week 8 (Gold)

**Days 36–40 | Literal comprehension**

**Matching learner file:** class-2-english-week-08-gold-learner.md

## Learning intention
Locate and communicate information explicitly stated in a short text; use the text as evidence rather than guessing.

## Access and teaching support
Ask learners to answer by locating exact details in the text. Model scanning for names, places, actions, and time words. For inference-like questions, keep the focus on explicit information stated in this passage. Accept oral, signed, AAC, pointing, or written evidence.

## Daily teaching and evidence
### Day 36 — Find a stated detail
**Teaching move:** Read the passage and model finding one clearly stated fact.

**Indicative evidence:** On a bench outside the classroom before joining a game at break time.

## Indicative answer guide
1. **Where and when did Lani leave the hat?**
   - Answer: On a bench outside the classroom before joining a game at break time.
2. **What made the bench empty when Lani returned?**
   - Answer: The hat had slipped from the bench and was placed beside the friend's bag.
3. **What two steps did Lani take to search?**
   - Answer: She looked underneath the bench and around the nearby bags.
4. **Why did the friend move the hat?**
   - Answer: To keep it safely out of the way after it slipped from the bench.
5. **Which detail shows Lani did not simply guess where the hat was?**
   - Answer: She searched carefully, checking underneath the bench and around the nearby bags.

Accept accurate equivalent wording and accessible communication modes. For literal comprehension, answers should be supported by information directly stated in the passage. Do not penalise spelling or handwriting where these are not the target skill.
