**Status:** Original teacher-review practice material. This is a proposed continuation, not a verified official Fiji syllabus sequence or timetable. Confirm the current authorized syllabus and exact outcome/indicator before marking as curriculum-verified.

**Use:** Teacher selects the starting task from observed evidence. Learners may answer by speaking, pointing, drawing, using communication supports, or writing. Use the four bands as flexible teaching groups, not permanent labels.

## Teacher marking notes for Days 6–10

These are quick checking notes, not a secret answer key:
- **Day 6:** Accept identification of real classroom objects; the reading/writing tools are pen or pencil depending on the objects shown.
- **Day 7:** /s/ words: sun, sock. Short /a/ words in the teacher’s model: apple, ant. Model pronunciation and accept local accent differences while checking the target skill.
- **Day 8:** Mere; school; “Mere has a bag” is first in the story. Sentence order: “I go to school.”
- **Day 9:** First: packs a school bag; next: walks to school; last: greets the teacher.
- **Day 10:** Check letter recognition and story understanding from the actual responses; do not assign a final ability label from this review alone.
