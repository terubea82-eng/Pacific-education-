# Teacher-only marking guide — Class 1 English Week 11, Gold

**Days 51–55 | Vocabulary: objects and actions**

**Teacher use only; do not distribute with the learner worksheet.** Use familiar, taught words and safe objects/pictures. This is not an official vocabulary list.

## Learning intention
Use familiar object names (nouns) and action words (verbs) to describe things and actions.

## Daily evidence and scoring
### Day 51 — Listen and notice

**Evidence:** one accurate object-name match and action identification.

**Expected response:** Learner correctly identifies/matches familiar object/action words and communicates the relevant phrase or caption specified in the daily task. Accept pointing, eye gaze, matching, speech, sign, AAC, dictation, drawing or writing.

**Scoring (0–2):** 2 = accurate evidence with planned access supports and no additional teaching prompt; 1 = accurate after verbal cue/model; 0 = not demonstrated / no valid opportunity. Record prompt level separately; do not penalize agreed access or communication mode.

## Marking principles
- Accept an accurate familiar local word or equivalent expression for the pictured object/action.
- Score the objective, not handwriting, speech, speed or spelling unless those were explicitly taught and assessed.
- If a word or picture was unfamiliar, replace it with a familiar taught example and record **not assessed**.
- Use evidence to plan next instruction; do not assign a fixed ability label from one week's result.
