## Guardrails for PacEdu

1. Keep every existing daily activity and worksheet marked `PENDING_OFFICIAL_INDICATOR_VERIFICATION` until exact official outcomes/indicators are extracted from the authorised edition.
2. Do not invent indicator IDs, wording, term allocations, prescribed texts or assessment criteria.
3. Do not use the Fiji National Curriculum Framework as a substitute for subject-specific class-level English indicators.
4. Do not apply Forms 3–4 guidance to Forms 5–7. Confirm each senior form's syllabus and examination requirements independently.
5. Record source title, issuing authority, edition/year, page number, exact outcome/indicator wording and verification date before marking any mapping SOURCE_VERIFIED.
6. Preserve the distinction between USER_REVIEWED (the user's review of PacEdu drafts) and SOURCE_VERIFIED (independent confirmation against an authoritative source).
