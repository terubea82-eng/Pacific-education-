## Worksheet 2 — Day 2: Who am I?

**Name:** __________________________ **Date:** ______________

### A. Complete your introduction
I am __________________________________.

I am ______ years old.

I like __________________________________.

### B. Read or listen
**My name is Litia. I am six years old. I come to school with my bag. I like drawing.**

1. What is the child’s name? ______________________________
2. How old is Litia? ______________________________________
3. What does Litia like? __________________________________
4. Draw or tell one thing you like to do.

[Drawing space]

____________________________________________________________

### C. Choose your level

**Foundation / Supported**
- Point to the name **Litia** in the reading.
- Choose: Litia likes **drawing / swimming**.
- Tell, point or draw one thing about yourself.

**Developing**
- Complete: “Her name is ______.”
- Complete: “Litia likes ______.”
- Say or dictate one sentence about yourself.

**Expected**
- Write two sentences about yourself.
- Underline the capital letter at the start of each sentence.
- Put a full stop at the end.

**Advanced**
- Write three connected sentences introducing yourself.
- Add one detail about something you enjoy and explain why.

## Worksheet 3 — Day 3: Letter Ss

**Name:** __________________________ **Date:** ______________

### A. Find the letter
Circle every **S** and **s**.

S   a   m   s   T   S   A   s   p   S

### B. Trace and write
S   S   S   ______________________________

s   s   s   ______________________________

### C. Listen for the first sound
Teacher reads each word aloud. Circle the words that begin with the /s/ sound:

**sun   bag   school   fish   sock   apple**

### D. Use a word
Choose **sun**, **school**, or **sock**. Draw it or use it in a sentence.

My word: ______________________

My sentence or spoken answer: __________________________________

### E. Choose your level
- **Foundation:** Match S to s; point to one /s/ word when the teacher says it.
- **Developing:** Copy S/s and identify two /s/ words from the list.
- **Expected:** Write one sentence using a selected /s/ word.
- **Advanced:** Think of another /s/ word and use it in a meaningful sentence.

## Worksheet 4 — Day 4: Letter Aa

**Name:** __________________________ **Date:** ______________

### A. Find the letter
Circle every **A** and **a**.

a   S   A   m   a   T   A   s   a   p

### B. Trace and write
A   A   A   ______________________________

a   a   a   ______________________________

### C. Listen and look
Teacher says each word. Circle the words that begin with the short /a/ sound:

**apple   ant   sun   bag   animal**

*Teacher note: Pronunciation may vary by accent. Model the target sound clearly and accept the school’s agreed pronunciation.*

### D. Draw or write
Draw an **apple** or an **ant**. Label your picture with the word.

[Drawing space]

Word: __________________________

### E. Choose your level
- **Foundation:** Find A/a among other letters.
- **Developing:** Copy A/a and point to an /a/ word.
- **Expected:** Write one word beginning with the target /a/ sound.
- **Advanced:** Write a sentence with one /a/ word and read it aloud or dictate it.

## Teacher implementation notes

1. These are **newly written practice worksheets** informed by the source section listed above; they do not reproduce the source pages.
2. Day 1 is a proposed diagnostic, not an official Ministry test. Days 2–5 are a suggested practice sequence based on the Unit 1 topic and S/s and A/a letter activities; do not claim the Ministry prescribes these exact daily slots.
3. Use Foundation, Developing, Expected and Advanced as temporary instructional groupings. The teacher decides placement from evidence and may change it.
4. Provide accessible response options. Do not mark a learner wrong solely for handwriting, spelling, speech difference or disability when the target skill is comprehension or communication.
5. Keep any teacher answer key and assessment rubric in a teacher-authorized workspace; never place secret keys in public student JavaScript or static HTML.
6. Before applying the “curriculum-verified” label, record the current authorized source version, exact official outcome/indicator wording and ID, page/section, subject reviewer, date reviewed and approved schedule evidence.
7. Do not reuse this mapping for ECE, another class, or another subject without separate source verification.
