## Read or listen to this complete practice text
Mere has a red bag. A book is in the bag. Mere goes to school.

**Important:** This is a self-contained review practice text. The teacher may also use a previously taught, approved decodable text from the class sequence. Do not assess untaught letter–sound patterns as mastered.

## Worked example
Teacher models: “Mere has a red bag.”  
1. Find the person: Mere.  
2. Find what she has: a red bag.  
3. Show the words or picture that helped.  
Now try one together: Who has the bag? **Mere.**

## Your learning support
Adult models each step; use large-print or tactile text, picture/word cards, a small choice set, repeated short directions and generous wait time. Accept pointing, matching, speech, sign, AAC, drawing or dictation.
