# Teacher-only marking guide — Class 1 English Week 10, Copper

**Days 46–50 | Vocabulary: people and places**

**Teacher use only; do not distribute with the learner worksheet.** Choose familiar, taught vocabulary and respectful local picture examples. This guide does not supply an official word list or indicator code.

## Learning intention
Understand and use familiar words for people, places and community settings; answer who/where questions.

## Daily evidence and scoring
### Day 46 — Listen and notice

**Evidence:** one correct people/place identification and match.

**Expected response:** Learner identifies/sorts the picture and gives a relevant label, clue or who/where response, as specified by the daily task. Accept pointing, eye gaze, matching, speech, sign, AAC, dictation, drawing or writing.

**Scoring (0–2):** 2 = accurate evidence with planned access supports and no additional teaching prompt; 1 = accurate after verbal cue/model; 0 = not demonstrated / no valid opportunity. Record prompt level separately. Do not penalize the agreed communication or access mode.

## Marking principles
- Accept locally appropriate familiar vocabulary when it accurately identifies the picture or context.
- Score the learning objective, not handwriting, speech, speed or spelling unless specifically taught and assessed.
- If a picture or word is unfamiliar, replace it with a familiar taught example; record **not assessed**, not incorrect.
- Use evidence to adjust instruction; do not use one week's score to fix a learner's pathway.
