# PacEdu Term Schedule and Revision Framework

Draft planning framework for Fiji pilot.

- Term 1: 9 teaching weeks, 5 daily activities per week.
- Term 2: 9 teaching weeks, 5 daily activities per week.
- Term 3: 5 teaching weeks, 5 daily activities per week.
- Revision follows the daily activities for each term.
- Revision papers are practice resources, not official examination papers.
- Achievement indicators remain unverified until mapped to a current authorised Fiji syllabus and reviewed by a qualified teacher.
