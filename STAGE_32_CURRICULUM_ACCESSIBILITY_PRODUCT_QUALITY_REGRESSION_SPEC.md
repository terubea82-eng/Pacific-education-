# Stage 32 — Curriculum, Accessibility and Product Quality Regression

**Status:** PREPARED — implementation and verification required.

## Purpose
Maintain learning quality after release through controlled regression testing.

## Controls
- versioned official Fiji curriculum-source monitoring;
- achievement-indicator mapping regression tests;
- integrated-learning coverage checks;
- assessment/progression regression tests;
- teacher revision and examination coverage checks;
- accessibility regression testing;
- low-bandwidth/offline regression testing;
- supported-device/browser regression matrix;
- lesson-content quality review;
- defect severity, ownership, retest and closure;
- release impact assessment for material changes.

## Evidence
Every material regression cycle records source/version, test scope, results, defects, corrective action, reviewer and decision. Internal mappings must not be represented as official curriculum authority without verification.

## Completion
A regression cycle is complete only when mandatory tests pass or approved exceptions are documented and required review is complete.

## Transition
Material curriculum or product changes route back to the affected earlier stage rather than bypassing controls.

**Boundary:** Testing evidence is not itself a legal or Ministry approval.
