## How to take part
Work with an adult if you need help. The adult may read the passage and directions aloud. You may answer by saying, signing, pointing, matching, using AAC, drawing, dictating or writing. Use letter cards, tiles or tactile materials if helpful. Allow time to think and respond.

## Read or listen to this complete passage

Tavi has a pet cat. The cat sat on a mat. Tavi fed the cat. The cat ran to the bed. Tavi read a short note about the cat.

**Word bank:** cat · mat · sat · fed · ran · bed · note

## Worked example
Compare “cat” and “mat”. Notice the shared ending, then check the first sound. Say or build each word and check it in the passage.
