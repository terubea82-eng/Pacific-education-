## 5. Mapping and activity safeguards

1. Preserve each record's year band. Do not split a Years 1–2 outcome into separate Year 1 and Year 2 official indicators without year-specific Ministry documentation.
2. Treat FJD currency thresholds ($20, $50, $100) as source-era learning context; verify current currency denominations and Ministry relevance before designing present-day tasks.
3. Use safe, age-appropriate fictional classroom examples. Do not require children to disclose family income, debts, remittances, bank details or household financial hardship.
4. Avoid recommending financial products, lenders or investments to children. Teach comparison and risk concepts neutrally; use fictional examples.
5. Keep learning outcomes cross-curricular where appropriate; map Mathematics/English/Social Studies links only after the current subject syllabus is checked.
6. Do not assign these records to the 115-day schedule until CAS confirms current curriculum status, year-specific sequence, contact time and assessment expectations.
7. If used for draft practice, label `PENDING_TEACHER_REVIEW` and `CURRENT_STATUS_UNCONFIRMED`. No formal assessment eligibility.
8. Do not copy the source manual's lesson text, stories, activities or worksheets into PacEdu. Create original material only after permissions and curriculum status are checked.
