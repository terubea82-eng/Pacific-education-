# PacEdu Fiji English curriculum mapping register

**Status:** Working register for original practice material. This is not an approved or complete syllabus map. Do not label any row “officially curriculum-verified” until a qualified Fiji English teacher checks the current authorized Ministry/FEMIS syllabus and records the exact outcome/indicator and page/section.

## Scope and naming

The workspace supports ECE, Classes 1–8 and Forms 3–7. Fiji public education references also use Year 1–8 and Year 9–13; the school must confirm which current class/year label applies in its own timetable and approved syllabus. Keep **ECE** as the app label.

## Daily worksheet quality rules

1. A worksheet is a **practice draft** until its exact current syllabus title/version, strand, outcome/achievement indicator, page/section, source URL, reviewer and review date are recorded.
2. A theme such as “family”, “school”, “environment” or “community” is a locally useful teaching context, not itself an official curriculum indicator.
3. Day 1 diagnostic evidence is separate from daily teaching practice. Use actual learner evidence to select support; do not assign permanent ability labels.
4. Four bands mean scaffolding/challenge choices: Supported start, Developing, Working at current class goal, Extension. Do not use them as fixed judgements of a child’s ability.
5. Auto-mark only objective selected-answer items. Short answers, oral responses, paragraphs, creative writing and essays require teacher review against a rubric.
6. Keep answer keys and teacher guidance in the authorized teacher view; do not expose them in a student worksheet.
7. Use school-approved term dates, assessment scope and examination timetable. The app’s default week counts are planning slots, not an official calendar.
8. Do not copy substantial text from syllabus/guide books into PacEdu. Create original activities and cite the source that informs the learning target.

### Additional secondary English candidate source

- *Revised Secondary English Curriculum (RSEC) Teachers Guide — Years 9 and 10* is available as a third-party-hosted copy. The document identifies the Fiji Ministry Curriculum Advisory Services team and Fiji Program Support Facility as preparers and describes three strands, basic skill-building/reading, student-centred approaches and formative/summative assessment. [Candidate copy](https://fliphtml5.com/gjgbb/naau/RSEC_Teachers_Guide_Year_9_%26_10/).
- Treat this as a candidate reference only until checked against the current authorized FEMIS/Ministry copy and the school’s applicable RSEC rollout/version. It does not certify Form 3–4 week/day placement or replace syllabus outcomes.
- The 2025 secondary English curriculum circular surfaced in research describes differing implementation arrangements by year and school cohort, so the workspace must not assume one secondary sequence applies to every school. [Search result for Circular No. 19-25](https://www.studocu.com/row/document/nakawa-vocational-training-college/english/circular-no-19-25-2025-secondary-english-curriculum/117881613). Confirm the actual current circular and applicable school cohort through authorized channels before using it for mapping.
