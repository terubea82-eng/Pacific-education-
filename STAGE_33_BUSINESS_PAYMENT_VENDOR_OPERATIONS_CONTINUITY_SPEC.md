# Stage 33 — Business, Payment, Vendor and Operational Continuity

**Status:** PREPARED — implementation and verification required.

## Purpose
Keep operational and commercial controls reliable after production launch.

## Controls
- server-authoritative pricing and entitlement;
- payment reconciliation;
- refund and dispute workflows;
- transaction exception handling;
- vendor register and service dependencies;
- vendor access review and termination;
- service-level and availability monitoring;
- support and complaint handling;
- operational continuity and disaster recovery exercises;
- financial/business record controls;
- tax and commercial obligations routed to appropriate professional advice;
- owner-controlled pricing/change approval.

## Evidence
Maintain reconciliation records, operational test results, vendor reviews, continuity exercises, unresolved issues and authorized decisions without exposing payment credentials or secrets.

## Completion
Operational cycles require objective evidence and appropriate review. Payment success reported by a client is never proof of entitlement.

## Transition
Material operational, payment or vendor changes re-enter the applicable controlled stage.

**Boundary:** This specification does not provide tax, legal, accounting or contractual certification.
