## Marking principles
- Award credit for accurate synonyms, equivalent wording and logically supported answers; do not require exact model wording.
- Accept spelling errors that do not obscure meaning; record recurring errors for feedback rather than automatically withholding comprehension credit.
- For open responses, assess relevance, evidence, explanation and clarity. Accept a different defensible interpretation.
- This is a diagnostic guide, not a formal Ministry assessment or a final ability-placement decision.
