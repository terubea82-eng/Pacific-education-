### Read or listen to the passage

**Making a name label**

Our class is making a name label for a book box. We need a piece of paper, a ruler, a pencil, blunt-ended scissors and a glue stick. The ruler helps us make a straight line. The pencil helps us mark where to cut. Scissors cut the paper. The glue stick attaches the label to the box.

We sit while cutting. We cut slowly and away from our bodies. We pass scissors with the handles first. We do not touch a damaged tool. We tell the teacher if a tool is broken.

### Worked example

**Question:** Which tool helps us draw a straight line?  
**Model answer:** A ruler.  
**Why?** The ruler has a straight edge.
