## 1. Source

- Title: *FinED Fiji Learning Outcomes: Years 9 to 12*.
- Publisher/host: National Financial Inclusion Taskforce Fiji (NFIT).
- Date shown on NFIT page: 30 March 2015.
- PDF: https://www.nfitfiji.com/wp-content/uploads/FinED-Fiji-Learning-Outcomes-Secondary.pdf

## 4. Critical legal, safety and currentness filters

1. The document refers to the UK Consumer Credit Act 1974 in its English section. Do not teach that as Fiji law. Replace legal learning with current Fiji legislation and regulator guidance only after legal verification and curriculum approval.
2. The source is from 2015 and itself says the Ministry would continue revising the outcomes. Obtain current CAS confirmation, subject syllabuses and effective dates before use.
3. Banking requirements, account access, TIN rules, financial products, tax rates and payment systems can change. Verify against current Fiji official sources before creating any present-day learning example.
4. Do not expose students to product promotion, lending solicitations or personalised financial advice. Use neutral hypothetical scenarios and age-appropriate safeguarding.
5. Do not ask learners to disclose household income, debt, remittances, bank accounts, family hardship or personal financial records.
6. Preserve source allocation: Year 9 and Year 10 each have a one-term Personal Finance component in the document. Do not assume that this is the current timetable or use it to override current Commercial Studies syllabuses.
7. Years 11–12 subject allocation is shared in the source; do not arbitrarily split the outcomes between the two years.
8. Do not create 115-day placements or formal assessments from these historical candidates until the current Ministry scope, contact hours, sequence and examination requirements are confirmed.
