## Marking principles
- Award credit for accurate synonyms, equivalent wording and logically supported answers; do not require exact model wording.
- Accept spelling errors that do not obscure meaning; record recurring errors for feedback rather than automatically withholding comprehension credit.
- For open responses, assess relevance, evidence, explanation and clarity. Accept a different defensible interpretation.
- This is a diagnostic guide, not a formal Ministry assessment or a final ability-placement decision.

## Suggested answer points
1. The shared tank's water level became low during a dry week, and a tap was dripping.
2. Repairing the tap stopped the leak; the notice encouraged careful use.
3. The water is important to all the families and must be protected collectively.
4. Turn the tap off / take only what is needed; it reduces waste.
5. Repairing the tap is most direct because it stops water escaping; other justified answers accepted.
6. A practical fair rule with explanation and a clear link to shared access.
