# Teacher-only guide — Class 2 English Week 7 (Silver)

**Days 31–35 | Fluency with familiar texts**

**Matching learner file:** class-2-english-week-07-silver-learner.md

## Learning intention
Build familiarity, accuracy, meaningful phrasing, and confidence through repeated reading or supported access to a short text. Fluency is not speed alone; understanding and comfortable pacing matter.

## Access and teaching support
Offer a first model reading, then paired or independent rereading. Encourage phrase-by-phrase reading rather than rushing. Ask the learner to notice commas and full stops. Accept oral, signed, AAC, sequenced, or written responses.

Use the complete passage printed in the matching learner worksheet. Do not require oral reading where this is not an accessible or appropriate response mode. For learners using AAC, sign, listening, or supported reading, assess the intended comprehension and engagement without treating speech as the only evidence.

## Indicative answer guide
1. **Summarise the passage in one sentence.**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: A bird visits outside the classroom, sings, and flies away when the bell rings.
2. **Find two action words that help you picture the bird.**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: Landed, tilted, listened, sang, lifted, or flew.
3. **Read one sentence aloud or silently in phrase groups. Where would you pause?**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: Pauses at commas and full stops; natural phrasing is acceptable.
4. **Why did the class pause?**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: To listen to the bird singing.
5. **Retell the events in order using first, next, and finally.**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: First the bird landed; next it sang and the class listened; finally it flew towards the trees.

Accept valid equivalent wording and accessible communication modes. Give feedback on one manageable next step (for example, noticing a full stop, rereading a phrase, or recognising an action word). Avoid ranking learners by reading speed.
