## Read this original story

### The Last Bus Home

When the rain began, Mere was waiting at the village bus shelter with a box of seedlings for the school garden. The last bus was already late. A younger student, Jone, stood nearby without an umbrella. Mere moved her bag to make room and covered the seedlings with her jacket. They listened as the rain drummed on the shelter roof.

At last, the bus appeared around the bend. The driver slowed when he saw the two students and the flooded patch across the road. He asked them to wait while he checked a safer place to cross. Mere felt impatient, but Jone pointed to the water moving quickly over the road. She understood why the driver had stopped. When they finally boarded, Mere gave Jone the drier seat and held the seedlings carefully on her lap.

## Worked example — read the steps
Question: Where was Mere waiting? Step 1: Locate the setting in the opening sentence. Step 2: Select the exact place. Step 3: Write a complete sentence: Mere was waiting at the village bus shelter.
