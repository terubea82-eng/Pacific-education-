# Teacher-only marking guide — Class 1 English Week 9, Gold

**Days 41–45 | High-frequency words in context**

**Do not give this guide to learners.** Use the same familiar target words and sentence examples that were taught in class. Select only words already introduced in the current approved sequence; do not assume an official high-frequency list.

## Learning intention
Recognise selected common words from current class reading texts and locate them in meaningful sentences.

## Daily evidence and scoring
### Day 41 — Listen and notice

**Evidence:** one correctly found or matched target word.

**Expected response:** Learner identifies or matches the teacher-selected familiar word in the supplied card/sentence context. Accept equivalent accessible response modes (pointing, eye gaze, matching, speech, sign, AAC, dictated response or writing).

**Scoring (0–2):** 2 = accurate identification/match with the planned access supports and no additional teaching prompt; 1 = accurate after a verbal cue or model; 0 = not yet demonstrated / no valid opportunity. Record the prompt level separately; do not penalize disability-related response mode or use of agreed access tools.

## Marking principles
- Score the demonstrated objective, not handwriting, speech, speed or unsupported decoding unless explicitly part of the taught objective.
- Accept a semantically equivalent explanation or correct identification in any agreed communication mode.
- If the target word was not taught, mark **not assessed** and replace it with a taught word; do not mark the learner wrong.
- Use evidence to plan support; do not use one week's score to assign a fixed pathway.
