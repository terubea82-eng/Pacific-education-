# PacEdu English Subject Box — Form 2 Daily Activities (Days 1–115)

**Location:** English Subject Box → Form 2 → Term → Week → Day → Worksheets.
**Programme:** 23 PacEdu planning weeks, 5 learning days per week (115 days). Term 1 = Weeks 1–9; Term 2 = Weeks 10–18; Term 3 = Weeks 19–23. Reconcile dates with the approved school calendar.
**Status:** ORIGINAL PACEDU PRACTICE — PENDING_TEACHER_REVIEW — PENDING_OFFICIAL_INDICATOR_VERIFICATION. Working indicators are PacEdu planning outcomes, not verbatim Ministry indicators.
**Accessibility:** Accept appropriate speech, sign, AAC, pointing, drawing, dictation, Braille, large print or writing. Do not assess accent or disability-related speech differences as errors.
**Pathways:** Copper, Bronze, Silver and Gold share the same daily learning goal. Vary scaffolding and independence; do not assign permanently or infer ability from disability, language or access needs.

## Day 1 — Term 1, Week 1, Day 1

**Weekly concept:** Evaluating community information
**Daily working achievement indicator:** The learner can identify the central idea and select accurate details that support it.
**Teaching sequence:** Activate prior knowledge; explicitly model the skill; guide one example; let learners practise with the selected pathway; gather one observable piece of evidence.
**Worked model:** The central idea is the main message; a supporting detail is a specific fact that helps explain it.
**Core task:** What is the central idea? Select two details that support it. Explain how one detail develops the main idea. Write a one-sentence summary using the strongest evidence.
**Resources:** Passage in the matching learner worksheet; vocabulary support; paper or accessible digital response tools.
**Evidence:** A response that demonstrates the daily goal, with the learner's communication method and prompt level recorded.
**Next step:** Reteach with a shorter text or more explicit model if needed; extend with comparison, justification or transfer when evidence is secure.

## Day 2 — Term 1, Week 1, Day 2

**Weekly concept:** Evaluating community information
**Daily working achievement indicator:** The learner can infer the meaning of key words from context and explain how word choice shapes meaning.
**Teaching sequence:** Activate prior knowledge; explicitly model the skill; guide one example; let learners practise with the selected pathway; gather one observable piece of evidence.
**Worked model:** Use the words around an unfamiliar term, then test whether your meaning fits the whole sentence.
**Core task:** Find one unfamiliar or important word. Use nearby words to infer its meaning. Explain why the writer may have chosen that word. Use the word accurately in a new sentence and justify your choice.
**Resources:** Passage in the matching learner worksheet; vocabulary support; paper or accessible digital response tools.
**Evidence:** A response that demonstrates the daily goal, with the learner's communication method and prompt level recorded.
**Next step:** Reteach with a shorter text or more explicit model if needed; extend with comparison, justification or transfer when evidence is secure.

## Day 3 — Term 1, Week 1, Day 3

**Weekly concept:** Evaluating community information
**Daily working achievement indicator:** The learner can analyse how the text is organised and distinguish stated facts from inference.
**Teaching sequence:** Activate prior knowledge; explicitly model the skill; guide one example; let learners practise with the selected pathway; gather one observable piece of evidence.
**Worked model:** A sequence of events may show cause and effect; an inference must be supported by clues in the text.
**Core task:** Identify the text's structure or sequence. Find a sentence that provides evidence. Explain one viewpoint or implied message. Evaluate what further information would strengthen the text.

## Day 4 — Term 1, Week 1, Day 4

**Weekly concept:** Evaluating community information
**Daily working achievement indicator:** The learner can create a coherent response using details from the text and language suited to an audience and purpose.
**Teaching sequence:** Activate prior knowledge; explicitly model the skill; guide one example; let learners practise with the selected pathway; gather one observable piece of evidence.
**Worked model:** Plan the audience and purpose first, select relevant details, then organise ideas into a clear beginning, middle and end.
**Core task:** Name the audience and purpose for your response. Select two relevant details from the passage. Create the requested text using a clear structure. Explain one language choice that suits your audience.
**Resources:** Passage in the matching learner worksheet; vocabulary support; paper or accessible digital response tools.
**Evidence:** A response that demonstrates the daily goal, with the learner's communication method and prompt level recorded.
**Next step:** Reteach with a shorter text or more explicit model if needed; extend with comparison, justification or transfer when evidence is secure.
