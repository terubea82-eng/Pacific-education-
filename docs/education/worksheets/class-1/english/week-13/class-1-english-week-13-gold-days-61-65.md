## My story — read this page
Before class, Mere places her red ball beside the large tree in the school playground. During break, she plays with it there. When the bell rings, she collects the ball and returns to her classroom.

## My pathway support
Read independently if accessible; otherwise listen to the passage. Extend by selecting precise evidence and explaining how it supports an answer.

## Worked example
Teacher models: “Who is the story about?” Find the name in the story. Example answer: “Mere.” Then model: “Where does the story happen?” Find the place word(s), such as “school”. A **character** is who the story is about. The **setting** is where the story happens.
