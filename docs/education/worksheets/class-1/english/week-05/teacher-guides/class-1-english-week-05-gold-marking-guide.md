# PacEdu Teacher-Only Marking Guide — Class 1 English
## Week 5 · Days 21–25 · Gold pathway

**Matched learner worksheet:** class-1-english-week-05-gold-days-21-25.md
**Learning intention:** Explain letter-case relationships and apply S/s and A/a recognition to familiar taught words.

**Fair evidence:** Accept spoken, signed, AAC, dictated or written work. Judge the concept, not accent or handwriting.

### Day 21
- Correct classification: S/s group and A/a group.
- Explanation: capital and small forms differ in appearance but represent the same letter.

### Day 22
- Correct pairs: S–s and A–a.
- Learner should reject the claim that A and s are the same letter and explain they are different letters.

### Day 23
- Examples must be drawn from words already taught.
- **sat** is conditional: S/s at the beginning and A/a in the middle, only if all correspondences are known.
- Credit correct location and comparison without requiring decoding of untaught letters.

### Day 24
- Three short-a words only from the taught and approved class word list.
- If short-a has not been taught, do not score it as a failure; use the alternative letter classification task.

### Day 25
- Credit independent letter forms, valid familiar word examples, and an explanation distinguishing letter recognition from reading a whole word.
