# Teacher-only guide — Class 2 English Week 7 (Gold)

**Days 31–35 | Fluency with familiar texts**

**Matching learner file:** class-2-english-week-07-gold-learner.md

## Learning intention
Build familiarity, accuracy, meaningful phrasing, and confidence through repeated reading or supported access to a short text. Fluency is not speed alone; understanding and comfortable pacing matter.

## Access and teaching support
Invite independent reading first, followed by expressive rereading. Discuss how punctuation and phrase boundaries support meaning. Ask for evidence from the passage. Allow the learner to rehearse before responding.

Use the complete passage printed in the matching learner worksheet. Do not require oral reading where this is not an accessible or appropriate response mode. For learners using AAC, sign, listening, or supported reading, assess the intended comprehension and engagement without treating speech as the only evidence.

## Indicative answer guide
1. **Explain the main idea of the passage.**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: A brief visit from a bird becomes memorable because the class notices and listens.
2. **Choose two verbs that make the bird's actions clear.**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: Settled, tilted, listening, gave, opened, disappeared; any two.
3. **Read a sentence with expression. Explain how punctuation or phrasing guides your reading.**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: A suitable sentence with pauses at punctuation and phrasing that supports meaning.
4. **What does “brief visit” mean in this passage?**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: A short visit.
5. **What wider idea does the final sentence suggest? Use one detail from the passage.**
   - Indicative answer/evidence: Paying attention can make ordinary moments meaningful; the class paused and listened to the bird.

Accept valid equivalent wording and accessible communication modes. Give feedback on one manageable next step (for example, noticing a full stop, rereading a phrase, or recognising an action word). Avoid ranking learners by reading speed.
