## Learning focus
I can find nouns (naming words), find verbs (action words), sort words, and use nouns and verbs to make meaningful sentences.

## Read this story
The school gardening group worked beside the classroom. Ana carried a bucket while Ravi loosened the soil. The learners planted three young seedlings in a row. Ana poured water around each plant. A teacher checked the labels and praised the careful work. The group cleaned the tools before returning to class.

**Word bank:** group • classroom • Ana • Ravi • bucket • soil • seedlings • row • plant • teacher • labels • tools • carried • loosened • planted • poured • checked • praised • cleaned

## Worked example
In “The bird sings,” **bird** is a noun because it names an animal. **sings** is a verb because it tells what the bird does. The words make a meaningful sentence.
