## Learning goal
I can build and read familiar consonant-vowel-consonant (CVC) words using only letter-sound correspondences my class has already learned.

## How to work
Build/read familiar decodable words independently where possible. Compare words or change one tile only when the new word uses taught correspondences.

Your teacher chooses the actual decodable words and picture cards from the taught class sequence. Do not try to read an unfamiliar or untaught word. These pages provide sound boxes; your teacher may show approved letter tiles and the word card alongside you.

## Day 36 — Listen and notice

**My task:** Listen to a familiar teacher-selected word. Notice the first, middle and last sounds.

**Worked example:** Teacher says a familiar word, stretches its three sounds, places one taught tile in each box, then blends and says the word.

**My activity:** Build a familiar word and tell, show or sign one sound in it.
