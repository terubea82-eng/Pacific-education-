### Read this passage
The class has no safe place to keep its storybooks during reading time. Books are sometimes left on the floor and become bent. Jone suggests making a small book stand from a clean cardboard carton. He measures the space on the table and sketches a wide base with a raised back. He knows that thin paper may bend under the weight of books, while firm cardboard can keep its shape. He will ask the teacher to check the plan before cutting. After making the stand, he will test it with two books and adjust the base if it wobbles.

### Worked example
A design begins with a need. The need is to keep books upright. A material must be strong enough for the job. A wide base can help the stand stay steady. The first test shows whether the design works.
