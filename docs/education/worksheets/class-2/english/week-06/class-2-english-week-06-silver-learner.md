## Read or listen to this passage

HOW OUR CLASS GARDEN GREW
Our class decided to turn a bare patch beside the classroom into a garden. We loosened the soil, planted bean seeds, and watered them carefully. Mia checked the soil each morning, while Tom removed weeds that could take water from the young plants. After several days, green shoots appeared. The class understood that growing plants need water, space, and regular care.

## Helpful words
bare patch · carefully · checked · removed · appeared · regular care

## Worked example
**Question:** What is the passage mainly about?

**How to work it out:** Look at the title. Notice what happens in most of the sentences. Join those ideas into one short answer.

**Example response:** The class plants and cares for a garden. (Your passage may use more details; answers can be spoken, signed, shown, drawn, or written.)
