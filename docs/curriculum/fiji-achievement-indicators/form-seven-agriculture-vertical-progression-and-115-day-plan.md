# Form Seven Agriculture — Vertical Progression and 9/9/5 Planning

**Status:** Working crosswalk and timetable proposal; not a Ministry-approved curriculum map or school timetable.  
**Date:** 12 November 2026  
**Branch:** `pacedu/simple-subject-workspace-staging-20261111`

## 1. Purpose and governing rule

Check whether the Form Seven Agriculture SLOs build on learning already developed in earlier Fiji classes and related subjects. Preserve the Form Seven EQAP outcome as the destination; link it to prerequisite knowledge, skills and evidence in earlier levels. Do not duplicate a full earlier lesson where the learner has already mastered the prerequisite. Diagnose and bridge gaps where needed.

The 2023–2025 South Pacific Form Seven Certificate Agriculture syllabus explicitly expects successful completion of national Year 12/Form 6 Agriculture or equivalent. It also expects related outcomes from Year 12 or equivalent. Therefore Form 7 must not be treated as a first introduction to agriculture. The school must confirm the student's actual Year 12 Agriculture pathway and any equivalent route.

## 4. Confirmed cross-curricular connections already identified

1. **Mathematics:** measurement, mass/volume/area, tables, graphs, data interpretation and financial calculations are foundational to farm planning, field trials, yield, price and supply/demand analysis.
2. **Science/Biology:** plant structures and reproduction, living things, ecosystems, soil, water, biodiversity, pests and experimental method underpin crop/livestock production and sustainability.
3. **English:** reading technical information, interpreting instructions, recording a logbook, writing explanations/reports, presenting findings and using subject vocabulary support the Form Seven investigation and case study.
4. **Social Studies/Geography:** land use, resources, weather, environment, community organisations and trade/policy context support production planning and market controls.
5. **Commercial Studies/Accounting/Economics:** budgeting, costs, capital, profit, markets, supply/demand and financial decisions support the economics of primary production. Fiji's Financial Education programme documents integration through the named subjects across Year 1–12.
6. **Technology/ICT:** tools, technology, data recording, information search and communication support modern production and market research. Exact Agriculture-related outcomes must be verified before mapping.

These are cross-curricular prerequisite links. They do not mean that the same Agriculture SLO should be copied into every linked subject's assessment record.

## 5. 9/9/5 calendar arithmetic and 140-hour reconciliation

The EQAP syllabus suggests 28 teaching weeks at 5 hours/week = **140 hours**. PacEdu's 9/9/5 calendar contains 45 + 45 + 25 = **115 school days**. Assuming five school days per week, that is 23 teaching weeks.

- 140 hours ÷ 115 days = **1.217 hours per school day**, approximately **73 minutes/day**.
- 140 hours ÷ 23 weeks = **6.09 Agriculture hours/week** on average.
- A 5-hour/week timetable over 23 weeks supplies only 115 hours, leaving a **25-hour shortfall**.
- Do not label 115 days as equivalent to the syllabus's 28 weeks. The syllabus duration is subject-specific and must be reconciled with the school's actual timetable.

### Proposed planning envelope if the school approves 140 hours in 23 weeks

| PacEdu term | School days | Weeks (5-day assumption) | Agriculture hours | Average hours/week |
|---|---:|---:|---:|---:|
| Term 1 | 45 | 9 | 55 | 6.11 |
| Term 2 | 45 | 9 | 45 | 5.00 |
| Term 3 | 25 | 5 | 40 | 8.00 |
| **Total** | **115** | **23** | **140** | **6.09 average** |

This preserves the syllabus's strand time allocations (Production Management 55h; Sustainable Primary Production 45h; Marketing in Primary Production 40h) and its strand sequence. It makes Term 3 intensive. The school must approve the timetable, practical access and assessment calendar before this option is adopted.

**Alternative if the school cannot provide 140 hours:** keep the 28-week/140-hour course duration by extending the Agriculture teaching window beyond the 115-day general calendar or by formally changing the school calendar. Do not silently reduce the hours to 115 or delete SLOs.

### Day-to-hour conversion rule for PacEdu

Do not assume one school day equals one lesson. Every day record must carry:
- planned minutes/hours;
- number and length of periods;
- theory/practical split;
- prerequisite indicator(s);
- EQAP SLO code and SOLO level;
- evidence of learning;
- internal/external assessment classification;
- teacher confirmation of local plant/animal availability and safe practical access.

## 6. Recommended sequence for a coherent Form Seven course

1. **Entry diagnostic and bridging:** check Year 12/Form 6 Agriculture or equivalent evidence, science concepts, numeracy, technical reading and practical investigation skills. Provide short prerequisite bridges without replacing the formal Year 12 prerequisite.
2. **Production Management (55h):** schedule of operations for one locally available plant and one animal primary product; site, cultivar/breed, planting material, management, establishment, production, harvest and post-harvest; resources influencing decisions; field investigation planning and execution.
3. **Sustainable Primary Production (45h):** analyse management practices, genetic resources, waste, soil, water, husbandry/agronomy, pests/diseases/weeds, hygiene, invasive species, biodiversity, genetic engineering, organic husbandry and pollution.
4. **Marketing in Primary Production (40h):** seasonality, imports, market access, storage/shelf life, transport, channels, quality, international markets, grower organisations, supply/demand, biosecurity, import/export controls, trade agreements and policy; complete the case study.
5. **Assessment and moderation:** preserve the syllabus internal-assessment tasks and weightings. The practical investigation and case study require authentic learner evidence and teacher oversight; they must not be replaced by auto-generated multiple-choice worksheets.

## 7. PacEdu integration rules

- Use a many-to-many prerequisite link: one earlier concept can support multiple Form Seven SLOs; one SLO can rely on multiple subjects and earlier outcomes.
- Each link stores source authority, title, edition/effective date, year/class, subject, exact outcome code, page/section, source URL, verification state and reviewer.
- Distinguish `VERIFIED_PREREQUISITE`, `SUPPORTING_LEAD_ONLY`, `TEACHER_BRIDGE_REQUIRED`, `NOT_FOUND_YET`, and `NOT_A_PREREQUISITE`.
- If the learner has mastered the prerequisite, use a brief retrieval/review, not a repeated full lesson. If not, offer an accessible bridge and record the gap.
- Preserve official source outcome and SOLO level. Bloom taxonomy question types are a separate design field.
- Do not assign a prerequisite from another country or a different Fiji year level merely because the concept sounds similar.
- Keep learner-facing Form Seven Agriculture disabled until source version, Fiji adoption, school timetable, prerequisite pathway, reproduction permission and teacher assessment arrangements are confirmed.
