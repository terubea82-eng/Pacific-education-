# PacEdu Class 3 Home Economics — Term 2, Week 7 (Overall Week 16)
## Theme: Healthy Food Choices, Food Hygiene, Safe Preparation and Caring for Resources

**Status:** PacEdu-adapted draft for teacher review. The achievement indicator is provisional and is not quoted as official Fiji Ministry wording. Check the school's ready-to-use Fiji NCF 2013 scheme and local food-safety procedures before classroom release.

**Provisional shared achievement indicator:** Learners identify a balanced everyday food choice, explain basic hygiene before handling food, follow safe adult-supervised preparation steps, reduce food waste and describe ways to care for shared household and classroom resources.

**Pathways:** Copper, Bronze, Silver and Gold address the same weekly objective with different levels of scaffolding and independence. All practical food preparation requires adult supervision, clean water, safe ingredients and school-approved procedures. Learners must not use knives, stoves, hot water or electrical appliances independently.
