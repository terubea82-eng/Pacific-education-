## Assessment boundaries
Assess only familiar words and grapheme–phoneme correspondences already taught in this class. The teacher selects and records the actual examples before the activity. Do not penalise a learner for not knowing an untaught correspondence. Accept speech, pointing, matching, sign, AAC, dictated response, drawing, or writing where appropriate. Record the response mode and prompt level; this is formative evidence, not a permanent ability label.

## Day 31 — Listen and notice

**Evidence sought:** Recognises or matches one familiar word/object by its taught initial sound.

**Suggested scoring:**
- **2 — demonstrated:** accurate response for the selected familiar example, independently or using the learner's established access method.
- **1 — developing:** response is accurate after a verbal/visual cue, repetition, or model.
- **0 — not yet demonstrated:** no reliable response observed after accessible modelling and adequate wait time; plan another opportunity rather than infer inability.
- **NO — not observed:** task not completed or access conditions were unsuitable.
