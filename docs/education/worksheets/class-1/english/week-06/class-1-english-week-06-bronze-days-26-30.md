**Learning intention:** Understand that print carries meaning, distinguish a letter from a word, and notice how print is arranged on a page.

**Teacher preparation:** Use a familiar, approved picture book or short printed class text. Check that its cover/title and words are visible. Do not require learners to decode text beyond what has been taught. Use a clean, enlarged copy or tactile/raised print where useful.

**Ways to answer:** Point, speak, sign, use AAC, trace, match, draw, dictate or write. Teacher reads instructions aloud and describes visual information. Accept a response by eye gaze or partner-assisted scanning where appropriate.

**Access:** Large print, high contrast, uncluttered print samples, tactile letter cards, a line guide, extra wait time and repeated instructions. Do not treat visual tracking or speech as the only way to demonstrate understanding.

**Ability pathway:** Bronze — visual cues, matching and supported print search

## Day 26 — Understand a book cover
**Worked example:** The title is the name of the book. Teacher points to the title on a familiar cover.

1. On the cover, circle or point to the title.
2. Point to one picture.
3. What can the printed title tell us? the book's name / the weather.
4. Draw or dictate one thing a printed sign or label can tell us:
____________________________________________________________

## Day 27 — Letter or word?
**Worked example:** **m** is one letter. **map** is a word made from letters. Use only familiar words where decoding is requested.

1. Write L for one letter and W for a word:
**s** ___   **sun** ___   **A** ___   **cat** ___
2. Circle the single letter: **t** / **tap**.
3. Underline the word: **m** / **map**.
4. Write or select one letter: ______ and one familiar word: ______.

## Day 28 — Notice spaces
**Worked example:** In **I see a cat.**, spaces separate the words.

1. Count the words in **I see a cat.**: ______
2. Put a slash ( / ) at each space:
**We can play.**
3. Copy one word from the sentence: ___________________________
4. Explain or show why spaces help a reader:
____________________________________________________________

## Day 29 — Search a printed page
Use a familiar class book or printed passage.
