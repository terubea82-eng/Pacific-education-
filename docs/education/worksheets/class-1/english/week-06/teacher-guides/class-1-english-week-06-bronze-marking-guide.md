# PacEdu Teacher-Only Marking Guide — Class 1 English
## Week 6 · Days 26–30 · Bronze pathway

**Matched worksheet:** class-1-english-week-06-bronze-days-26-30.md
**Learning intention:** Identify the title, distinguish letters and words, and notice spaces.

**Fair evidence:** Accept pointing, oral, signed, AAC, dictated or written responses. Do not score reading fluency or handwriting on this print-concept task.

### Day 26
- Title is the book's name; picture is not the title.
- Credit an appropriate example of information print can communicate.

### Day 27
- **s** L; **sun** W; **A** L; **cat** W.
- **t** is a letter; **map** is a word.
- Credit one accurate letter and one familiar word.

### Day 28
- **I see a cat.** has four words.
- **We can play.** → We / can / play.
- Credit a correct copied word and explanation that spaces separate words.

### Day 29
- Use a familiar class text; accept correct title, first word, named letter/word and direction shown with pointer/line guide.

### Day 30
- Credit meaningful print example, A as letter and fish as word, a marked space and “letters” as the completion for “A word is made from…”
