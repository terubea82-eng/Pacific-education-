## Learning goal
I can listen to instructions, follow steps in order, ask or answer questions and give a clear direction.

## Read or listen to this complete message
On Monday, the teacher gives Class 2 three directions for a reading activity: take one book from the shelf, sit on the mat, and open the book at the first page. The class listens before beginning. After reading, learners place the book back on the return trolley.

## Worked example
Instruction: “Take one book from the shelf, then sit on the mat.”  
Step 1: Take one book.  
Step 2: Sit on the mat.  
The word **then** helps show the order.

## Support for this pathway
Use the same instruction objective. Extend by giving a clear instruction to a partner, checking order or explaining a practical reason.
