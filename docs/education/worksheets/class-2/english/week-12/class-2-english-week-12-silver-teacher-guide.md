# Class 2 English — Week 12 (Silver Pathway)

**Teacher-only guide — Days 56–60: Sentence Building and Completeness**

**Matching learner file:** class-2-english-week-12-silver-learner.md  
**Pathway:** Silver

**Official indicator mapping:** PENDING_VERIFICATION

## Teaching intention
Learners arrange words in meaningful order, recognise complete ideas, expand incomplete sentences and communicate using complete sentences. Reinforce capital letters and suitable end punctuation without making handwriting the only evidence of learning.

## Suggested daily teaching sequence
- **Day 56:** Model a complete idea orally and in print. Ask who/what and what happens.
- **Day 57:** Use word cards or movable symbols to arrange a sentence; read it back to check meaning.
- **Day 58:** Offer a sentence starter and invite a meaningful completion.
- **Day 59:** Compare a complete sentence with a fragment. Support the learner to add the missing idea.
- **Day 60:** Invite connected sentences about a familiar topic and review meaning, order and punctuation.

## Indicative responses and marking notes
- **Day 56:** Accept a correct selection or identification of a complete sentence or suitable missing word.
- **Day 57:** Accept the target words in a grammatical order. Capitalisation and punctuation may be corrected with support.
- **Day 58:** Accept any contextually sensible completion that forms a whole idea.
- **Day 59:** Look for recognition of an incomplete idea and a meaningful completion.
- **Day 60:** Look for complete, understandable sentences in a logical order. Credit valid equivalent wording and accessible communication.

## Pathway-specific answer guidance
- **Copper:** Likely answers include “Sera walks to the beach”; “Sera sees a shell”; “pink”; and a relevant sentence about the wave or Sera's actions.
- **Bronze:** “Jone sweeps the floor”; “His sister washes the cups”; “together”; and a combined sentence such as “Jone helps by sweeping.”
- **Silver:** “Our class has a small garden”; “Each group waters the seeds”; a meaningful completion such as “The green shoots appear”; and “After some days” can be completed as “After some days, green shoots appear.”
- **Gold:** In “Another group makes neat labels”, subject = “Another group”, verb = “makes”; “The class arranges the work”; accept a logical reason after “because”; “On Friday” is a time phrase, not a complete sentence by itself.

## Accessibility and differentiation
Provide large print, uncluttered layouts, word cards, symbol or picture supports, tactile materials, extra processing time, repeated directions, sign, AAC, oral response or scribing. Reduce the number of word cards when needed while retaining the sentence-building goal. Extend learners by adding conjunctions, reasons or details and checking that the longer sentence remains clear.
