## Learning intention and pathway support
Listen for key words and details, identify people/places/objects, answer questions and retell information in order.

Use the same message and objective. Extend by selecting precise evidence, comparing details or explaining why a detail matters; do not add untaught content.

## Complete spoken message
On Friday, Class 2 will visit the school library. The class will meet their teacher beside the mango tree at nine o’clock. Each learner should bring a reading book and a water bottle. First, the teacher will explain the library rules. Next, learners will choose a book. Before returning to class, everyone will place the books on the return trolley.

## Worked example and indicative answers
Model the first sentence and ask: “Where will Class 2 go?” Expected answer: **the school library**.

- Day 1: Class 2 will go to the school library.
- Day 2: Accept reading book or water bottle (one item).
- Day 3: Class meets beside the mango tree at nine o'clock.
- Day 4: Teacher explains library rules first; learners choose a book next.
- Day 5: Accept an accurate retell in sequence: meet the teacher; hear the rules; choose a book; put books on the return trolley before returning to class.

Accept equivalent meaningful wording and responses in speech, sign, AAC, pointing, matching, drawing, dictation or writing. For Day 3, the expected details are the meeting place and time. Do not require spoken answers if another response mode demonstrates listening comprehension.

## Daily teaching and evidence guide
| Day | Teacher action | Evidence |
|---|---|---|
| 1 — Listen for key words | Read the message clearly. Repeat once; ask learner to identify a key place or topic. | One relevant key word/detail. |
| 2 — Identify people, places and objects | Read again. Invite identification of a person, place or object named. | Correct identification of at least one named item. |
| 3 — Listen for two details | Ask for two details from the meeting information. | Mango tree and nine o’clock, with accessible response options. |
| 4 — Answer questions | Ask who/what/where or order questions from the message. | Accurate answer and evidence of listening to the message. |
| 5 — Retell | Invite learner to sequence the main information using first/next/last or an equivalent sequence. | Ordered retell; record number of relevant details. |

## Marking and accessibility
- Mark the listening/comprehension objective, not speech fluency, accent, handwriting or memory beyond the task demand.
- Repeat the message as the agreed access support; record when repetition or visual support is used.
- Copper: small choice set, picture/object cues, one question at a time, adult model and generous wait time.
- Bronze: word/picture bank and guided responses, gradually reducing prompts.
- Silver: core response with minimal prompting.
- Gold: credit reasoned explanations supported by the message; do not require one exact phrase.
- Record prompt level: independent / with prompt / with model / not yet observed.
- Pathway is adjustable and must not become a permanent learner label.
