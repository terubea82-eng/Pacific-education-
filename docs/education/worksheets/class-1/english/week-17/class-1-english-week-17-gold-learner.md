**How to answer:** You may write, draw, point, choose, sign, use AAC or dictate your answer. Your teacher can record your response. Take your time.

## Read or listen to the whole passage

> At the school garden, Jone notices a narrow wooden bench beside a tall hibiscus plant. A small brown bird hops onto the rough path and pauses near a fallen red flower. Jone describes what he sees in his notebook so that a classmate who was absent can imagine the scene. Carefully chosen describing words make the information clearer.

**Remember:** A noun (naming word) names a person, place, animal or thing. A describing word gives more information. An expanded noun group puts a noun and one or more describing words together, for example “a red ball”.

## Worked example

Noun group: “a small brown bird”. Naming word: bird. Describing words: small and brown. The expanded group gives the reader two details about the bird.
