# Pacific Education — Complete Legal Publication, Compliance, Governance and Release Implementation Master Specification\n\n**Status:** MASTER IMPLEMENTATION SPECIFICATION — prepared for practical implementation, specialist review and controlled publication.\n**Production status:** BLOCKED until actual implementation and required independent/professional evidence are complete.\n**Authority boundary:** This document organizes evidence and workflows; it is not legal advice, certification, government approval or proof of compliance.\n\n## 1. Master lifecycle\n\nUse one controlled lifecycle:\n\nDESIGN → DOCUMENT → PROTOTYPE → CONTROLLED PILOT → PRACTICAL IMPLEMENTATION → EVIDENCE VERIFIED → SPECIALIST REVIEW → LEGAL/REGULATORY REVIEW → REQUIRED REVISIONS → OWNER APPROVAL → PUBLICATION PREPARATION → PUBLICATION → EFFECTIVE VERSION → PRODUCTION RELEASE → CONTINUOUS MONITORING → PERIODIC REVIEW\n\nEvery transition must have a recorded actor, timestamp, evidence and decision.\n\n## 2. Master legal/publication register\n\nThe app must maintain one authoritative register containing:\n\n- requirement ID and stable code;\n- category;\n- title and description;\n- jurisdiction;\n- affected population;\n- applicability status and documented reasoning;\n- official/source authority;\n- source title, version, date and reference;\n- implementation owner;\n- affected feature/control;\n- evidence records;\n- specialist review;\n- legal/professional review;\n- government/authority decision where applicable;\n- owner approval;\n- document version;\n- publication status;\n- effective date;\n- next review date;\n- unresolved issues;\n- audit history.\n\n## 3. Required policy/document library\n\nPrepare and manage, as applicable:\n\n1. Terms of Service/User Terms;\n2. Privacy Notice;\n3. Data Governance Policy;\n4. Child Safeguarding Policy;\n5. Parent/Guardian Information and Consent materials;\n6. Learner-facing notice;\n7. Teacher/Staff responsibilities and terms;\n8. Acceptable Use Policy;\n9. Communication/community rules;\n10. Assessment and academic-integrity rules;\n11. Complaints and dispute procedure;\n12. Refund/cancellation policy;\n13. Payment/subscription terms;\n14. Accessibility statement;\n15. Security and responsible-disclosure policy;\n16. Incident/breach response procedure;\n17. Data retention/deletion policy;\n18. Individual data-request procedure;\n19. Vendor/data-processing requirements;\n20. Hosting/provider records;\n21. Curriculum-source register;\n22. Intellectual property and ownership notice;\n23. Third-party/open-source licence register;\n24. Marketplace/app-store disclosure package;\n25. Marketing/claims evidence register;\n26. International-jurisdiction terms where applicable;\n27. Business/tax/insurance records where applicable;\n28. Professional/legal review records;\n29. Government/Ministry correspondence and approvals only when actually obtained.\n\n## 4. Document metadata\n\nEvery controlled document must contain:\n\n- document ID;\n- document type;\n- title;\n- owner;\n- responsible operational owner;\n- jurisdiction;\n- audience;\n- purpose;\n- version;\n- status;\n- publication date;\n- effective date;\n- superseded date;\n- review date;\n- source references;\n- related system controls;\n- reviewer;\n- reviewer role;\n- approval authority;\n- approval date;\n- change reason;\n- change history;\n- accessibility status;\n- language/translation status;\n- public location where applicable.\n\n## 5. Document state machine\n\nNormal state:\n\nDRAFT → INTERNAL_REVIEW → EVIDENCE_READY → SPECIALIST_REVIEW → LEGAL_REVIEW → OWNER_APPROVAL → PUBLISH_READY → PUBLISHED → EFFECTIVE → SUPERSEDED\n\nOther states:\n\n- REVISION_REQUIRED;\n- BLOCKED;\n- NOT_APPLICABLE;\n- WITHDRAWN.\n\nThe server must reject invalid transitions.\n\n## 6. Actual database domains\n\nProduction storage must include controlled records for:\n\n- accounts;\n- roles;\n- profiles;\n- organizations/schools;\n- classes/groups;\n- education relationships;\n- curriculum references;\n- lessons/activities;\n- learning progress;\n- assessments;\n- assessment attempts/responses;\n- authoritative results/marks;\n- offline synchronization operations;\n- compliance requirements;\n- evidence;\n- reviews;\n- approvals;\n- legal/authority decisions;\n- controlled documents and versions;\n- publication records;\n- compliance tasks;\n- compliance issues;\n- audit events;\n- incidents;\n- complaints;\n- vendors/providers;\n- retention/deletion controls.\n\nEach record needs a server-generated unique identifier and created/updated timestamps. Critical relationships must use integrity constraints.\n\n## 7. Authorization\n\nProduction authorization must follow:\n\nIDENTITY → ACCOUNT STATUS → ROLE → VERIFIED RELATIONSHIP → RESOURCE SCOPE → ACTION → POLICY → DECISION\n\nDefault decision is DENY.\n\nBrowser JavaScript, localStorage, URL parameters, hidden fields and prototype flags are never authoritative.\n\n## 8. Roles\n\nPossible roles:\n\n- learner/student;\n- teacher;\n- parent/guardian;\n- authorized education authority;\n- compliance administrator;\n- technical/security reviewer;\n- curriculum reviewer;\n- legal/professional reviewer;\n- owner/authorized administrator.\n\nA role alone does not grant access to another person's information.\n\n## 9. Education relationships\n\nRelationship records must contain:\n\n- parties;\n- relationship type;\n- scope;\n- verification state;\n- verification source/reference;\n- effective dates;\n- revocation/expiry;\n- audit history.\n\nExamples: learner-teacher, learner-parent/guardian, learner-class and teacher-class.\n\nRelationship availability never means information access.\n\n## 10. Privacy/data governance\n\nBefore production, inventory every personal-data field and record:\n\n- purpose;\n- necessity;\n- collection point;\n- user notice;\n- authorized use;\n- access scope;\n- recipient/vendor;\n- storage location;\n- retention;\n- deletion/anonymization method;\n- security control;\n- incident pathway;\n- review status.\n\nDo not assume a legal basis or consent requirement without appropriate review for the actual service and jurisdiction.\n\n## 11. Children and safeguarding\n\nPrepare controls for:\n\n- child-facing design;\n- adult/child communication boundaries;\n- reporting and escalation;\n- prohibited conduct;\n- moderation;\n- safeguarding incident handling;\n- evidence preservation;\n- access restriction;\n- staff/vendor responsibilities;\n- training/acknowledgement where applicable;\n- parent/guardian involvement where applicable.\n\nSafeguarding case information must be restricted and must never appear in ordinary public logs.\n\n## 12. Curriculum/legal-source controls\n\nMaintain separate labels for:\n\n- official authority source;\n- verified official reference;\n- Pacific Education internal mapping;\n- teacher-created material;\n- generated/AI-assisted material.\n\nEvery official curriculum reference must record authority, title, version/date, applicable level/subject, source reference, verification status, verifier and verification date.\n\nInternal mapping must never be represented as government approval.\n\nCurriculum changes must trigger source revalidation and impact review.\n\n## 13. Assessment and marks\n\nThe client may submit responses but cannot establish an authoritative mark.\n\nServer must verify identity, assessment availability, learner scope, submission integrity and applicable assessment rules before creating an authoritative result.\n\nChanges to authoritative results require controlled authorization and an audit trail.\n\n## 14. Payment/publication controls\n\nMaintain:\n\n- current price;\n- currency;\n- billing period;\n- payment method;\n- provider;\n- taxes/charges where applicable;\n- cancellation;\n- refunds;\n- failed payments;\n- duplicate payments;\n- receipts;\n- entitlement status;\n- reconciliation;\n- disputes.\n\nBrowser-side payment status is never proof of payment.\n\nPayment-provider and processing arrangements must be reviewed for applicable requirements before production activation.\n\n## 15. Ownership/IP\n\nPublic ownership information must identify the actual owner and applicable licence terms.\n\nMaintain a private register for:\n\n- contributors;\n- contracts;\n- third-party components;\n- open-source licences;\n- content licences;\n- vendor rights;\n- assignment/licensing documents.\n\nFunding, hosting, technical support, distribution or services do not by themselves establish transfer of platform ownership.\n\n## 16. Vendor register\n\nFor each production provider record:\n\n- legal entity;\n- service;\n- purpose;\n- data accessed;\n- jurisdiction;\n- data location;\n- security evidence;\n- contractual status;\n- sub-processors where relevant;\n- incident obligations;\n- deletion/return requirements;\n- termination process;\n- owner approval;\n- professional/legal review.\n\n## 17. Complaints\n\nWorkflow:\n\nRECEIVED → ACKNOWLEDGED → CLASSIFIED → ASSIGNED → INVESTIGATED → RESPONSE → ESCALATED IF REQUIRED → RESOLVED → CLOSED\n\nCategories must include access, privacy, safeguarding, curriculum, assessment, payment, accessibility, conduct, technical service, content and IP.\n\nHigh-risk safeguarding/security matters follow their dedicated escalation procedures.\n\n## 18. Security incidents\n\nIncident records must include:\n\n- incident ID;\n- detection time;\n- source;\n- affected system;\n- severity;\n- affected data category;\n- containment;\n- investigation owner;\n- evidence;\n- notification assessment;\n- professional/legal review;\n- corrective action;\n- closure approval.\n\nThe system must not automatically publish an incident merely because an alert exists.\n\n## 19. Accessibility\n\nTest public and authenticated workflows on supported Android and desktop environments.\n\nVerify:\n\n- responsive layout;\n- readable text;\n- usable controls;\n- keyboard/accessibility support where applicable;\n- meaningful headings;\n- accessible errors;\n- no color-only status communication;\n- low-bandwidth behavior;\n- assistive-technology behavior where applicable.\n\nRecord device, environment, tester, date, defects and resolution.\n\n## 20. Offline operation\n\nOffline operations must use unique operation IDs, revalidation, idempotency and conflict handling.\n\nFinal legal review, owner approval, publication and effective activation cannot be completed solely offline.\n\nServer state remains authoritative.\n\n## 21. Public/private separation\n\nPUBLIC:\n- current effective terms;\n- privacy notice;\n- required policies/notices;\n- support/complaints information;\n- approved ownership/licensing statements.\n\nRESTRICTED:\n- legal advice;\n- security vulnerabilities;\n- safeguarding cases;\n- private contracts;\n- credentials;\n- sensitive personal information;\n- confidential reviewer material.\n\n## 22. In-app dashboard\n\nRoute: `/admin/compliance`.\n\nHeader: **Legal & Compliance Register**.\n\nDisplay summary counts for:\n\n- Total;\n- Evidence Ready;\n- Specialist Review;\n- Legal Review;\n- Owner Approval;\n- Publish Ready;\n- Effective;\n- Blocked;\n- Overdue.\n\nDo not display a single legal-compliance score or overall political/marketing-style rating.\n\nFilters: category, jurisdiction, status, priority, reviewer, due date and publication status.\n\n## 23. Requirement detail UI\n\nSections:\n\nOverview → Source → Applicability → Implementation → Evidence → Reviews → Authority Decisions → Approval → Publication → Issues → Audit\n\nEvery decision must show the exact version and evidence used.\n\n## 24. Publication gate\n\nBefore publication the server checks:\n\n- correct jurisdiction;\n- correct document type;\n- approved version;\n- required implementation evidence;\n- required specialist review;\n- required legal/professional review;\n- authority decision where applicable;\n- owner approval where applicable;\n- zero blocking issues;\n- effective date;\n- accessibility check;\n- public/private classification.\n\nFailure produces `PUBLISH_BLOCKED`.\n\nPassing applicable checks produces `PUBLISH_READY`, not automatic publication.\n\n## 25. Automatic practical-to-legal transition\n\nWhen a practical stage is independently verified:\n\nSTAGE VERIFIED → CREATE REVIEW PACKAGE → LINK EVIDENCE → IDENTIFY REVIEWER → PREPARE QUESTIONS → REQUEST REVIEW → REVISION IF REQUIRED → OWNER APPROVAL → PUBLICATION GATE\n\nGuardian may automate low-risk documentation preparation.\n\nGuardian must never:\n\n- invent legal conclusions;\n- fabricate approval;\n- claim Ministry/government authorization;\n- certify compliance;\n- bypass professional review;\n- expose confidential legal advice;\n- activate production authorization.\n\n## 26. Automatic change triggers\n\nNew personal-data field → privacy review.\nNew child-facing feature → safeguarding/privacy review.\nNew communication feature → safeguarding/privacy/terms review.\nNew payment method → payment/consumer/privacy/reconciliation review.\nNew curriculum mapping → official-source verification.\nNew jurisdiction → jurisdictional review.\nAuthentication/authorization change → security/privacy review.\nMaterial terms/privacy change → re-review and publication gate reset.\n\n## 27. Server API\n\nRequired protected API families:\n\n- compliance requirements;\n- evidence;\n- reviews;\n- approvals;\n- documents and versions;\n- publication checks;\n- publication execution;\n- authority decisions;\n- tasks/issues;\n- audit history.\n\nEvery endpoint must authenticate and authorize server-side.\n\nResource IDs supplied by clients must never bypass authorization.\n\n## 28. Audit\n\nRecord creation, edit, status transition, evidence verification, review, approval, rejection, publication, supersession, authority-decision update and relevant restricted-record access.\n\nEvery material event records actor, timestamp, affected entity, action, reason and correlation/change reference.\n\n## 29. Legal-source register\n\nMaintain:\n\n- authority;\n- jurisdiction;\n- source title;\n- official reference/location;\n- version/date;\n- effective status;\n- retrieved/checked date;\n- next review;\n- affected requirements;\n- reviewer.\n\nA Bill, proposal or draft must not be treated as an enacted requirement without verification.\n\n## 30. Review package generation\n\nWhen a practical requirement is evidence-verified, generate:\n\n- requirement summary;\n- implementation evidence index;\n- affected feature list;\n- current policy/document version;\n- change impact;\n- unresolved questions;\n- proposed reviewer;\n- professional/legal questions;\n- authority-approval questions;\n- owner decision requirements.\n\n## 31. Professional/legal review questions\n\nGenerate questions such as:\n\n- What laws/regulations/standards apply?\n- What data-processing requirements apply?\n- What child-safeguarding obligations apply?\n- What education-sector approvals or permissions are required?\n- Are licences or registrations required?\n- What payment/consumer requirements apply?\n- Are cross-border transfers involved?\n- What contracts are required?\n- What retention/deletion requirements apply?\n- What incident-notification obligations apply?\n- What accessibility obligations apply?\n- What IP/licensing obligations apply?\n- What business/tax requirements apply?\n- Which documents must be public?\n- Which claims require documentary authority?\n\nThe app records answers supplied by authorized reviewers; it does not generate legal authority by itself.\n\n## 32. App-store/public marketplace package\n\nMaintain a versioned submission record containing, as applicable:\n\n- application identity;\n- developer/owner information;\n- version;\n- package identifier;\n- privacy URL;\n- terms URL;\n- support route;\n- age/content declarations;\n- data-safety disclosures;\n- permissions justification;\n- payment/subscription disclosures;\n- screenshots/descriptions;\n- claims evidence;\n- target jurisdictions;\n- reviewer notes;\n- submission date;\n- platform decision;\n- rejection/issues;\n- corrective submission history.\n\nMarketplace approval must remain distinct from government/legal approval.\n\n## 33. Publication execution\n\nAfter all applicable approvals:\n\n1. freeze approved version;\n2. run final publication checks;\n3. generate publication package;\n4. publish through approved channel;\n5. record public location;\n6. record timestamp;\n7. activate effective version;\n8. preserve evidence;\n9. notify affected users where required;\n10. schedule review.\n\n## 34. Release gate\n\nLegal publication status must feed into, but never replace, the production release gate.\n\nProduction remains BLOCKED if any mandatory requirement is incomplete, including applicable:\n\n- production database;\n- secure authentication;\n- authorization;\n- hosting;\n- cybersecurity;\n- privacy;\n- child safeguarding;\n- accessibility;\n- curriculum verification;\n- pilot/user testing;\n- payment verification;\n- backup/recovery;\n- monitoring/incident response;\n- professional/legal review;\n- owner approval.\n\n## 35. Required automated tests\n\nTest that:\n\n1. unauthorized users cannot access the register;\n2. roles cannot be self-assigned;\n3. reviewers cannot impersonate owner approval;\n4. unverified evidence cannot satisfy a gate;\n5. invalid state transitions are rejected;\n6. blocked items cannot publish;\n7. superseded documents cannot become current accidentally;\n8. document changes trigger required review;\n9. fake authority decisions cannot be created;\n10. Guardian cannot mark legal approval;\n11. confidential records remain restricted;\n12. audit events are generated;\n13. offline mode cannot finalize approval/publication;\n14. resource-ID manipulation cannot bypass authorization;\n15. backups can restore the implemented schema;\n16. deletion/retention controls behave as designed;\n17. accessibility checks are recorded;\n18. publication creates an immutable version reference.\n\n## 36. Evidence requirements\n\nEach completed gate requires objective evidence with:\n\n- evidence ID;\n- requirement ID;\n- exact requirement text;\n- evidence type;\n- status VERIFIED only after actual verification;\n- verifier;\n- verification timestamp;\n- environment;\n- safe reference;\n- reviewer/authority where required.\n\nDo not place production secrets, passwords, private keys, payment credentials or unnecessary child data in the repository.\n\n## 37. Guardian automation classes\n\nClass A — low-risk documentation/status/index changes may be automatically prepared when verified conditions are satisfied.\n\nClass B — curriculum, assessment, accessibility, database schema and similar matters require review-controlled pull requests.\n\nClass C — production credentials, signing keys, payment/auth/database secrets, infrastructure authorization and final release controls are protected and cannot be autonomously changed or deployed.\n\nAll automation must fail closed when evidence, reviewer, allowlist or completion state is missing.\n\n## 38. Owner governance\n\nOwner-controlled records must remain separate from technical implementation records.\n\nOwner approval must be explicit, version-specific and auditable.\n\nOwner approval does not replace professional, legal, regulatory or government approval where required.\n\n## 39. Continuous governance\n\nAfter publication monitor:\n\n- source/legal changes;\n- policy review dates;\n- evidence expiry;\n- security incidents;\n- privacy incidents;\n- safeguarding incidents;\n- accessibility defects;\n- curriculum changes;\n- payment reconciliation;\n- complaints;\n- user feedback;\n- production defects;\n- vendor changes;\n- infrastructure changes.\n\nMaterial changes reopen affected review gates.\n\n## 40. Final boundary\n\nPacific Education must not publish a claim that it is legally compliant, government-approved, Ministry-approved, certified, licensed or registered unless the applicable status is actually established by appropriate evidence and authorized review.\n\nThe application is an evidence and workflow system. Professional/legal reviewers and competent authorities determine legal/regulatory conclusions within their respective authority.\n\n**MASTER STATUS: PREPARED FOR IMPLEMENTATION — NOT LEGALLY APPROVED — PRODUCTION RELEASE REMAINS BLOCKED UNTIL ALL REQUIRED EVIDENCE AND APPROVALS ARE ACTUALLY COMPLETED.**
