## Read or listen to this text

THREE NOTES BEFORE THE BELL
A small bird settled on the fence outside our classroom just before the lesson began. While we arranged our books, it tilted its head as if listening to the sounds inside. Then it gave three clear notes. Our conversation stopped, and for a few seconds everyone listened. The school bell rang; the bird opened its wings and disappeared towards the trees. We returned to our work, but the brief visit gave us something to talk about. Even a short moment can become memorable when we pay attention.
