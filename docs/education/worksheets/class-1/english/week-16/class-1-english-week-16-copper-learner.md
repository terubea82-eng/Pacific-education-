**Learning intention:** I can use clues in a text to make a sensible prediction and connect the text to something I know or have experienced.

**How to answer:** You may write, draw, point, choose, sign, use AAC or dictate your answer. Your teacher can record your response. Take your time.

## Read or listen to the whole passage

> Mere sees dark clouds. She takes an umbrella. What might happen next?

**Remember:** A prediction is what you think may happen next. A connection is how the text reminds you of something you know or have experienced. Use clues. A prediction is not a fact unless the text tells us it happened.

## Worked example

Clue: Mere takes an umbrella. Prediction: It may rain. I used the clue about the umbrella.
