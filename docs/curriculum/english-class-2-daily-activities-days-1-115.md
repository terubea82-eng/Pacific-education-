## Programme structure
- 23 planning weeks × 5 learning days = 115 activities. Term 1: Weeks 1–9; Term 2: Weeks 10–18; Term 3: Weeks 19–23. Reconcile with the school's approved calendar and actual teaching days.
- Four flexible pathways: Copper, Bronze, Silver and Gold. All pathways share the same learning intention; only scaffolding, response mode and independence vary.
- Learning areas used as PacEdu planning headings: Listening and Speaking; Reading and Viewing; Writing and Shaping; vocabulary/word study; language conventions. Confirm exact current syllabus terminology with the authorised school copy.

## Source and alignment safeguard
The Fiji Ministry's Policy on Free Educational Resources lists *English Communication Year 2* among English resources supplied by the Curriculum Development Unit. A separate 2021 Year 2 literacy worksheet hosted by Telecom Fiji is a practice reference only. The Ministry's 2020–2021 Annual Report records Y1–Y8 literacy/numeracy syllabus changes and work on scope-and-sequence overviews and teacher guides. These references establish useful source leads, not a complete current Class 2 indicator map. Obtain the school's currently authorised Year 2 English/Language, Literacy and Communication syllabus or teacher guide; record exact edition and page before marking any activity verified.

## Differentiation rules
- **Copper:** adult models each step; two-choice options, picture/tactile cues, word/picture bank, shared reading and ample wait time. Accept pointing, sign, AAC, gesture, oral response, drawing or adult scribing.
- **Bronze:** one worked example, visual prompt and guided practice; gradually reduce prompts.
- **Silver:** complete the shared task with minimal prompts and use familiar taught vocabulary/patterns independently.
- **Gold:** extend the same objective through an explanation, comparison, extra detail, independent revision or alternative example. Do not introduce untaught phonics patterns as assumed knowledge.
- Do not score accent or disability-related speech differences as errors. Do not force speech. Match assessment mode to the skill being assessed.

## Daily activities

### Term 1 — Week 1 (programme Week 1): Myself and my community

**Weekly learning intention:** Introduce self; ask and answer simple questions; recognise names and familiar words.

#### Day 1 — Listen and speak
**Shared objective:** Introduce self; ask and answer simple questions; recognise names and familiar words.

**Teacher-led activity:** Teacher models a short exchange connected to the weekly theme. Learners listen, take turns and respond to a question using a complete idea.
**PacEdu working achievement indicator:** The learner can listen, take turns and communicate a relevant idea about Myself and my community, showing meaning through an accurate oral, signed, AAC, drawn, dictated or written response.

**Worked example:** Teacher models one theme-related example aloud and visually, thinks through the steps, then completes a second example with the class. Select words, text and spelling patterns already taught in the authorised school sequence.

**Copper:** Complete the task with adult modelling, two-choice prompts and picture/tactile supports; learner may point, sign, use AAC, gesture, draw or dictate.

**Bronze:** Complete a parallel example using a word bank, visual cue and one guided step; adult reduces prompts where possible.

**Silver:** Complete the shared task independently using familiar vocabulary and taught spelling patterns; reread or explain the response.

**Gold:** Extend the same task with a reason, comparison, extra relevant detail or independent revision; do not move to a different learning objective.

**Evidence / achievement check:** Record what the learner actually did (for example, took a turn, located a detail, sorted words by a taught feature, sequenced steps or produced a meaningful written response). Mark `PENDING_TEACHER_REVIEW` until reviewed.
