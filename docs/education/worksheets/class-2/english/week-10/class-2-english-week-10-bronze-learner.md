## Learning focus
I can find nouns (naming words), find verbs (action words), sort words, and use nouns and verbs to make meaningful sentences.

## Read this story
After school, Jone visited the garden with his sister. They carried a small watering can. Jone filled it with water. His sister planted two flowers. A bee landed on one flower. The children watched it and smiled.

**Word bank:** Jone • sister • garden • watering can • water • flowers • bee • visited • carried • filled • planted • landed • watched • smiled

## Worked example
In “The bird sings,” **bird** is a noun because it names an animal. **sings** is a verb because it tells what the bird does. The words make a meaningful sentence.
