## Read or listen to the whole passage

> During reading time, Litia visits the school library with her class. She chooses a book about sea animals. The librarian, Mrs Rao, explains that the book must be returned on Friday. Litia reads the first page with her friend Saki.

**Remember:** “Who” asks about a person. “What” asks about a thing or action. “Where” asks about a place. “When” asks about a time. Listen to the whole question and give an answer that matches it.

## Worked example

Question: When must the book be returned? Answer: On Friday. I notice the question word “when”, then locate the time information in the passage.
