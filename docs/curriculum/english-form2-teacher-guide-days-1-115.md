# PacEdu English — Form 2 Teacher Guide and Marking Notes (Days 1–115)

**Status:** PENDING_TEACHER_REVIEW and PENDING_OFFICIAL_INDICATOR_VERIFICATION. Original PacEdu material; not a Ministry-issued scheme or worksheet.
**Use:** Same daily learning goal for Copper, Bronze, Silver and Gold. Adjust access and scaffolding, not the learner's entitlement to the objective. Accept equivalent correct wording and accessible response modes. Mark factual comprehension against the passage; for open responses, credit a defensible interpretation supported by evidence.

## Day 1 — Evaluating community information

**Daily objective:** The learner can identify the central idea and select accurate details that support it.
**Key content:** The Notice on the Board. Main information: A notice on a community board announces a meeting about water supply. It includes a date, venue, organiser and contact details. A second copy shared online leaves out the date and adds an unverified claim. Students compare both versions and discuss why readers should check the complete notice before acting.
**Marking guide:** Award credit for accurate details drawn from the passage, clear explanation and completion of the stated task. Accept equivalent wording. Do not require a single phrasing for inference or creative responses; require textual evidence where the question asks for it.
**Pathway evidence:** Copper — accurate response with model/choice support; Bronze — response using a prompt or frame; Silver — independent relevant response; Gold — precise, justified or extended response. These are task supports, not fixed learner labels.

**Daily objective:** The learner can review a response for accuracy, cohesion and purpose, then apply the learning to a new situation.
**Key content:** Evidence of Growth. Main information: A learner reviews work from three terms and selects examples that show improvement in reading, writing and speaking. The learner annotates each selection with the skill demonstrated and a next goal. The reflection distinguishes effort, progress and final achievement rather than treating them as the same thing.
**Marking guide:** Award credit for accurate details drawn from the passage, clear explanation and completion of the stated task. Accept equivalent wording. Do not require a single phrasing for inference or creative responses; require textual evidence where the question asks for it.
**Pathway evidence:** Copper — accurate response with model/choice support; Bronze — response using a prompt or frame; Silver — independent relevant response; Gold — precise, justified or extended response. These are task supports, not fixed learner labels.
