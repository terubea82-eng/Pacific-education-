## Read or listen to the complete passage
Lani has a cat. The cat is black. It sits on a mat.

The teacher may read the passage aloud. You may read it yourself or use accessible print, tactile, pointing, sign, AAC, drawing or dictated responses. You do not have to speak aloud.

## Learning goal
Answer who, what, where and when questions by finding information stated in the passage.

## Worked example
Question: “What animal is in the story?”
Step 1: Look or listen for the animal word.
Step 2: Find “cat”.
Answer: “A cat.” This is a literal answer because the passage says it.

## Your questions
1. WHO has a cat? Choose: Lani / Sera.
2. WHAT animal is in the story? Choose: cat / dog.
3. WHERE does the cat sit? Choose: mat / tree.
4. WHAT colour is the cat? Choose: black / white.
