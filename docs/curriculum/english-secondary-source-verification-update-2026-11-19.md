## New evidence located

### 1. Revised Secondary English Curriculum (RSEC), Years 9–10

A copy of the Ministry of Education Curriculum Advisory Services *Revised Secondary English Curriculum — Teachers Guide* identifies:
- Three strands: Language, Text Types and Critical Literacy.
- Year-specific classroom learning objectives (KUDs).
- Achievement Standards and Content Learning Outcomes (CLOs).
- Model units, lesson plans, Daily Text Engagement (DTE), formative Dry Run Tasks, summative Performance Tasks and assessment rubrics.
- Differentiated learning and reading support.

The copy located is hosted by FlipHTML5, not on the Ministry's own domain. It identifies Ministry Curriculum Advisory Services copyright and a 2024 guide, but is retained as a **secondary-hosted copy pending confirmation against the current school-authorised original**:
https://fliphtml5.com/gjgbb/naau/RSEC_Teachers_Guide_Year_9_%26_10/

A copy of Ministry Circular No. 19-25, dated 27 January 2025, surfaced on Studocu. Its extracted text states that:
- Year 9 RSEC national rollout commenced Term 1, 2025.
- 2024 pilot/Batch 1 schools continued into Year 10 using RSEC in 2025.
- 2024 Batch 2 schools continued into Year 10 using the prior MOE Year 10 English curriculum in 2025.
- Year 9/10 RSEC used five units and internal Performance Tasks rather than short-test examinations.
- Years 11–13 literature coverage and Years 12–13 examination/project requirements were treated separately.

This is a **third-party copy of a Ministry circular**, not the original official circular/annexes. It is strong evidence of 2025 rollout arrangements but does not prove each school's 2026 cohort implementation:
https://www.studocu.com/row/document/nakawa-vocational-training-college/english/circular-no-19-25-2025-secondary-english-curriculum/117881613

### 2. Years 11–13

The 2025 circular copy indicates that Years 11–13 were not simply moved into the Years 9–10 RSEC sequence. It refers to separate literature coverage, writing styles, marking criteria, oral communication assessment and Year 12/13 external examination/project requirements.

Therefore:
- Do not apply the Years 9–10 RSEC model wholesale to Forms 5–7.
- Obtain the 2026 authorised Year 11, Year 12 and Year 13 English syllabi, current assessment/examination instructions, prescribed literature lists, project booklets and marking rubrics separately.
- The 2023 South Pacific Form Seven Certificate English syllabus is an official EQAP document but is titled 2023 and must not be assumed current for 2026:
https://eqap.spc.int/sites/default/files/resources/2024-05/SPFSC%20English%20Syllabus_2023-2025_Final_EQAP_SR%20Checked.pdf

### 3. Curriculum approval authority — legal update requiring care

The Fiji Parliament-hosted *Education Act 2026* (Act 5 of 2026) contains Part 5 on Curriculum Development and Assessment and section 23, which states that registered and recognised schools must meet curriculum, assessment and educational standards developed, designed and approved by the Authority. This is a material legal source for current curriculum governance:
https://www.parliament.gov.fj/wp-content/uploads/2026/06/Act-5-Education-Act-2026.pdf

Before using it as the operative approval process, verify its commencement date, any transitional provisions, regulations and the identity/current role of the Authority. Do not infer commencement or supersession solely from the Act's publication.

## Verification record protocol

For every level, the reviewer must record:
1. Official issuing authority and exact document title.
2. Edition/year and effective school year.
3. Exact school level/year and cohort applicability.
4. Exact indicator, Achievement Standard, CLO or assessment outcome wording.
5. Page/section reference.
6. Whether the source is the official original or a third-party copy.
7. Verification date and reviewer role.
8. Any difference between the source requirement and PacEdu's proposed activity.

Until those details are captured, keep `PENDING_OFFICIAL_INDICATOR_VERIFICATION`. User review is `USER_REVIEWED`, not independent source verification.
