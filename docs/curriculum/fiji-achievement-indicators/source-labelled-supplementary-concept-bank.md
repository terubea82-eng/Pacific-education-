# Source-labelled supplementary concept bank

**Status:** REFERENCE ONLY — NOT THE ACTIVE FIJI PRESCRIPTION-INDICATOR BANK  
**Review date:** 12 November 2026  
**Coverage goal:** ECE through Year 13 / Form 7  
**Purpose:** Preserve useful concepts and learning-language found in curriculum-related materials while keeping every record labelled by source, authority, date, status and permission. These records may support later worksheet design only after local relevance, age/level fit, curriculum alignment and reuse permission are checked.

## Mandatory separation rules

1. **PRESCRIBED_INDICATOR** is reserved for exact wording and codes from a current, formally authorised Fiji prescription confirmed by CAS or an equivalent controlled Ministry source. No such records are added in this file.
2. **OFFICIAL_REFERENCE_OUTCOME** records official Ministry reference/guideline outcomes that are not proven to be mandatory current prescriptions.
3. **CURRICULUM_GUIDE_CONCEPT** records concepts, strands, text types, skills or teaching routines found in curriculum-related guides.
4. **HISTORICAL_OR_TRIAL_LEAD** records old, trial, draft or third-party-hosted materials that require current CAS verification.
5. **SUPPLEMENTARY_INTERNATIONAL_RESOURCE** records external/regional materials that can inspire supplementary learning only; never represent them as Fiji-mandated curriculum.
6. Every concept must retain its source URL, source title, publisher/authority, year/version if known, page/section, level/subject scope, copyright/reuse status, and verification state.
7. Do not silently rewrite a source concept as an indicator. Preserve exact quoted wording only where brief and necessary; otherwise paraphrase and label it as a concept.
8. Do not generate a daily worksheet from a reference-only record as though it were prescribed. A worksheet may be labelled **supplementary revision** only after a teacher validates relevance and rights. Official daily curriculum worksheets remain blocked until the prescribed indicator bank is populated and verified.

## Recommended PacEdu data model

Keep separate tables/files rather than a single mixed indicator list:

- `prescription_sources`: controlled source metadata, approval/effective dates, amendments, copyright, provenance.
- `prescribed_indicators`: exact official code, wording, subject, year/form, strand, source page, approval state.
- `reference_outcomes`: official guideline/framework outcomes not verified as mandatory prescriptions.
- `supplementary_concepts`: concepts from guides, textbooks and regional materials, with explicit non-prescription status.
- `activity_mappings`: worksheet/activity ID linked to one or more source records, with relationship type (direct prescribed, teacher-designed elaboration, supplementary revision), reviewer and rights check.
- `worksheet_versions`: student copy, teacher answer/marking guide, ability scaffold, accessibility version, date and revision history.

Never store a third-party concept as a prescribed indicator. Never allow a worksheet to lose its source labels when exported, printed, scanned or marked.

## Worksheet creation gate

- **Prescribed daily worksheet:** blocked until the exact current Fiji prescription and its scope/sequence/contact hours are verified; map one or more exact indicators and teacher-reviewed lesson objectives.
- **Supplementary revision worksheet:** may be drafted separately from a labelled concept after level fit, teacher review, and copyright/reuse check; visibly mark “SUPPLEMENTARY — NOT A SUBSTITUTE FOR THE FIJI PRESCRIPTION”.
- Keep Copper, Bronze, Silver and Gold scaffolds on the same validated learning objective; do not alter the prescribed objective between ability groups.
- Include worked example, clear student instructions, A/B/C/D questions where appropriate, accessible text/audio support, answer key and teacher marking notes. Keep student-facing answers hidden until marking/review.
- Record parent/home assessment separately from official teacher assessment; do not overwrite school records.
- Keep assessment review state `PENDING_TEACHER_REVIEW` until an authorised teacher validates it.
