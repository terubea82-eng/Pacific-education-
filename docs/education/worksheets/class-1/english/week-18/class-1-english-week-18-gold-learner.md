## Read or listen to the whole passage

> On Wednesday afternoon, the Class 1 group visits the community garden with their teacher, Mr Kumar. They observe bean plants growing beside the fence. Ana measures one plant with a ruler and records that it is twelve centimetres tall. Before returning to class, the learners ask the gardener, Mere, how often the plants need water. She explains that the soil should be checked each morning and watered when it feels dry.

**Remember:** “Who” asks about a person. “What” asks about a thing or action. “Where” asks about a place. “When” asks about a time. Listen to the whole question and give an answer that matches it.

## Worked example

Question: Why do the learners check the soil before watering? Answer: To see whether it feels dry and needs water. I connect the question to the relevant information in the final sentence and answer in a complete response.
