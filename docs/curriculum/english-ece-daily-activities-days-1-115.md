# PacEdu English Subject Box — ECE Daily Activities (Days 1–115)

**Placement:** English → ECE → Term → Week → Day → activity and teacher evidence. This pack belongs inside the English Subject Box; it is not a separate learner subject.

**Status:** ORIGINAL PACEDU PRACTICE DRAFT — PENDING TEACHER REVIEW. The sequence is a 23-week PacEdu planning distribution (Term 1: 9 weeks; Term 2: 9 weeks; Term 3: 5 weeks), not an official Fiji Ministry timetable. Do not assign official achievement-indicator codes from this file. A Fiji-qualified ECE/English educator must map each activity to the current authorised ECE curriculum and record source edition, strand/sub-strand, exact outcome/indicator, page and reviewer before claiming alignment.

**ECE approach:** Play-based, child-centred, observation-led and developmentally appropriate. No long written worksheets, timed tests, forced speech, or formal phonics/grammar drills. Accept speech, home-language contribution, sign, gesture, pointing, object choice, AAC, movement, drawing, mark-making and adult-scribed words as appropriate. Respect each child's language and communication needs.

**Four pathways:** Copper, Bronze, Silver and Gold are flexible support/challenge pathways, not fixed labels. All groups share the same weekly learning intention. Adjust access, prompting, complexity and independence—not the child's worth or expected participation.

**Daily record:** Date (school calendar): ______ | Week: ___ | Day: ___ | Learning day: ___ | Teacher: ______ | Evidence / next step: ______. Dates are intentionally blank until the school's calendar is connected.

## Ability pathway guide

- **Copper:** Adult models first; use one object or picture at a time, two clear choices, repetition, tactile/large-print access, gesture/sign/AAC, and generous wait time. Child may point, match, move, vocalise, sign or draw.
- **Bronze:** Use a clear visual cue and one worked example; offer a small word/picture bank and a familiar partner; invite the child to complete the next similar step.
- **Silver:** Invite the child to complete the core play-based task with minimal prompting, use relevant vocabulary and communicate a simple reason or sequence.
- **Gold:** Extend the same concept through a new example, a child-created question, a longer retell, a comparison or an independent explanation; never add inappropriate formal written demands.

## 115-day activity sequence

### Term 1 — Week 1 (overall Week 1): My name, identity and belonging

**Shared learning intention:** Recognise and communicate own name; listen to and respond to familiar names.

**Context/resources:** Name cards, mirrors, family-approved identity words. Use familiar, safe Fiji home, school and community contexts without assuming every child has the same family structure, culture or experience.

#### Day 1 — Meet, notice and connect

**Teacher activity:** Introduce the weekly idea through a welcoming circle and a short play invitation. Welcome each child by name; invite them to find, point to, say or show their name card.

**Child activity:** Choose or match own name/photo card; communicate one thing about self.
**PacEdu working achievement indicator:** The child can choose, match or communicate one familiar idea about My name, identity and belonging, using an individual communication or play-based response.

**Copper:** Adult models the action; child responds by looking, touching, matching, moving, choosing or using their preferred communication.

**Bronze:** Child completes the same action with a visual cue, repeated key words and one example.

**Silver:** Child completes the action with minimal prompting and communicates one detail.

**Gold:** Child extends the action with a new object/person/example and explains or demonstrates the connection.

**Teacher prompts:** “Show me…”, “What did you notice?”, “Can you find another?”, “What happened next?” Use only prompts suited to the child and the week's focus.

**Evidence:** Observe response to name and preferred communication mode. Record what the child did, the support provided and the communication mode; do not score accent, speech fluency or handwriting unless that is the explicit approved learning focus.

#### Day 2 — Listen and explore

**Teacher activity:** Build shared understanding through sensory exploration and repeated language. Model the week's key words/concept with real objects, pictures, gestures and a short oral model.

**Child activity:** Explore the objects/pictures; listen, point, match, move or respond to a simple prompt.
**PacEdu working achievement indicator:** The child can explore and identify a sound, object, picture or word connected with My name, identity and belonging, using an individual communication or play-based response.

## End-of-programme evidence review

Review the child's evidence across the whole period, not one isolated performance:

- Listening and responding: attends, responds, follows familiar directions and participates in turn-taking in an individual way.
- Speaking and communicating: shares meaning through an appropriate communication mode, describes, chooses, asks or responds.
- Becoming a reader: engages with books, pictures, stories, meaningful print, rhyme and repeated language.
- Becoming a writer: uses drawing, marks, symbols, name attempts or dictated/scribed messages to communicate meaning.
- Participation and access: record the environment, materials, language and communication supports that enabled participation.

**Teacher decision:** ☐ continue current support ☐ introduce a new example ☐ provide additional small-group experience ☐ discuss access needs with family/specialist ☐ map to verified official outcome.

**Answer/marking policy:** ECE activities are observed and documented, not marked with a generic numeric test score. Any teacher prompt or assistance must be recorded. No child is failed for choosing a non-verbal response.
