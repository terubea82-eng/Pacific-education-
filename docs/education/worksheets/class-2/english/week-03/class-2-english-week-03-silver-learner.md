## What I am learning
I am learning to notice, sort, read and build words using sound and spelling patterns that my class has already been taught. I will not be expected to use a new or untaught pattern.

## How to take part
Attempt each task independently first. Ask for a direction to be repeated if needed. All sound and spelling analysis must use patterns already taught in class.

## Read or listen to this complete passage

Mia has a red bag. She puts a pen in the bag. The bag is on a mat. Mia gets the bag and sits with her class.

**Word bank:** bag · red · pen · mat · gets · sits · class
