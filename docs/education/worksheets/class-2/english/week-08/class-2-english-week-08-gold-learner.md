## Read or listen to this passage

THE HAT BESIDE THE BAG
Lani set her red hat on a bench outside the classroom before joining a game at break time. In her hurry, she left it behind. After the bell, she returned and noticed that the bench was empty. Rather than guessing where it had gone, she searched carefully: first underneath the bench, then around the bags nearby. She spotted the hat beside her friend's school bag. Her friend explained that the hat had slipped from the bench and had been placed safely out of the way. Lani thanked her friend and carried the hat back to class.
