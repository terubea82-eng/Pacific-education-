# Pacific Education — Operational to Legal Transition Framework

## Purpose
Prepare Pacific Education documentation and controls in advance so practical implementation can later transition into formal legal, regulatory, contractual and professional procedures without treating planning documents as legal approval.

## Operating principle
Documentation may prepare requirements, evidence, workflows and decision points. It must not represent itself as legal advice, legal compliance, Ministry approval, professional certification, production authorization or a binding contract.

## Lifecycle
1. Design and documentation
2. Prototype validation
3. Controlled pilot
4. Practical implementation and evidence collection
5. Specialist review
6. Legal/regulatory review against the current applicable requirements
7. Contract, policy and consent finalization where required
8. Owner approval
9. Production release authorization
10. Controlled launch
11. Continuous monitoring, incident response, review and change control

## Automatic transition rule
When a practical requirement is marked VERIFIED by its authorized reviewer, Pacific Guardian may automatically prepare the next documentation package, checklist update, evidence index, review request or controlled pull request when the relevant stage is allowlisted.

Guardian must not automatically convert a practical record into a legal conclusion. Any legal, regulatory, child-safeguarding, privacy, education-sector, payment, employment, contractual or professional requirement must be routed to the appropriate authorized professional/reviewer before it can be treated as satisfied.

## Evidence rule
A requirement is complete only when:
- the required implementation exists;
- objective evidence is recorded;
- the evidence is independently verified where required;
- required specialist/legal/authorized review is approved;
- blocking defects are resolved or formally dispositioned;
- the applicable owner approval is recorded.

## Change control
Every transition records stage, requirement, evidence reference, reviewer, date/time, change class, affected files, decision status and release status. No secret, credential, payment credential or unnecessary child personal information belongs in the repository.

## Fail-closed conditions
Missing evidence, ambiguous authority, failed tests, unresolved critical defects, missing required professional review or uncertain legal applicability keeps the requirement OPEN/INCOMPLETE.

## Ownership
Pacific Education remains owner-controlled. Technical services, funding, infrastructure, support, distribution or review do not by themselves create ownership, equity, partnership or control.

## Important limitation
This framework is an operational governance specification. Applicable Fiji law, regulations, education requirements, privacy requirements, child-safeguarding requirements, contracts and professional advice must be checked against the current requirements before production launch.
