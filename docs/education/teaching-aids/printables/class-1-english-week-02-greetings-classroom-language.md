# PacEdu Class 1 English — Printable Teaching-Aid Pack
## Programme Week 2 · Days 6–10 · Greetings and classroom language

**Status:** Original PacEdu printable master; pending teacher review. Not an official Ministry resource. Exact Fiji curriculum outcome/indicator remains `PENDING_VERIFICATION`.

**Shared learning intention:** Use a suitable greeting, polite request or classroom expression in a meaningful exchange.

**Teacher preparation:** Print the prompt cards below. Add safe, familiar classroom objects (book, pencil, ruler, bag). Describe every symbol/card aloud. Offer large print, tactile markers, signed/AAC versions and extra response time. Learners may speak, sign, point, use AAC, draw, dictate or pass. Do not require public speaking.

---

## Day 6 — Listen and notice: identify a greeting or classroom expression

**Teacher model:** Present one expression at a time in a real context. Model “Hello” when greeting someone and “Thank you” after receiving an item.

**Aid 6A — Expression cards**

**HELLO**

**GOOD MORNING**

**GOOD AFTERNOON**

**GOODBYE**

**PLEASE**

**THANK YOU**

**HELP, PLEASE**

**MAY I HAVE A PENCIL, PLEASE?**

**Learner task:** Match an expression card to a simple teacher-described situation. The teacher reads/describes each card; learners may point, match, sign or use AAC.

**Worked example:** “Someone gives me a book. Which words can I use?” Model selecting “Thank you.”

**Copper:** Choose between two cards with adult modelling.  
**Bronze:** Choose from three cards using a situation cue.  
**Silver:** Select a suitable expression from the full set.  
**Gold:** Explain why an expression fits a situation and offer another suitable expression.

**Evidence:** Correct/suitable selection, response mode and prompt level.

## Day 7 — Model and explore: make a polite request

**Teacher model:** Hold up a pencil and model: “May I have a pencil, please?” Then model “Thank you” after receiving it.

**Aid 7A — Request frame**

**MAY I HAVE A __________________, PLEASE?**

**THANK YOU.**

**Aid 7B — Object word cards**

**BOOK** · **PENCIL** · **RULER** · **PAPER** · **HELP**

**Learner task:** Choose an object card and complete the request orally, by sign/AAC, pointing with a partner, or using the printed frame.

**Worked example:** Select BOOK; place/read it in the blank: “May I have a book, please?” The teacher responds by handing over the safe classroom book and models “Thank you.”

**Copper:** Choose one of two objects; adult models the whole request and accepts pointing/AAC.  
**Bronze:** Use the request frame and a two- or three-word card bank.  
**Silver:** Complete a request with a suitable object word and respond appropriately.  
**Gold:** Make a second request using a different object or ask for help politely.

**Evidence:** Request communicates the intended need; note the support provided.

---

## Day 8 — Practise together: take turns in a greeting exchange

**Teacher model:** Demonstrate a short exchange with a partner. Allow learners to choose whether to speak, sign, use AAC, point to a card or observe.

**Aid 8A — Exchange strip**

**PERSON A:** HELLO.

**PERSON B:** HELLO.

**PERSON A:** HOW ARE YOU?

**PERSON B:** I AM __________________.

**PERSON A:** THANK YOU.

**Aid 8B — Response choices**

**I AM WELL.**

**I AM HAPPY.**

**I AM OKAY.**

**I WOULD LIKE TO PASS.**

**Learner task:** Practise a two-turn or longer exchange with a partner, using any accessible communication mode. “Pass” is a valid choice.

**Worked example:** Teacher A selects HELLO; Teacher B selects HELLO; Teacher A selects HOW ARE YOU?; Teacher B selects I AM OKAY.

**Copper:** Adult-supported one-turn greeting or response; learner may pass.  
**Bronze:** Use the exchange strip and response choices.  
**Silver:** Complete the exchange with fewer prompts.  
**Gold:** Adapt the exchange for morning, afternoon or goodbye and explain the choice.

**Evidence:** Turn-taking and meaningful communication, not accent or volume.

## Day 9 — Apply independently: choose an expression for a classroom situation

**Teacher model:** Read/describe a situation and think aloud about the expression that would help.

**Aid 9A — Situation cards**

**I CANNOT FIND MY BOOK.**

**I NEED HELP WITH MY WORK.**

**SOMEONE SHARES A PENCIL WITH ME.**

**I AM LEAVING THE CLASSROOM.**

**I ARRIVE AT SCHOOL IN THE MORNING.**

**Aid 9B — My choice record**

| Situation | My expression / card |
|---|---|
| I need help. | |
| Someone helps me. | |
| I arrive in the morning. | |

**Learner task:** Choose an expression for each situation. Learners may match cards rather than write.

**Worked example:** Situation: “I need help with my work.” Select “Help, please.”

**Copper:** Work with one situation and two expression choices.  
**Bronze:** Complete two situations with a word bank.  
**Silver:** Match all situations to suitable expressions.  
**Gold:** Create a new classroom situation and select or compose an appropriate expression.

**Evidence:** Suitability of selected expression and degree of independence.

## Day 10 — Review and demonstrate: classroom communication mini-book

**Teacher model:** Demonstrate selecting one greeting, one request and one polite response from the cards.

**Aid 10A — My classroom words mini-book**

**PAGE 1 — A GREETING I CAN USE**

My card/words: __________________________________

**PAGE 2 — A REQUEST I CAN USE**

My card/words: __________________________________

**PAGE 3 — A POLITE RESPONSE I CAN USE**

My card/words: __________________________________

**PAGE 4 — HOW I COMMUNICATE**

□ speech  □ sign  □ AAC  □ pointing  
□ picture/card  □ drawing  □ writing  □ another way

**Aid 10B — Teacher evidence**

**Learner task:** Select one greeting, one request and one response for the mini-book. Learner may choose cards, dictate, sign, use AAC or write.

**Worked example:** Select HELLO; MAY I HAVE A BOOK, PLEASE?; THANK YOU. Teacher records the learner's chosen communication mode.

**Copper:** Select one card for each page from two options.  
**Bronze:** Complete the mini-book with prompt cards and a small word bank.  
**Silver:** Select suitable expressions independently.  
**Gold:** Add a new classroom expression and explain when it is useful.

**Evidence:** Mini-book selections, communication mode, prompt level and next step.

## Reusable prompt cards

**I WOULD LIKE TO PASS** — I choose not to answer now.
