# PacEdu Teacher-Only Marking Guide — Class 1 English
## Week 5 · Days 21–25 · Copper pathway

**Matched learner worksheet:** class-1-english-week-05-copper-days-21-25.md
**Learning intention:** Recognise S/s and A/a through matching and supported familiar-word search.

**Fair evidence:** Accept pointing, matching, tactile selection, sign, AAC, gesture, tracing, dictation or writing. Record prompt level. Do not penalise speech or handwriting.

### Day 21
- Correct S/s identification: capital S and small s.
- Correct A/a identification: capital A and small a.
- Credit matching one target letter to its identical form.

### Day 22
- S matches s; A matches a.
- Accept card placement, tracing or selection.

### Day 23
- Use only words already taught in this class.
- In the sample **Sam**, target S/s is initial; in **sat**, S/s is initial and A/a is medial. Use these only if the words and all required correspondences have been taught.
- If not taught, substitute an approved familiar word or assess letter matching only.

### Day 24
- S and a selections should be accurate.
- Short-a response is assessed only if it has been taught; otherwise record “not yet taught.”

### Day 25
- Credit S–s and A–a matching and one correct target-letter identification in a familiar taught word.
