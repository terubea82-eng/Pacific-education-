## Learning intention and pathway support
Follow two- and three-step oral instructions, give a clear instruction, ask/answer questions and retell steps in order.

Use the full instruction, a three-picture sequence and one worked example. Repeat and reduce prompts gradually.

## Complete practice message
On Monday, the teacher gives Class 2 three directions for a reading activity: take one book from the shelf, sit on the mat, and open the book at the first page. The class listens before beginning. After reading, learners place the book back on the return trolley.

## Worked example and indicative answers
Model: “Take one book from the shelf, then sit on the mat.” Demonstrate both actions in order.

- Day 6: Accept an accurate response to the first simple direction; use an actual book/object or picture.
- Day 7: Expected sequence: take one book from the shelf; sit on the mat; open the book at the first page.
- Day 8: Credit a clear instruction containing an action and object/place; the learner may dictate, sign, use AAC or arrange symbols.
- Day 9: Accept relevant questions about the people, objects, place or actions in the message.
- Day 10: Accept an ordered retell of the three actions. For Gold, credit a practical three-step instruction and an appropriate check for understanding.

## Marking and accessibility
- Assess understanding and clarity, not accent, speech production, handwriting or speed.
- Give processing time and repeat the instruction where this is an agreed access support; record the support.
- Use real objects, pictures, symbols, tactile cues, sign, AAC or speech as appropriate.
- Copper: one action at a time, adult model, reduced choices and generous wait time.
- Bronze: visual sequence and guided practice; reduce prompts gradually.
- Silver: three-step sequence with minimal prompting.
- Gold: clear independent instruction and a meaningful check for understanding.
- Accept valid equivalent responses; do not require one exact phrase.
- Record prompt level: independent / with prompt / with model / not yet observed.
- Pathway is adjustable and must not become a permanent learner label.
