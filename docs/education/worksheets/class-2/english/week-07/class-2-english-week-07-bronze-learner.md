A BIRD IN THE TREE
A little bird sits in a tree.
It sings a bright song.
The sun warms the leaves.
A soft wind moves the branches.
The bird looks down at the garden.

## Worked example
**Task:** Read one sentence slowly, then read it again smoothly.

**Example:** First, I read the words carefully. Next, I join the words into meaningful groups and pause at the full stop. Smooth reading is clear and meaningful; it does not mean rushing.
