# Class 2 Mathematics — four ability worksheets

Status: **original pilot material; official Fiji syllabus outcomes not yet mapped or teacher-verified.** Confirm the current Class/Year 2 Mathematics syllabus and taught content before release.

Suggested instructional bands: Support 0–49%, Developing 50–64%, Expected 65–79%, Advanced 80–100%. Use evidence for Mathematics specifically; bands are flexible and can change.

## Worksheet A — Support (0–49%)

**Focus:** Count, compare, add, subtract, and find a missing number.

**Example:** 3 + 2 = 5. Count three dots, then two more.

1. Write the number: ● ● ● ● = ___
2. Which is greater: 7 or 4?
3. 5 + 3 = ___
4. 9 − 2 = ___
5. Fill the gap: 4, 5, ___, 7.
6. Ana has 3 coconuts. Her uncle gives her 2 more. How many does she have now?

**Teacher key (10 marks):** Q1 4 (1); Q2 7 (1); Q3 8 (2); Q4 7 (2); Q5 6 (2); Q6 5 (2).

**Next step:** Use counters, fingers, or drawings, then explain how you counted.

## Worksheet B — Developing (50–64%)

**Focus:** Add and subtract within 100 using place value and solve everyday problems.

**Example:** 24 + 10 = 34 because one ten is added.

1. 16 + 7 = ___
2. 32 − 8 = ___
3. Write 47 as ___ tens and ___ ones.
4. Fill the gap: 20, 25, 30, ___, ___.
5. A market seller has 18 bananas and sells 6. How many are left?
6. There are 12 learners on one mat and 9 on another. How many learners altogether?

**Teacher key (10 marks):** Q1 23 (2); Q2 24 (2); Q3 4 tens and 7 ones (2); Q4 35, 40 (1); Q5 12 (1); Q6 21 (2).

**Next step:** Show a number sentence and explain the operation used.

## Worksheet C — Expected (65–79%)

**Focus:** Use number facts, patterns, and two-step word problems.

**Example:** 15 − 4 + 3 = 14. First subtract 4, then add 3.

1. 28 + 17 = ___
2. 54 − 26 = ___
3. Complete: 6, 12, 18, ___, ___.
4. A class collected 24 cans on Monday and 19 on Tuesday. They recycled 15. How many cans remain to be recycled?
5. Write two different addition sentences that equal 20.
6. Explain why 38 + 9 is the same as 38 + 10 − 1.

**Teacher key (12 marks):** Q1 45 (2); Q2 28 (2); Q3 24, 30 (2); Q4 28 (2); Q5 any two valid equations (2); Q6 explanation of adding ten then subtracting one (2).

**Next step:** Choose an efficient method and check the answer using the inverse operation or another strategy.

## Worksheet D — Advanced (80–100%)

**Focus:** Multi-step reasoning, explain a method, and create a problem.

**Example:** A team has 26 shells and finds 18 more. It gives 9 shells to another team: 26 + 18 − 9 = 35.

1. A school garden has 4 rows with 6 seedlings in each row. How many seedlings are there?
2. The class plants 8 more seedlings. How many seedlings are there now?
3. They give 7 seedlings to another class. How many remain?
4. Find the missing number: ___ + 27 = 63.
5. A learner says 46 − 19 = 33. Explain the error and give the correct answer.
6. Create a two-step story problem using numbers between 10 and 50. Solve it and show both steps.

**Teacher key (12 marks):** Q1 24 (2); Q2 32 (2); Q3 25 (2); Q4 36 (2); Q5 correct answer 27 with a clear explanation (2); Q6 valid two-step problem and correct solution (2).

**Next step:** Explain why the method works and check that the answer is reasonable.

## Accessibility and delivery

- Use counters, raised/tactile marks, large print, or screen-reader-friendly linear equations as appropriate.
- Read word problems aloud when reading is not the skill being assessed; also provide written text.
- Do not rely on color, sound, or a phone camera to solve any question.
- Accept an equivalent correct strategy. Keep answers teacher-only until approved.

**Teacher release check:** Verify the current Fiji Mathematics syllabus outcome and number range taught; adjust if necessary; keep the answer key in the teacher workspace; approve before student release.
