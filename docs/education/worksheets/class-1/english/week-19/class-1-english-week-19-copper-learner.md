## My own prompt — read or listen
Picture or object prompt: a child places a red ball in a basket. If a picture is not available, the teacher may arrange a real ball and basket or describe the scene.

**Think about the idea:**

Who? a child. What? puts. What object? a red ball. Where? in a basket.

## Worked example

The sentence tells an idea. It starts with a capital letter, has spaces between words and ends with a full stop. Your teacher may point out each part while reading it aloud or showing it.
