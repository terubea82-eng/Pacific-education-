# PacEdu · Class 1 English · Week 3
## Listening carefully and following directions · Days 11–15

**Programme:** Term 1 · Week 3 · Days 11–15 of 115  
**Learning intention:** Listen for key words, understand order words, and follow familiar one- and two-step spoken directions.  
**Curriculum status:** PENDING_TEACHER_REVIEW / NOT_YET_MAPPED. This is original PacEdu practice material, not an official Ministry-issued worksheet. Teacher must verify the applicable current Fiji curriculum outcome, source edition and page before learner release.

**Access note:** The teacher or support person reads every task aloud. Learners may respond by pointing, placing objects, signing, using AAC, drawing, dictating or writing. Do not score accent, speech clarity, hearing status, disability-related communication, or handwriting unless that is the stated skill. Check the learner's preferred access method and hearing/vision supports before starting.

**Materials:** 2 familiar classroom objects (for example, pencil and book), 3 picture/object cards, a 3-box sequence strip, crayons or counters. Use safe, familiar objects; do not use small items that present a choking risk.
