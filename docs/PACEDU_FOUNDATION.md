# Pacedu Foundation — Teaching Experience and Research-Informed Architecture

## Locked foundation

Pacific Education (Pacedu) was primarily conceived from the Owner's 21 years of practical experience as a primary-school teacher.

That classroom experience is the foundation of the system. Pacedu is intended to reduce unnecessary teacher workload, help teachers understand learners better, identify learner strengths and areas requiring development, and turn daily learning activities into meaningful evidence of skills and progress.

## Real-life education principle

Pacedu is designed around the principle that education should connect with real life. Daily activities are therefore intended to reflect practical classroom experience, learner needs, observation, practice, application, and real-life situations rather than treating education as isolated information.

## Locked educational architecture

The core learning pathway is:

**Registration → Individual Workspace → Class/Subject → Daily Activities → Learner Responses → Skills/Evidence → Teacher Review → Targeted Support → Progress → Assessment**

The architecture is designed so that daily learning can help reveal what a learner can already do, where the learner needs support, and what should be strengthened next.

## Teacher-support principle

Pacedu is designed to assist teachers, not replace teachers. Technology should reduce repetitive workload, organize learning evidence, support accessibility, and help teachers make better-informed professional decisions.

Teacher professional judgment remains central to review, intervention, approval, assessment, and learner support.

## Voice and listener accessibility

The mandatory Pacedu welcome experience must make this foundation available to listeners as well as readers.

The protected voice architecture must preserve:

- the #856 voice baseline;
- two-person AI Playback with distinct voices;
- automatic welcome/voice attempt;
- manual **Hear Welcome & Introduction**;
- **Stop Speech**;
- top-right Play instruction;
- accessibility speaker controls;
- voice navigation support;
- voice availability through the guided learning flow and user workspace where supported.

The listener explanation should communicate that Pacedu grew from real classroom experience and is intended to make teaching work more manageable, identify learner skills and needs, and connect daily activities to practical progress.

## Research and evidence

External education research may support and validate areas of the design, including digital learning, accessibility, inclusion, learning continuity, offline/low-connectivity learning, teacher support, and education during emergencies.

Such research must be described as **research informing or supporting the design**, unless a documented partnership, funding arrangement, or direct technical assistance exists.

Pacedu must not claim that UNICEF, UNESCO, or another organization directly built or funded Pacedu without documented evidence.

## Architectural governance

These principles are locked as the educational foundation while the technical architecture remains future-safe. Features, page order, role sequences, activities, curriculum mappings, and integrations may be adjusted by authorized project controls without removing the core teaching-experience foundation.

## Status

**Foundation status: LOCKED**

The educational philosophy, teacher-support purpose, real-life learning principle, and listener-accessible explanation are protected requirements. Technical improvements may extend them but should not silently replace or contradict them.
