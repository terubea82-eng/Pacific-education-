# Pacific Education — Effectiveness, Efficiency and Quality System

## Objective
Ensure Pacific Education is useful to learners, teachers, parents and authorized education stakeholders while remaining safe, maintainable, affordable and evidence-driven.

## Learning effectiveness
- Align learning content to verified applicable curriculum sources.
- Preserve core achievement-indicator coverage.
- Keep integrated learning level-appropriate and prevent integration from displacing required core coverage.
- Gate progression where mandatory assessments are genuinely required.
- Record only the minimum learning data necessary.
- Test lessons for clarity, accessibility and age appropriateness.

## User efficiency
- Minimize unnecessary screens, duplicate data entry and repeated actions.
- Support low-bandwidth and appropriate-device use.
- Provide clear status, error and recovery messages.
- Design offline synchronization to fail safely and reconcile with authoritative server state in production.
- Keep teacher workflows focused on planning, teaching, assessment and review.

## Operational efficiency
- Automate low-risk documentation, validation and evidence indexing.
- Keep security, privacy, payment, curriculum and production authorization review-controlled.
- Use reusable evidence contracts and checklists.
- Maintain versioned change records.
- Monitor defects and recurring operational work.

## Quality controls
- Automated syntax and repository validation.
- Security and secret-pattern scanning.
- Accessibility and real-device testing.
- Curriculum-source verification.
- Pilot feedback and defect reconciliation.
- Independent specialist review where required.
- Owner-controlled release approval.

## Measures
Track, where appropriate:
- lesson completion and progression reliability;
- assessment reliability;
- accessibility/device success;
- offline synchronization success;
- defect rates and resolution time;
- support burden;
- availability and recovery results;
- payment reconciliation accuracy;
- privacy/security incidents;
- verified curriculum coverage.

Metrics are evidence for decisions, not proof of legal compliance by themselves.

## Continuous improvement
A material change triggers impact assessment and, where applicable, renewed testing/review before release. Production changes must use approved change-control and deployment procedures.
