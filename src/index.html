👨‍🏫 Owner's Experience

Pacific Education was created by Tion Terubea, with 21 years of classroom teaching experience in Fiji — a tiny island located in the middle of the South Pacific.

This experience informs Pacedu's purpose: to support learners, teachers, families and education communities with practical, accessible and connected education.

🌊 Why Pacific Education / Pacedu?

Pacedu is designed to connect learning, daily activities, practice, assessment, progress and education support in one step-by-step platform.

Its purpose is to help make meaningful education more accessible across Pacific communities, support teachers and families, and help learners continue learning even when circumstances or connectivity are difficult.

Vision • Mission • Purpose

🌊 Vision

Accessible, inclusive and meaningful education for learners across the Pacific and beyond.

🎓 Mission

Use modern education technology to connect learners, teachers, families and education partners with learning, assessment, evidence and progress.

🔐 Pacedu Rules & Conditions

Registration cannot be completed until the required agreement is confirmed.

These controls protect learners, families, teachers and the integrity of the controlled pilot.

Read the mandatory rules
  1. Educational use: Use Pacedu for lawful educational, assessment, support and pilot activities.
  2. Respect and safety: Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, abuse or deliberately disrupt other users.
  3. Accurate registration: Provide information that is truthful and appropriate for the role selected.
  4. Role protection: Select your genuine primary role. Privileged Owner/Technician controls are restricted.
  5. Privacy: Do not enter unnecessary sensitive child information, passwords, payment credentials or exact child-location data during this prototype pilot.
  6. Workspace protection: Do not attempt to access another person's workspace or bypass role permissions.
  7. Learning evidence: Do not falsify learner responses, assessments, progress or teacher-review evidence.
  8. Payments: Buy Plans and real purchasing are inactive during this controlled pilot.
  9. Pilot status: Pacedu is a controlled pilot, not a fully approved production education service.
  10. Connectivity: Offline-first features may be available, but local/offline operation does not guarantee permanent data storage or synchronization.
  11. Accessibility: Use available voice, text, speaker and accessibility controls responsibly. The protected #856 voice architecture is part of the pilot baseline.
  12. Safety controls: Pacedu may restrict, suspend or disable access when necessary for safety, testing, maintenance or pilot governance.
  13. Changes: Pilot features, curriculum mappings and technical controls may be improved under documented change control.
  14. Pilot evidence: Authorized testing and feedback may be recorded for pilot verification and improvement.

Agreement required before registration can continue.

📱 Termux technician workflow

  1. Open Termux on the Android technician device.
  2. Pull the latest Pacedu source from GitHub.
  3. Run the approved tests and Gradle build.
  4. Commit and push only reviewed changes to the main repository.
  5. Use GitHub Actions to verify the Android pilot build before installation.

Approved command sequence:

cd ~/pacific-education/Pacific-education-
git pull --ff-only origin main
./gradlew clean test assembleDebug
git status

🔐 Technical safety rules

  • Never place GitHub tokens, Firebase secrets, passwords, private keys or payment credentials in Pacedu source or this workspace.
  • Do not use Termux as a user-facing app feature; it remains a technician/developer tool.
  • Every feature upgrade should be committed, built and tested before pilot distribution.
  • Keep the controlled pilot boundary active; production services remain separately gated.
