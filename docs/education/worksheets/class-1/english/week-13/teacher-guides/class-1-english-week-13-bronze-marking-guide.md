## Learning intention
Learner identifies who a story is about (character) and where it happens (setting), using story words and/or illustrations as evidence.

## Access and marking rules
Accept speech, pointing, sign, AAC, picture selection, dictation, drawing or writing when it demonstrates the target. Score comprehension, not speech/accent, handwriting neatness or speed. Read aloud or repeat as needed and provide adequate wait time. The complete matching passage is printed in the learner's worksheet. Accept equivalent wording and valid evidence from the passage.

## Worked example
Ask: “Who is the story about?” Model finding the name. Ask: “Where does the story happen?” Model locating the place clue. Explain: character = who; setting = where.

## Passage and expected evidence
Mere takes her red ball to school. She plays near a big tree. The bell rings and Mere carries her ball to class.

Expected character: Mere. Expected setting: school / school playground / near the tree at school, depending on the question and passage wording. Accept a supported answer using a picture or quoted/paraphrased clue.

## Daily guidance and formative scoring

### Day 61 — Meet the character
- **Expected evidence:** Identifies Mere as the character.
- **Teacher prompt:** Who is the story about? Show me the name.
- **Score:** 0 = not yet demonstrated even with access support; 1 = demonstrated with model/prompt; 2 = demonstrated independently or with usual access support. Local formative rubric, not standardized.

### Day 62 — Find the setting
- **Expected evidence:** Identifies the school or school playground as the setting.
- **Teacher prompt:** Where do the events happen? Show me a clue.
- **Score:** 0 = not yet demonstrated even with access support; 1 = demonstrated with model/prompt; 2 = demonstrated independently or with usual access support. Local formative rubric, not standardized.

### Day 63 — Use story clues
- **Expected evidence:** Selects or indicates a relevant word/sentence clue from the passage.
- **Teacher prompt:** Which words helped you decide?
- **Score:** 0 = not yet demonstrated even with access support; 1 = demonstrated with model/prompt; 2 = demonstrated independently or with usual access support. Local formative rubric, not standardized.

### Day 64 — Character and setting together
- **Expected evidence:** Shows both who and where, keeping the concepts distinct.
- **Teacher prompt:** Show me who, then show me where.
- **Score:** 0 = not yet demonstrated even with access support; 1 = demonstrated with model/prompt; 2 = demonstrated independently or with usual access support. Local formative rubric, not standardized.

### Day 65 — Shared-reading review
- **Expected evidence:** Answers the pathway questions and supports at least one answer with story evidence.
- **Teacher prompt:** Can you show one clue in the story that supports your answer?
- **Score:** 0 = not yet demonstrated even with access support; 1 = demonstrated with model/prompt; 2 = demonstrated independently or with usual access support. Local formative rubric, not standardized.

## Suggested answer checks (not rigid answer key)
- Character: Mere.
- Setting: school / school playground. Accept a more specific description if supported by the passage.
- Evidence: relevant names, place words or sentences from the learner's own passage. Do not require verbatim copying.
- Gold extension: accept a reasoned comparison with another possible setting if it addresses how the place would change.
