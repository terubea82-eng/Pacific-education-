## Read or listen to the whole passage

> Read or listen: “Mere has a red bag. She puts a book in the bag. Mere walks to school.”

**Remember:** “Who” asks about a person. “What” asks about a thing or action. “Where” asks about a place. “When” asks about a time. Listen to the whole question and give an answer that matches it.

## Worked example

Question: What does Mere have? Answer: A red bag. I look for the thing Mere has and answer with the information from the passage.
