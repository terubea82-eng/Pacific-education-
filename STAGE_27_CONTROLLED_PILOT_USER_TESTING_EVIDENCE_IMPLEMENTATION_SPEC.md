# Stage 27 — Controlled Pilot & User Testing Evidence Implementation Specification

**Status:** PREPARED — controlled pilot evidence required; production remains BLOCKED.

## 1. Purpose
Test learning workflows, usability, safety, reliability, curriculum alignment and authorized feedback. Pilot success is not production or legal approval.

## 2. Governance
Record owner approval, scope, dates, participants/roles, inclusion and exclusion criteria, support, incident handling, data limits and stop conditions. Current planned pilot period is 21 September 2026 to 21 October 2026; any extension requires explicit owner approval and documented need.

## 3. Test areas
Access; lessons/progression; assessments/marks; student-teacher-parent relationships; accessibility; low-bandwidth/offline; security/privacy/safeguarding; support/recovery; safe payment testing; curriculum alignment; communication controls.

## 4. Test data
Prefer synthetic/de-identified data. Do not enter passwords, payment credentials, unnecessary sensitive child information or exact child location into the prototype.

## 5. Evidence model
Each test records scenario, expected result, actual result, tester role, environment/device, date/time, evidence reference, defect ID and retest status.

## 6. Feedback
Collect structured feedback on usefulness, clarity, accessibility, learning workflow, teacher workload and support needs. User opinion is not proof of technical, legal or curriculum compliance.

## 7. Defects
Critical security, privacy, safeguarding, data-integrity and release-blocking defects stop the affected activity until resolved or formally handled.

## 8. Closeout
Produce a report with participation summary, tests, results, defects, unresolved risks, curriculum findings, feedback, evidence index and owner decision.

## 9. Completion
Stage 27 is COMPLETE only when required pilot scope is completed, evidence is VERIFIED, required review is APPROVED and blocking defects are resolved.

## 10. Guardian boundary and transition
Guardian may organize evidence but cannot convert pilot success into legal approval or production authorization. Verified completion prepares Stage 28 payment, operations and business-control review.

**Stage 27 remains incomplete until the controlled pilot and evidence review are actually completed.**
