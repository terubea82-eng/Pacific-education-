## Read this story
### The Little Seed
Mila planted a seed in a small pot. First, she put soil in the pot. Next, she placed the seed in the soil. Then she gave it a little water. She put the pot near a sunny window. After some days, a small green shoot appeared. Mila kept watering it carefully. Finally, the plant grew two green leaves. Mila smiled and showed it to her family.

**Word bank:** first • next • then • after some days • finally • seed • soil • shoot • leaves

## Worked example
If a story says, “First, Ana puts on her shoes. Next, she walks outside,” the correct order is: 1. Ana puts on her shoes. 2. Ana walks outside. The word **first** tells us which event happens earlier.
