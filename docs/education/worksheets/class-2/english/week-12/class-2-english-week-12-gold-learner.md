## Read the passage
### Preparing for the Class Display
Our class is preparing a display about local plants. First, the learners collect clear pictures and write short captions. A group sorts the pictures by plant type. Another group makes neat labels. On Friday, the class arranges the work on a large board and checks that every caption is easy to read.

## Word bank
who • what happens • where • when • because • and • then • first • after

## Worked example
Mixed words: **the / boy / runs**

Complete sentence: **The boy runs.**

A complete sentence shares a whole idea. It usually tells who or what the sentence is about and what happens. Start with a capital letter and finish with suitable punctuation.
