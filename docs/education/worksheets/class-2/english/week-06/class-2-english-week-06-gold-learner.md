## What I am learning
Interpret a shared text by connecting details, vocabulary, and the central message. Support an explanation with evidence from the text.

## Read or listen to this passage

A SHARED GARDEN, A SHARED RESPONSIBILITY
The empty strip of ground beside our classroom did not seem useful, so the class planned a small garden. We loosened the soil and planted bean seeds in rows. Each morning, Mia checked whether the soil was dry, and Tom removed weeds that competed with the young plants for water. Several days later, green shoots pushed through the soil. Watching the change helped us understand the text's central message: living things grow best when people notice their needs and care for them consistently.

## Helpful words
