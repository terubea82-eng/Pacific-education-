### Read this passage
Mere keeps her exercise books in a pile. When the wind comes through the classroom window, some pages move onto the floor. Mere wants a holder to keep the books together. She looks at clean cardboard from a box. Cardboard is light and can be folded. She asks her teacher before using scissors. Her plan is to fold the cardboard into a standing shape with two sides. She will test it with one book first. If it falls over, she can make the base wider.

### Worked example
**Need:** Books move and become untidy.  
**Material:** Clean, strong cardboard.  
**Reason:** It can be folded into a holder.  
**Safe action:** Ask an adult before cutting.  
**Test:** Put one book in the holder and check whether it stands.
