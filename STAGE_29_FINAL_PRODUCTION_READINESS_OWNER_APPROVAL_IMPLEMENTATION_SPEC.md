# Stage 29 — Final Production Readiness & Owner Approval Implementation Specification

**Status:** PREPARED — final evidence assembly; production remains BLOCKED.

## 1. Purpose
Consolidate Stages 20–28 evidence and obtain explicit authorized owner approval without substituting documents for missing implementation or professional review.

## 2. Readiness dossier
Include backend/data; authentication/security; hosting/infrastructure; cybersecurity/privacy/safeguarding; data governance; accessibility/device; official curriculum-source verification; pilot; payment/operations; applicable legal/regulatory review; unresolved risks; ownership/IP; vendors/contracts; backup/recovery; incident response.

## 3. Gate
Every mandatory requirement must be VERIFIED and current. Critical/blocking defects keep the gate BLOCKED. One approved document cannot override missing technical evidence.

## 4. Professional review
Record qualified reviewer/authority, scope, date, findings, conditions and corrective actions. Legal questions go to the appropriate legal/professional reviewer.

## 5. Owner approval
Approval identifies release/version, evidence package, residual risks, conditions, scope and date. Owner approval does not replace required authority approvals.

## 6. Release candidate
Freeze and identify source commit/version, build artifact, configuration reference, migration version and verification results.

## 7. Fail-closed
Missing, stale, contradictory or unverifiable mandatory evidence keeps production eligibility false.

## 8. Guardian boundary
Guardian can assemble dossiers and review requests but cannot self-approve Stage 29, grant legal approval or declare production eligible.

## 9. Completion and transition
Stage 29 is COMPLETE only after evidence is verified, required reviews are approved, owner approval is explicit and the release candidate is identified. Completion prepares Stage 30 release-gate procedures; it does not deploy.

**Stage 29 remains incomplete until real evidence and approvals exist.**
