### Marking guidance

Accept oral, signed, Braille, pointing, drawing, object selection or written responses when they demonstrate the same learning. Do not mark spelling or handwriting unless it is part of the verified learning objective. Accept equivalent words and sensible explanations.

| Item | Expected evidence | Marks |
|---|---|---:|
| 1 | Ruler | 1 |
| 2 | Glue stick | 1 |
| 3 | Blunt-ended scissors are safer for a classroom paper task / reduce risk of injury; accept equivalent explanation | 1 |
| 4 | Drawing or description clearly identifies one passage tool | 1 |
| 5 | Stop using it and tell the teacher / do not touch or continue with damaged tool | 1 |
| Safety sentence | “handles” | 1 |

**Total: 6 marks**

### Accepted equivalents
- Ruler: measuring tool, straight edge.
- Glue stick: glue, adhesive stick.
- Damaged tool response: stop, put it down safely, move away if needed, inform teacher/adult.

### Evidence record
Record whether the learner names tools, identifies one use and states one safe action. Record the response method and any prompts. A single worksheet must not permanently assign a pathway.

**Next step:** Practise naming one tool, matching it to its job and rehearsing the safe passing rule with a safe classroom prop.
