## Read or listen to the complete information text

### A school bag and its uses

A school bag helps a learner carry books, writing materials and other school items.

The main compartment is the largest space inside the bag. Smaller pockets can hold items such as pencils or a lunch box.

A handle can be used to lift the bag. Shoulder straps help a learner carry it on their back.

A learner can keep the bag organised by putting similar items together and checking that it is not too heavy.

**Useful words:** compartment, pockets, handle, straps, organised.

## Worked example

A school bag helps learners carry school items. Its main compartment holds larger things, while smaller pockets can hold pencils. A handle and shoulder straps help a learner carry it. Keeping items organised makes them easier to find.

A title tells the reader the topic. Facts give information. Labels can group facts that belong together.
