🔐 Pacedu Rules & Conditions

Registration cannot continue until you confirm the required agreement.

These rules protect learners, families, teachers and the integrity of this controlled pilot.

Read the mandatory rules
  1. Educational use: Use Pacedu for lawful educational, assessment, support and pilot activities.
  2. Respect and safety: Do not harass, threaten, impersonate, abuse or deliberately disrupt other users.
  3. Accurate registration: Provide truthful information appropriate for the role selected.
  4. Role protection: Select your genuine primary role. Privileged Owner/Technician controls are restricted.
  5. Privacy: Do not enter unnecessary sensitive child information, passwords, payment credentials or exact child-location data during this prototype pilot.
  6. Workspace protection: Do not attempt to access another person's workspace or bypass role permissions.
  7. Learning evidence: Do not falsify learner responses, assessments, progress or teacher-review evidence.
  8. Payments: Buy Plans and real purchasing are inactive during this controlled pilot.
  9. Pilot status: Pacedu is a controlled pilot, not a fully approved production education service.
  10. Connectivity: Offline-first features may be available, but offline operation does not guarantee permanent storage or synchronization.
  11. Accessibility: Use voice, text, speaker and accessibility controls responsibly. The protected #856 voice architecture remains the pilot baseline.
  12. Safety controls: Access may be restricted for safety, testing, maintenance or pilot governance.
  13. Changes and evidence: Features and curriculum mappings may change under documented control; authorized testing feedback may be recorded for pilot verification.

Agreement required before registration can continue.
