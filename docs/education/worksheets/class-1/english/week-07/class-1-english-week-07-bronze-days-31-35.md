## What I am learning
I am learning to listen for the first sound in familiar words and to listen to simple sound sequences blended aloud by my teacher. I use only sounds and words my class has already learned.

## How to use this worksheet
Use the picture/word bank and the adult's model. Try each task with a visual cue first; ask for the model again if needed. You may point, match, sign, use AAC, dictate, draw, or write.

**Important:** Your teacher chooses the actual words, pictures, and sound cards from the class's taught sequence. A blank sound is not a test of a new letter. You are not expected to read an untaught word.

## Day 31 — Listen and notice

**My task:** Listen to the teacher say each familiar word. Look at the picture or object. Find the first sound you hear.

**Worked example:** The teacher says a familiar word, stretches its first sound gently, then says the whole word again. The learner points to the matching picture or sound card. Fill the blank with a taught sound only.

**My activity:** Match a picture to the teacher-selected first-sound card, then try one more.

**My task:** Watch the teacher model how to listen for the first sound. Use picture cards, objects, counters, or a sound box.

**My activity:** Use the picture/word bank to match one familiar word to its taught beginning sound, then try a similar item.

## Day 33 — Practise together

**My task:** Take turns with a partner or adult. Listen to each familiar word and sort or match it by its first sound. Practise oral blending with teacher-modelled sound sequences already taught.

**Worked example:** The teacher says a taught sequence slowly with a pause between sounds, then says it smoothly as a whole word. Learners listen and choose the matching picture. Use only words whose sounds have already been taught.

**My activity:** Complete two guided matching/listening turns using the picture bank and teacher model.

## Day 34 — Apply independently

**My task:** Use the same listening skill with a fresh familiar example chosen by the teacher. Do not guess from a new or untaught spelling pattern.

**Worked example:** Listen to the whole familiar word; listen again for the beginning; choose the matching taught sound/picture. For blending, listen to the teacher's taught sound sequence and select the matching familiar picture.

**My activity:** Use the visual cue to complete one fresh familiar item, then attempt a second if ready.
